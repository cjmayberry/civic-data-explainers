## What this is  
This dataset tracks designated solid waste collection areas in the city.

## Why it matters to you  
Where your trash gets picked up affects how often service happens, what rules you follow for sorting recycling, and whether you need special stickers for bulk items. If you're moving or starting a business, checking your waste area could mean the difference between weekly pickups or hauling trash yourself.

## Where this leaves you  
While this data identifies waste service areas, you’ll need to cross-reference with the sanitation department’s pickup schedules and rules for your specific address. The dataset alone won’t tell you your collection day or what items are accepted.
