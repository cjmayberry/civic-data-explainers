## What this tracks  
This dataset records physical barriers in Nevada's public right-of-way that may impact ADA accessibility, such as uneven surfaces or obstructions.

## Why it matters to you  
If you use a wheelchair, walker, or stroller in Nevada, this data shows where you might encounter unexpected barriers on sidewalks and crossings. Business owners can check if their storefront's public walkways meet accessibility standards before customers arrive. Parents can identify problematic routes before planning walks with young children.

## How to read this data  
**Height** — Measures vertical gaps in inches (sample: 1 inch) that could catch wheelchair wheels.  
**Type** — Numeric code classifying the barrier (sample: 9).  
**Confirmed** — Whether the issue was verified (sample: "Yes").  
**ComplianceStatus** — Shows if the barrier meets ADA standards (sample: None in this dataset but likely values like "Compliant" or "Non-Compliant").  

## Try it yourself  
Check the map near your home or business address for any discontinuities marked "Non-Compliant" to report accessibility concerns to Nevada DOT.
