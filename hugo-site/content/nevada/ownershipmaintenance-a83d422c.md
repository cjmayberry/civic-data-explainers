## What this tracks  
This dataset records which Nevada state routes are owned and maintained by the Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT) versus other agencies.  

## Why it matters to you  
If your business depends on reliable road access (like trucking or tourism), this tells you whether NDOT or another entity handles pothole repairs or snowplowing on your route. Nevada residents can use this to confirm who to contact about road damage—since reporting to the wrong agency delays fixes.  

## How to read this data  
**RouteID** — The state route identifier (e.g., "100002LY").  
**Ownership** — A code indicating the owning agency (sample value: 4, where 4 = NDOT).  
**Maintenance** — A code showing who maintains the road (sample value: 4, matching NDOT ownership).  
**Agency** — A numeric code for the responsible agency (sample: 29, likely another entity).  

## Try it yourself  
Check who maintains your nearest state highway by searching NDOT's public road inventory with the RouteID (e.g., "100002LY").
