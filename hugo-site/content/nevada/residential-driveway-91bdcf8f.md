## What this tracks  
This dataset records measurements and compliance status of residential driveways in Nevada for ADA accessibility standards.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning to modify your driveway or add curb cuts, this data tells you whether your existing setup meets ADA requirements before you invest in changes. Knowing the slope measurements helps wheelchair users identify potentially hazardous driveways in their neighborhood. The compliance status shows which properties have already passed city inspections for accessibility.

## How to read this data  
**Width** — The driveway width in feet (sample: 4.2 feet)  
**RunningSlope** — The slope along the driveway's length (sample: 0.6% grade)  
**ComplianceStatus** — Whether the driveway meets ADA standards (sample: "Compliant")  
**InspectionDate** — When the driveway was last checked (sample: Feb 2021)  

## Try it yourself  
Check your property's driveway compliance status using Nevada's online ADA inventory map before scheduling any concrete work or accessibility modifications.
