## What this tracks  
This dataset shows planned transportation improvement projects across Nevada, including timelines and technologies being considered.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning to open a business near a major road, this data tells you when construction might disrupt customer access. Homebuyers can check whether a problematic intersection near a potential house is scheduled for upgrades. Trucking companies operating in Nevada can anticipate route changes from upcoming bridge repairs or lane expansions.

## How to read this data  
**Timeframe** — When work is expected (e.g., "FY2025-2027" or "Under Design")  
**Purpose** — The project's goal (e.g., "Intersection Safety Improvements" or "Pavement Rehabilitation")  
**Location** — Specific roads or areas affected (e.g., "US 95 between Silver King Rd and SR 160")  
**Priority** — NDOT's ranking of project urgency (e.g., "High", "Medium", or "Tier 1")
