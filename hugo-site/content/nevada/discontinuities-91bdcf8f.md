## What this tracks  
This dataset records physical barriers in Nevada's public right-of-way that may affect ADA accessibility, such as uneven pavement or problematic slopes.

## Why it matters to you  
If you use a wheelchair, walker, or stroller in Nevada, this data shows where you might encounter difficult transitions between sidewalks and streets. Business owners can check if their storefront has accessibility issues before customers arrive. Parents can identify problematic routes before taking children to school or parks.

## How to read this data  
**Height** — Measures vertical gaps (sample: 1.0 inches) where surfaces don't align properly.  
**Type** — Codes for different barrier categories (sample: 9), though the specific types aren't labeled in this sample.  
**Confirmed** — Whether the issue was verified (sample: "Yes").  
**ComplianceStatus** — Shows if the barrier meets ADA standards (sample: None in this data, but could indicate "Compliant" or "Non-Compliant").  

## Try it yourself  
Check Nevada's interactive ADA map to see barrier locations near your home or business by searching your address in their ArcGIS Online portal.
