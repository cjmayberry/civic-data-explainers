## What this tracks  
This dataset records cultural landmarks and facilities in Nevada, including their names, types, locations, and operational statuses.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where cultural facilities like golf courses, museums, or theaters are located helps you plan weekend activities or scout venues for events. If you're considering moving neighborhoods in Las Vegas, this data shows which cultural amenities are nearby. Business owners can identify gaps in local offerings — like an area lacking public gathering spaces — to inform new ventures.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The facility's name (e.g., "Highland Falls Golf Course").  
**CULTURAL_TYPE** — What kind of facility it is (e.g., "Golf Course").  
**ADDRESS** — Physical location (e.g., "10201 Sun City Blvd").  
**STATUS** — Whether the facility is currently operational (e.g., "Active").
