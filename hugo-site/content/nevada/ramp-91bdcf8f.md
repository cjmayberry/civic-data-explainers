## What this tracks  
This dataset measures the slope, dimensions, and compliance status of ADA-accessible ramps at intersections across Nevada, NV.

## Why it matters to you  
If you use a wheelchair, walker, or stroller, this data tells you which ramps meet accessibility standards before planning your route. Business owners can verify if their storefront’s nearest ramp complies with ADA requirements, avoiding potential accessibility complaints. Parents can check if ramps near schools or parks have safe slopes for strollers.

## How to read this data  
**RampSlopeCenter** — The slope at the ramp’s center (e.g., 6.3%), where values under 8.3% are ADA-compliant.  
**ComplianceStatus** — Whether the ramp meets standards (e.g., "Compliant" or non-compliant).  
**LandingWidth** — The flat area at the ramp’s top (e.g., 4.0 ft), critical for turning mobility devices safely.  
**DWS_Present** — Indicates if detectable warning surfaces (textured ground) exist (e.g., 1 for yes, 0 for no).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your nearest intersection in Nevada’s [ADA Ramp Inventory map](https://www.nevadaada.org/ramps) to check if its ramps are compliant before heading out.
