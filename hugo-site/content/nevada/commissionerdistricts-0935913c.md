## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries of commissioner districts in Nevada's Carson City County.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your commissioner district helps you identify which local official represents your neighborhood when reporting road maintenance issues or advocating for transportation improvements. If your street needs repaving or better snow plowing, this data tells you exactly which commissioner to contact for faster resolution. It also shows whether your business falls in a district that's prioritized for infrastructure upgrades.

## How to read this data  
**District** — The numbered commissioner district (e.g., "1").  
**County** — Always shows "CARSON CITY" since these are county commissioner districts.  
**Symbology** — Combines county and district for mapping purposes (e.g., "CARSON CITY 1").  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your address on Carson City's official district map to see which commissioner represents your neighborhood for transportation concerns.
