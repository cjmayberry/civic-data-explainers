## What this tracks  
This dataset records proposed transportation technology projects across Nevada, including their locations, purposes, and priority levels.

## Why it matters to you  
If you regularly commute on Valley View Boulevard or Summerlin Parkway, this data tells you when NDOT plans to install new traffic monitoring systems that could affect your route. Business owners near CC215 can anticipate construction timelines for smart traffic systems that may impact customer access. The priority levels show which projects Nevada considers most urgent for safety improvements.

## How to read this data  
**Location** — Specific road segments being considered for upgrades, like "Valley View Boulevard to SR-613 (Summerlin Pkwy)"  
**Priority** — How urgently NDOT views the project, with values like "Low" or (presumably) "High"  
**Prop_Tech** — Types of intelligent transportation systems being studied, such as "ITS Study"  
**Timeframe** — When the project might happen, with values like "Long-term"
