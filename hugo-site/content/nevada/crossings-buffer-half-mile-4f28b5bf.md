## What this tracks  
This dataset records wildlife crossing structures (like underpasses and culverts) near Nevada's highways, including what species they're designed for.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're driving US Route 93 through rural Nevada, this data tells you where animals are most likely to cross the highway — helping you stay alert in high-risk areas. Hunters can see where large ungulates (like deer) frequently cross, while conservationists can verify if crossings near their property are marked as complete.

## How to read this data  
**Structure** — Type of crossing (e.g., "Underpass").  
**FocalSpecies** — Which animals the crossing serves (e.g., "Lrg Ungulates" for deer/elk).  
**RoadName** — Highway where crossing is located (e.g., "US93").  
**Status** — Whether construction is "Complete" or other phases.
