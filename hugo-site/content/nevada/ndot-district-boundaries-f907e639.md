## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT) district boundaries across the state.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your NDOT district helps you direct road maintenance requests and winter storm response inquiries to the right regional office. If you're planning a commercial truck route through Nevada, the district boundaries tell you which NDOT office handles permits for your specific route. Residents can use this to check which district maintains their highway exit ramp or overpass.

## How to read this data  
**DISTRICT_N** — The numbered district (like "2" for northern Nevada), which determines which NDOT office handles maintenance requests and projects in that area.  
**created_date** — When this district boundary was last updated in the system (shown as a timestamp like 1727474455454).
