## What this tracks  
This dataset records telecom network construction and partnership opportunities along Nevada roadways managed by NDOT.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a local contractor or telecom provider, this data shows active infrastructure projects where NDOT is seeking partners. Knowing which highway corridors have open joint construction projects (like "CONTRACT 4500 EA 74239") helps you bid on work before deadlines. Residents can see planned fiber optic work that may temporarily affect their commute routes.

## How to read this data  
**activityname** — The project identifier (e.g., "CONTRACT 4500 EA 74239") that contractors reference when bidding.  
**activitystatus** — Current phase like "Joint Construction" indicating NDOT is seeking partners.  
**easementwidth** — The width in feet (e.g., 6) of the utility corridor being developed.  
**status** — Direct guidance like "Actively Seeking Partners for Joint Construction Projects."  

## Try it yourself  
Check [NDOT's telecom partnership portal](https://www.dot.nv.gov/) for active projects near your business address that match the "Joint Construction" activity status.
