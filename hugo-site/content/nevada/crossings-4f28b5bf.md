## What this tracks  
This dataset records wildlife crossing structures (like underpasses and culverts) along Nevada roads to help animals safely cross.

## Why it matters to you  
If you frequently drive US93 near 10 Mile Summit, this data tells you where deer and other large animals are most likely to cross — helping you stay alert in high-risk areas. Hunters can use it to identify migration paths, and RV drivers should note these locations when planning overnight stops near highways. The "Complete" status means these crossings are fully operational.

## How to read this data  
**Structure** — Type of crossing (e.g., "Underpass").  
**FocalSpecies** — Which animals use it (e.g., "Lrg Ungulates" means deer/elk).  
**RoadName** — Highway where the crossing is located (e.g., "US93").  
**SiteName** — Nearby landmark (e.g., "10 Mile Summit").
