## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and details of fiber optic cable routes installed along Nevada highways.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business in Nevada that requires high-speed internet (like a call center or cloud services company), this data shows where fiber infrastructure already exists along major routes like US95. Homebuyers near these routes can also check whether their potential neighborhood has fiber backbone access, which affects property values and work-from-home capabilities.

## How to read this data  
**RouteID** — Identifies the highway where fiber is installed (e.g., "US95" for U.S. Route 95).  
**Start_Latitude/Start_Longitude** — GPS coordinates where the fiber segment begins (e.g., 36.28417914, -115.27292146 near Las Vegas).  
**End_Latitude/End_Longitude** — GPS coordinates where the fiber segment ends, helping you measure coverage spans.  
**ProjectID** — Unique identifier for the installation project (e.g., "D1-013"), useful for tracking expansion plans.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your business address falls between any Start/End coordinate pairs using a mapping tool like Google Earth to see fiber optic access along your nearest highway.
