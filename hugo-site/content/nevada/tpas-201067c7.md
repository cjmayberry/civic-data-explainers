## What this tracks  
This dataset records planned transportation projects and improvements across Nevada, including their purpose, priority, and proposed technologies.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a major commute route change or considering a business location near a highway, this data shows upcoming road projects that could affect your travel time. Nevada residents can check if their regular routes will have construction delays. Business owners near proposed improvements can anticipate changes in customer access or foot traffic patterns.

## How to read this data  
**Location** — The specific area where the project will occur (sample value: "I-80 near Wells").  
**Purpose** — Why the project is happening (sample value: "Intersection safety improvements").  
**Priority** — How urgently the project is needed (sample value: "High").  
**Prop_Tech** — What kind of technology or infrastructure will be used (sample value: "Adaptive traffic signals").

## Try it yourself  
Check the "Location" field for projects near your neighborhood by searching for your nearest highway or major road in Nevada's open data portal.
