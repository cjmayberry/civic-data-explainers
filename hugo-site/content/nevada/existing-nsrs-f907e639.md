## What this tracks  
This dataset records locations in Nevada that serve as National Spatial Reference System (NSRS) points, which are precise geographic markers used for mapping and surveying.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're hiring a surveyor to mark your property boundaries in Clark County, these reference points ensure their measurements align with national standards. For small businesses planning construction near UNLV Thomas & Mack Center, knowing these markers exist helps avoid costly positioning errors. The data also helps residents verify if their property surveys used accurate base points.

## How to read this data  
**Description** — Names the landmark or location of the reference point, like "UNLV Thomas-Mack Center".  
**Range** — Shows how far the reference point's accuracy extends (e.g., "25" miles).  
**Lat/Lon** — The exact coordinates (e.g., 36.105, -115.14333) used for precise measurements.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if there's an NSRS point within your area by searching the coordinates of your home or business against the Lat/Lon values in this dataset.
