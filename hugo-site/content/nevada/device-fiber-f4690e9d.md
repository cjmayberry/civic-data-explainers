## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and details of fiber optic cable installations along Nevada roadways.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business that requires high-speed internet in Nevada, this data shows where fiber infrastructure already exists—saving you from expensive private installations. Residents planning home offices can check if their neighborhood has fiber access before signing leases. The **LengthInMiles** field reveals how extensive each fiber route is, helping you compare connectivity options near your location.  

## How to read this data  
**Start_Latitude/Start_Longitude** — The GPS coordinates where a fiber route begins (e.g., 36.28417914, -115.27292146).  
**RouteID** — The highway or road the fiber follows (e.g., "US95" for U.S. Route 95).  
**ProjectID** — A unique identifier for the installation project (e.g., "D1-013"), useful for tracking construction timelines.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your nearest major roadway (like US95) appears in the RouteID field to see if fiber runs parallel to your commute or neighborhood.
