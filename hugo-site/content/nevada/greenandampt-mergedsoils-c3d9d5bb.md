## What this tracks  
This dataset measures soil properties across different areas in Nevada, including water retention and permeability characteristics.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a garden, installing a septic system, or considering basement waterproofing, this data tells you how quickly water will drain through your soil. A landscaper in Nevada could use this to select drought-resistant plants for areas with low water retention (WPOINT), while a homeowner might check SAT values before digging a rain garden to avoid standing water.

## How to read this data  
**WPOINT** — The wilting point where plants can't extract water (0.192 means 19.2% of soil volume).  
**SAT** — Saturation level showing maximum water content (0.459 = 45.9% water by volume).  
**XKSAT** — How fast water moves through saturated soil (0.1032 cm/hour means slow drainage).  
**PERC_ROCK** — Percentage of rock fragments in soil (0 means no rocks in this sample).
