## What this tracks  
This dataset records all federally managed lands in Nevada, including national parks, forests, and other protected areas.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing which lands near you are federally managed helps plan outdoor activities and understand land use restrictions. If you're considering buying property near Carson City, this data tells you whether adjacent land is protected federal territory that can't be developed. Hunters and hikers can verify which agencies manage specific recreation areas before visiting.

## How to read this data  
**Agency** — Which federal organization manages the land (e.g., "National Park Service" or "Bureau of Land Management").  
**unit_name** — The official name of the protected area (e.g., "Hopewell Culture National Historical Park").  
**link** — Direct URL to the managing agency's website with visitor information (e.g., "https://www.nps.gov/hocu").

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for federal lands within 20 miles of your Nevada address to see nearby protected areas and their visiting rules.
