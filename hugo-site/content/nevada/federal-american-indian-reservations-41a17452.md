## What this tracks  
This dataset maps federally recognized American Indian reservations in Nevada and nationwide, including their boundaries and key attributes.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning land use near reservation borders—whether for a home expansion, business location, or recreational activity—this data shows exactly where tribal jurisdiction begins. Nevada residents can also use this to identify nearby tribal communities for cultural events or tourism opportunities. Knowing reservation boundaries helps avoid accidental trespassing on sovereign land.

## How to read this data  
**BASENAME** — The reservation's common name (e.g., "Ute Mountain").  
**NAME** — Official full designation (e.g., "Ute Mountain Reservation").  
**FSRFLG** — Indicates federal recognition status with "F" for federally recognized.  
**AREALAND** — Total land area in square meters (e.g., 2,302,794,374 for Ute Mountain).
