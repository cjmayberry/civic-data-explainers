## What this tracks  
This dataset records the types and locations of transportation devices (like beacons) installed along Nevada highways.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where devices like beacons are located helps you anticipate safety features on your regular routes. If you're a trucker planning an overnight haul through Nevada, this data shows whether your route has visibility aids in low-light areas. For commuters, it reveals which stretches of I-15 have safety infrastructure near merging lanes or sharp curves.

## How to read this data  
**Device_Typ** — The type of installed device; sample value "Beacon" indicates a flashing safety light.  
**Location** — Specific highway and position; "I15 SB N of Silverado Ranch" means southbound Interstate 15 north of the Silverado Ranch exit.  
**District** — Maintenance jurisdiction; value "1" corresponds to NDOT's Las Vegas district.

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular commute route has safety devices by searching the dataset for highway names like "I15" or "US95" near your exit.
