## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nevada DOT's planned Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) projects along state highways, including traffic monitoring devices and infrastructure improvements.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where NDOT plans to install traffic cameras (CCTV), dynamic message signs (DMS), or weather sensors (RWIS) helps you anticipate construction delays on your regular routes like US-395. Carson City residents can check if their commute corridor has adaptive traffic signals coming soon. Truckers passing through rural Nevada can see which mountain passes (like the 5,000ft elevation change near Lakeview) will get new visibility sensors for safer winter travel.

## How to read this data  
**Route** — The highway being improved (e.g., "US-395").  
**Direction** — Travel direction affected (e.g., "NORTHBOUND").  
**ProjectPa** — Project scope (e.g., "Urban Lite - Permanent Full" means long-term urban area upgrades).  
**CCTV/DMS/RWIS** — Counts of planned cameras (0 means none), message signs, or weather stations in that segment.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular highway exit (like "EASTLAKE BLVD/EXIT 42") appears in the recommendations field to see planned safety upgrades.
