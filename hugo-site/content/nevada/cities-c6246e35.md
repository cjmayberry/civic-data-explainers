## What this tracks  
This dataset records official city boundaries and key attributes like elevation across Nevada.

## Why it matters to you  
When considering a move within Nevada, this data shows whether a property falls within city limits (affecting taxes and services) versus unincorporated county areas. For hikers or outdoor businesses, the elevation fields help prepare for altitude differences—Crestline sits at 5,981 feet, which means thinner air and cooler temperatures than Las Vegas (2,001 ft).

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — Official city name (e.g., "Crestline").  
**COUNTY** — County where the city is located (e.g., "Lincoln").  
**ELEV_FT** — Elevation in feet above sea level (e.g., "5981").  
**COUNTY_SEAT** — "1" if the city is a county seat, "0" if not (e.g., Crestline is not).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your neighborhood is technically inside city limits by searching the dataset for your city's name, then cross-referencing with zoning maps from your county.
