## What this tracks  
This dataset records missing sidewalk features in Nevada that need to be addressed for ADA compliance.

## Why it matters to you  
If you use a wheelchair, walker, or stroller, this data shows where sidewalk gaps might force you into the street. Knowing these locations helps you plan safer routes. For business owners, it identifies areas where customers with mobility challenges may have difficulty accessing your storefront. The city uses this data to prioritize sidewalk repairs and new installations.

## How to read this data  
**SidewalkLengthInFeet** — The length of the missing sidewalk section (e.g., 5.0 feet).  
**Type** — A code indicating what type of feature is missing (sample value: 7).  
**Notes** — Specific details about the issue (e.g., "32" width" suggests a narrow sidewalk).  
**Confirmed** — Whether the issue has been verified (sample value: "Yes").  

## Try it yourself  
Check the map for missing sidewalks near your home or business by searching your address in Nevada's ArcGIS Online ADA inventory map.
