## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nevada Department of Transportation's active traffic management projects, including their locations, technologies deployed, costs, and priority issues.

## Why it matters to you  
If you regularly commute on I-515 or Boulder Highway, this data tells you where NDOT is installing new traffic cameras (CCTV), variable message signs (DMS), or ramp meters that could affect your drive times. Business owners near Charleston Blvd or Eastern Ave can see if upcoming construction might impact customer access. The "High_Issue" field reveals whether a project targets crash hotspots or wildlife crossings near your route.

## How to read this data  
**Route** — The highway being improved, like "I 515" or "SR 582/Boulder Highway".  
**X_St** — Major cross streets affected, such as "CHARLESTON BLVD/EXIT 72".  
**High_Issue** — Safety problems being addressed, like "Wildlife Crash Priority" or "Congestion Priority".  
**DMS** — Count of new digital message signs being installed (sample value: 2).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your commute route appears in the "Route" or "X_St" fields to see planned traffic tech upgrades near you.
