## What this tracks  
This dataset records planned transportation infrastructure projects in Nevada, including road improvements, freeway upgrades, and traffic management systems.

## Why it matters to you  
If you commute on I-515 between Boulder Highway and I-15, this data tells you exactly when and where lane closures will occur for the upcoming ATM system installation. Business owners along Charleston Blvd can anticipate construction impacts on customer access during the 2024 work season. Residents near SR 582 can check if their area will get new wrong-way driving detection systems to improve neighborhood safety.

## How to read this data  
**Title** — The project name and scope, like "I 515 Urban Freeway ATM = 5.285 Miles (NDOT D1)"  
**From_Loc/To_Loc** — The start and end points of construction, such as "SR 582/Boulder Highway" to "JCT I 15"  
**Timeframe** — When work occurs (though currently shows "Unavailable" in samples)  
**High_Issue** — The problems being addressed, like "Queue Priority" or "Wildlife Crash Priority"  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular commute route appears in the dataset by searching for cross streets like "CHARLESTON BLVD" or "LAS VEGAS BLVD" in the X_St field to see planned improvements.
