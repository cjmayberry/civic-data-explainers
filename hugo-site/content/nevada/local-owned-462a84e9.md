## What this tracks  
This dataset identifies which roads in Nevada are owned and maintained by the Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT).

## Why it matters to you  
If your street is listed here, NDOT—not your city or county—handles repairs, plowing, and maintenance. Knowing this helps you report potholes or snow removal issues to the right agency. For business owners, it clarifies who to contact about permits for roadside signage or construction access.  

## How to read this data  
**RouteID** — A unique identifier for each road segment (e.g., `100002LY` combines a route number and county code).  
**RouteNameFull** — The official name of the road (e.g., `B ST`).  
**JurisdictionCode** — Indicates NDOT’s maintenance responsibility (e.g., `040` means state-owned).  
**CountyCode** — The county where the road is located (e.g., `LY` for Lyon County).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your street is state-maintained by searching the dataset for its name (like `B ST`) or your county code (e.g., `LY`).
