## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nevada's existing National Scenic Byways (NSRS) locations, including their geographic coordinates and descriptions.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where Nevada's scenic byways are located helps you plan more enjoyable road trips through the state's most picturesque routes. If you're opening a roadside business near Las Vegas, this data shows whether you're located along a high-traffic scenic route like the one passing UNLV Thomas-Mack Center — which could mean more potential customers stopping at your establishment.

## How to read this data  
**Description** — Names the scenic byway or landmark (e.g., "UNLV Thomas-Mack Center").  
**Range** — Shows how many miles the scenic route covers (e.g., "25" miles).  
**Lat/Lon** — The exact coordinates where you can access the route (e.g., 36.105, -115.14333 for the UNLV location).
