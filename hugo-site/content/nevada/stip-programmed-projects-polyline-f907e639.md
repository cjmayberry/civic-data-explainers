## What this tracks  
This dataset shows planned transportation improvement projects across Nevada, including road repairs, signage upgrades, and smart traffic systems.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing which stretches of highway will be under construction helps Nevada drivers plan alternate routes and avoid delays — like checking whether I-80 will have lane closures before scheduling a Reno-to-Elko trip. Businesses near project zones (like the adaptive lighting upgrades) can anticipate brighter nighttime visibility for customers. The funding details reveal whether your tax dollars are prioritizing pavement fixes or traffic cameras.

## How to read this data  
**ProjectTitle** — Describes the work (e.g., "I-80 Pavement Preservation Including Sign Replacement").  
**Year2024** — Shows the budget allocated for next year (e.g., $30,500,000 for major 2024 work).  
**ITSElements** — Lists smart tech being installed (e.g., "DMS, CCTV" means digital message signs and traffic cameras).  
**StartLatitude/StartLongitude** — Geographic coordinates where the project begins (e.g., 40.8849°N, -114.2521°W).

Check Nevada DOT's map viewer to see if your regular commute route has any programmed projects by entering nearby coordinates like 40.7380°N, -114.0436°W.
