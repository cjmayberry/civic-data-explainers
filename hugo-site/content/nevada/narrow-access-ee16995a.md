## What this tracks  
This dataset records measurements and compliance status of narrow sidewalks and pathways in Nevada that may pose accessibility challenges.

## Why it matters to you  
If you use a wheelchair, walker, or stroller in Nevada, this data tells you which sidewalks might be too narrow for safe passage. Business owners can check if their storefront's sidewalk meets accessibility standards before renovating entrances. Residents planning routes to bus stops or schools can identify potential barriers in advance.

## How to read this data  
**Impediment** — Type of access issue (sample value "PP" likely indicates physical obstruction).  
**WidthInches** — Measured pathway width (sample shows a 38-inch wide path).  
**Confirmed** — Whether the measurement was verified (sample shows "Yes").  
**ComplianceStatus** — Whether the location meets ADA standards (sample empty but would show compliance issues).  

## Try it yourself  
Check the map for measurements near your home or business address to see if nearby sidewalks meet the 36-inch minimum width required by ADA standards.
