## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and types of traffic devices (like beacons and signals) along Nevada highways.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a commute or delivery route through Nevada, this data tells you where traffic devices are installed—helping you anticipate slowdowns or identify safer routes. For example, knowing a beacon exists on I-15 near Silverado Ranch prepares you for potential stops or speed changes. Businesses can also use this to optimize delivery times by avoiding areas with frequent traffic controls.

## How to read this data  
**Device_Typ** — The type of traffic device, like "Beacon" (sample value).  
**Location** — Where the device is installed, described by nearby landmarks or highway segments (e.g., "I15 SB N of Silverado Ranch").  
**District** — The NDOT district managing the device (sample = 1), useful for reporting maintenance issues.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular commute route has traffic devices by searching the dataset for highways like "I-15" or "US-95" in the **Location** field.
