## What this tracks  
This dataset maps state-owned land parcels in Nevada, including ownership details and acreage.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering leasing adjacent land for a business expansion in Washoe County, this data tells you whether the neighboring parcel is state-controlled (potentially opening partnership opportunities). Homebuyers near Reno-Tahoe Airport can check if surrounding land is publicly owned, which affects future development plans. The acreage measurements help assess the scale of nearby government-owned properties.

## How to read this data  
**APN** — The Assessor's Parcel Number (e.g., 015-210-34_W) used to identify specific land parcels in property records.  
**OWNER** — The government entity controlling the land (e.g., "RENO-TAHOE AIRPORT AUTHORITY").  
**COMMON_NAM** — Descriptive name of the property's use (e.g., "Reno Motor Pool - Airport Authority property").  
**CALC_ACRES** — The calculated acreage of the parcel (e.g., 1.0115 acres), often more precise than the assessed acreage.  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset using your neighborhood's APN (found on property tax documents) to identify nearby state-owned parcels and their designated uses.
