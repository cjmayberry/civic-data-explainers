## What this tracks  
This dataset defines the official geographic boundary of the state of Nevada.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing Nevada's precise boundary helps when checking which state laws or tax rates apply to your business location near state lines. Residents can verify if their property falls within Nevada for voting districts or emergency services. The data also clarifies which state agency manages roads or land if you're planning construction near borders.

## How to read this data  
**StateName** — Confirms the boundary applies to Nevada (sample value: "Nevada").  
**StateCapit** — Shows Carson City as the capital (sample value: "Carson City").  
**DataSource** — Lists the surveys used to define the boundary (sample: "NV Survey 1st-4th, NGS 2nd-4th, PLSS, DOQ, Private 1st").
