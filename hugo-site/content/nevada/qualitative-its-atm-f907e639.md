## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nevada's planned and existing Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) projects, including technologies like traffic cameras, digital message signs, and adaptive signals.

## Why it matters to you  
If you regularly commute on Nevada highways, this data tells you where NDOT is installing new traffic monitoring systems that could affect your drive time. Business owners along truck routes can see if their corridor is slated for smart work zone technologies that may cause construction delays. The **Priority** field shows which projects are scheduled soonest, helping you anticipate changes to your regular routes.

## How to read this data  
**Title** — The project name, like "Updated ITS Standards" for system-wide upgrades.  
**Proj_Desc** — Details about specific technologies being added, such as "wrong way systems" or "overhead lane control gantries."  
**Priority** — Indicates project timing with values like "Near" for imminent work.  
**Cost_Sum** — Total project budget in dollars (e.g., "$150,000" for standards updates).  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your nearest NDOT-owned roadway (like I-80 or US-95) to see if smart traffic systems are planned for your commute route.
