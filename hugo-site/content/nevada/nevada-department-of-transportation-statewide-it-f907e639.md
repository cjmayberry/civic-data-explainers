## What this tracks  
This dataset records planned and existing Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) devices like ramp meters across Nevada's roadways.

## Why it matters to you  
If you regularly commute on highways like IR515, this data shows where Nevada DOT plans to install traffic-flow devices that may affect your drive time. Business owners along truck routes can check if new ramp meters are proposed near their locations, which could impact delivery schedules. Residents can see whether congestion-relief projects are coming to their regular routes.

## How to read this data  
**ITS_Device** — The type of traffic management technology, like "Ramp Meter" in the sample.  
**Route_Name** — The highway where devices are located (e.g., "IR515").  
**Status** — Whether devices are operational ("Existing") or "Proposed."  
**Direction** — The compass direction of affected lanes (e.g., "Southbound").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular highway commute route (like IR515) has proposed traffic devices by searching the dataset for the route name and filtering for "Proposed" status.
