## What this tracks  
This dataset records cumulative mile markers along roadways in Nevada's Washoe County (WA), showing exact measurement points for routes like state highways.

## Why it matters to you  
When reporting a roadside emergency in Washoe County, knowing the exact mile marker helps first responders locate you faster. Truckers and delivery drivers use these markers to verify their position when planning routes or meeting clients along highways like SR443. The data also helps residents confirm their location when calling for roadside assistance or reporting hazards.

## How to read this data  
**PostedRoute** — The official highway designation (e.g., "SR443").  
**PostedMileage** — The mile marker number displayed on roadside signs (e.g., 3.0).  
**PostedCounty** — The county where the marker is located (always "WA" for Washoe County in this dataset).  
**Measure** — The precise engineering measurement of the location (e.g., 2.68538632).  

## Try it yourself  
Next time you're driving on a Washoe County highway like SR443, note the mile marker on roadside signs and compare it to the PostedMileage values in this dataset to verify your exact location.
