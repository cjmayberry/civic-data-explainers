## What this tracks  
This dataset records which roads in Nevada are maintained by the Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT) and their jurisdictional details.  

## Why it matters to you  
If your business depends on reliable road access—like a trucking company or delivery service—this data tells you whether NDOT or local authorities handle maintenance for your routes. In winter, NDOT-maintained roads may be plowed sooner than county roads, affecting your delivery schedules. For residents, knowing who maintains your road helps direct complaints about potholes or snow removal to the right agency.  

## How to read this data  
**RouteID** — A unique identifier for the road segment, like `10006CL`.  
**RouteNameFull** — The full name or description of the route, such as `IR11N TO SR599S INT90A`.  
**CountyCode** — The county where the road is located, abbreviated (e.g., `CL` for Clark County).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your street is NDOT-maintained by searching the dataset for your county code and route name.
