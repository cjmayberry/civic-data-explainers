## What this tracks  
This dataset records missing sidewalk features in Nevada that may need attention for ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) compliance.

## Why it matters to you  
If you use a wheelchair, walker, or stroller, this data tells you where Nevada’s sidewalks may be too narrow (like the 32" width noted in the sample) or missing entirely. Business owners can check if their storefront has accessible pathways, and residents can identify problem spots to report for repair. Knowing where gaps exist helps you plan safer routes.

## How to read this data  
**SidewalkLengthInFeet** — The length of the missing sidewalk segment in feet (e.g., "5").  
**Type** — A code indicating the kind of missing feature (sample value: "7").  
**Notes** — Details like "32" width" that explain specific accessibility issues.  
**Confirmed** — Whether the issue has been verified (sample: "Yes").

Check the map for missing sidewalks near your Nevada address to see if your usual walking route has accessibility gaps.
