## What this tracks  
This dataset records traffic count stations along Nevada highways, showing annual vehicle volumes at specific locations from 2007 through 2026.

## Why it matters to you  
Trucking companies can check **AADT_2023** values to compare traffic volumes on alternate routes like US50 vs SR305 when planning deliveries. Residents near **SR722 Carrol Summit Rd** can see if their commute corridor is getting busier by tracking the year-over-year AADT (Annual Average Daily Traffic) numbers. Nevada DOT uses this data to prioritize road repairs — higher traffic routes typically get maintenance first.

## How to read this data  
**LOCATION_D** — Describes where the counter is placed, like "US50 13.9 mi E of SR361 (Gabbs Valley Rd)"  
**AADT_2023** — The average daily traffic count for 2023 at that station (sample value: 670 vehicles/day)  
**ROUTE_NAME** — The highway being monitored, such as US50 or SR722  
**StationType** — Whether the counter is "Permanent" (always counting) or temporary  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your regular commute route (like US50) in the dataset to see current traffic volumes and whether they've increased over the past 5 years — this helps anticipate delays during peak seasons.
