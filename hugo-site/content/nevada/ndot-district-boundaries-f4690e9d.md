## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the geographic boundaries of Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT) maintenance districts across the state.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your NDOT district helps you report road maintenance issues to the correct team when you encounter potholes, damaged signs, or snow removal needs on state highways. If you're planning a business along a state route, this tells you which NDOT office handles permits for work in the right-of-way. Residents can use this to check which district handles winter road treatments before mountain travel.

## How to read this data  
**DISTRICT_N** — The numbered district (like "2" for Las Vegas area) responsible for maintaining roads in that region. Higher numbers typically indicate more rural northern districts.  
**created_date** — When this district boundary was last updated in NDOT's system (shown as a timestamp like 1716307174870).

## Try it yourself  
Visit Nevada's transportation website and enter your nearest state highway to see which district handles maintenance requests for your regular commute route.
