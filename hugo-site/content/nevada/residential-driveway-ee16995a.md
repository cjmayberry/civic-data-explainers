## What this tracks  
This dataset records measurements and compliance status of residential driveways in Nevada for ADA accessibility standards.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're modifying your driveway or dealing with accessibility issues, this data tells you whether your current setup meets legal requirements. Homeowners planning renovations can check compliance before hiring contractors, avoiding costly corrections. Nevada residents with mobility challenges can verify if their driveway's slope and width meet ADA standards for safe use.  

## How to read this data  
**Width** — The driveway's width in feet (e.g., 4.2 feet). ADA requires minimum widths for wheelchair access.  
**RunningSlope** — The slope along the driveway's length (e.g., 0.6%). Values above 5% may not comply with ADA standards.  
**ComplianceStatus** — Whether the driveway currently meets regulations (e.g., "Compliant" or non-compliant).  
**InspectionDate** — When the driveway was last assessed (e.g., converted from timestamp 1613510463000).
