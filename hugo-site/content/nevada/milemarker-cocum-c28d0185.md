## What this tracks  
This dataset records cumulative mile marker locations along transportation routes in Nevada's Washoe County (WA).

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing exact mile marker positions helps you verify locations when reporting roadside emergencies to 911 or NDOT. For business owners shipping goods, these markers provide precise reference points for delivery coordination along county routes like SR443. The data also clarifies jurisdiction — whether an issue falls under county (PostedCounty = WA) or state maintenance.

## How to read this data  
**PostedRoute** — The official route designation (e.g., "SR443").  
**PostedMileage** — The cumulative mile count (e.g., "3" means 3 miles from the route's starting point).  
**PanelColor** — Marker sign color code ("1" likely indicates standard green/white).  
**FromDate** — When the marker became active (e.g., 1524121200000 converts to April 19, 2018).  

## Try it yourself  
Next time you're on SR443, check your odometer against the nearest mile marker and compare it to the PostedMileage value in this dataset to verify your precise location.
