## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nevada's state legislative district boundaries.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your district helps you contact the right state senator or assembly member when advocating for local issues like school funding or road repairs. It shows which district's election results will directly impact your representation in Carson City. Before attending a town hall or voting in state elections, check this map to confirm which candidates appear on your ballot.

## How to read this data  
**DISTRICT_N** — Your district number (e.g., "2" for District 2), used to identify your representatives in the Nevada Legislature.  
**created_date** — When the district boundary was last updated (e.g., "1740175522723" as a timestamp), important for checking if redistricting may have changed your representation.

## Try it yourself  
Look up your address on Nevada's legislative district map to see which district number represents you at the state level.
