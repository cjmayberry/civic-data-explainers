## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the official boundaries of Metropolitan Planning Organizations (MPOs) in Nevada, which coordinate regional transportation projects.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your MPO boundary tells you which government body decides major road projects, bus routes, and bike lane expansions in your area. If you live in Washoe County, this data confirms whether your neighborhood's traffic congestion will be addressed by the Washoe County RTC or another agency. Business owners can use this to anticipate construction impacts or new transit access that might affect customer traffic.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The MPO's formal designation (e.g., "Washoe County Prelim").  
**COUNTY** — Which county the MPO primarily serves (e.g., "Washoe").  
**MPO_AREA** — The geographic scope described in plain terms (e.g., "Washoe County").  
**DATASOURCE** — Which agency provided the boundary data (e.g., "WC RTC" for Washoe County Regional Transportation Commission).
