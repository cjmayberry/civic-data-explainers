## What this tracks  
This dataset records proposed transportation projects in Nevada, including road improvements and technology installations planned by the Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT).

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning to open a business along Valley View Boulevard, knowing NDOT has a long-term project there for safety improvements could affect your location choice or construction timeline. Residents can check whether their commute route (like SR-613) has upcoming reliability upgrades before signing a lease. The "Priority" field reveals which projects might face delays if budgets tighten.

## How to read this data  
**Location** — Specific road segments being improved, like "Valley View Boulevard to SR-613 (Summerlin Pkwy)".  
**Timeframe** — Project phases (sample: "Long-term"); helps gauge when disruptions might occur.  
**Proposed Technologies** — Planned upgrades like "ITS Study" (Intelligent Transportation Systems) that may bring smart traffic signals or sensors to your area.  
**Priority** — Ranks urgency (sample: "Low"), indicating likely scheduling versus high-priority projects.

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your daily commute route or neighborhood name in the "Location" field to see planned NDOT projects that may affect your travel.
