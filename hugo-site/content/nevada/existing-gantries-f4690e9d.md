## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and identifiers of electronic toll collection gantries along Nevada highways.

## Why it matters to you  
If you frequently drive I-15 through Nevada, this data shows exactly where the state's electronic tolling system will charge your account. Trucking companies can verify their routes won't unexpectedly pass through toll zones. Commuters can check if their regular drive includes gantries between specific exits like Tropicana Avenue.

## How to read this data  
**GantryName** — The highway and nearest cross-street, like "I-15 NB @ S of W Tropicana Ave" for northbound gantries.  
**Latitude/Longitude** — Precise GPS coordinates (e.g., 36.09838, -115.18036) to map gantry locations.  
**LeftDMS/CenterDMS/RightDMS** — Unique identifiers (e.g., 10500) for each tolling sensor across multi-lane highways.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular commute passes a gantry by searching the dataset for highway names like "I-15" and cross-streets near your route.
