## What this tracks  
This dataset measures the accessibility features and compliance status of sidewalks, ramps, and pedestrian crossings in Nevada, NV, under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

## Why it matters to you  
If you use a wheelchair or walk with mobility aids, this data tells you whether ramps on your route have safe slopes (under 8.3%) and proper landings. Business owners can check if their storefronts meet accessibility standards before renovations, avoiding fines. Parents with strollers can identify smoother routes near schools or parks.

## How to read this data  
**RampSlopeCenter** — The steepness of a ramp's middle section (sample: 6.3% must be ≤8.3% to comply).  
**LandingWidth** — How wide the flat area at the top/bottom of a ramp is (sample: 4 feet; must be ≥4 feet).  
**ComplianceStatus** — Whether the feature meets ADA rules (sample: "Compliant" or non-compliant notes).  
**DWS_Width** — If detectable warning strips cover the full ramp width (sample: "DWS spans full width of ramp").  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your nearest crosswalk using Nevada's public ADA map to see if its ramp slopes are safe for wheelchairs (under 8.3%) or need reporting for repairs.
