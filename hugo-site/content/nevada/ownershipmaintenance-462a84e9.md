## What this tracks  
This dataset records which Nevada government agencies own and maintain specific sections of state roads.

## Why it matters to you  
If your business depends on reliable road access, this tells you who to contact when a pothole needs fixing on your delivery route. Nevada residents can check who's responsible for snowplowing on their commute route before winter hits. Knowing whether NDOT or local agencies maintain a road helps you report issues to the right place.

## How to read this data  
**RouteID** — The unique identifier for a road segment (e.g., "100002LY").  
**Ownership** — A code indicating which Nevada agency owns the road (sample value: 4).  
**Maintenance** — A code showing which agency handles repairs (sample value: 4).  
**Agency** — A numeric code for the responsible organization (sample value: 29).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your nearest state route number (like NV-439) and contact NDOT to verify who maintains the section you use daily.
