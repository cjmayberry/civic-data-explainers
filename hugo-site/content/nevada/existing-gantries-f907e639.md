## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and identifiers of overhead gantry structures along Nevada highways, primarily used for traffic monitoring or signage.

## Why it matters to you  
These gantries often hold speed cameras or electronic signs that display traffic alerts. Knowing their locations helps you anticipate speed enforcement zones when driving through Nevada, especially on I-15 near Tropicana Avenue. Trucking companies can use this to plan routes avoiding frequent enforcement areas, while commuters can check for congestion alerts before heading home.

## How to read this data  
**GantryName** — The highway and nearest cross-street (e.g., "I-15 NB @ S of W Tropicana Ave") shows where the structure is mounted.  
**Latitude/Longitude** — Precise coordinates (e.g., 36.09838, -115.18036) let you map the gantry's location.  
**LeftDMS/CenterDMS/RightDMS** — Unique IDs (e.g., 10500-10502) for each traffic lane's monitoring equipment.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if there's a gantry on your regular commute route by searching the coordinates in Google Maps alongside highway markers like "I-15 NB."
