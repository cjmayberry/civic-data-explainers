## What this tracks  
This dataset records proposed technology upgrades for Nevada's state highway system, including road weather sensors and communication equipment.

## Why it matters to you  
If you commute on SR-158 near Mount Charleston, knowing about the planned RWIS (Road Weather Information System) replacement helps you anticipate winter road condition updates. Local businesses along affected routes can check if construction timelines (when available) might impact deliveries. The "Priority" field shows which upgrades Nevada DOT considers most urgent for safety improvements.

## How to read this data  
**Location** — Specific highway and mile marker (e.g., "SR-158 near MP CL6") where upgrades will occur.  
**Prop_Tech** — Type of technology being installed (e.g., "RWIS" for weather sensors that detect ice conditions).  
**Risks_ROW** — Potential disruptions noted by engineers (e.g., "device not down during winter operations").  
**Priority** — NDOT's ranking of project urgency (sample shows "Medium" priority projects).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular commute route has pending technology upgrades by searching the dataset for highways like SR-158 or I-15 at [Nevada DOT's public maps](https://gis.dot.nv.gov/portal/).
