## What this tracks  
This dataset tracks planned Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) projects across Nevada's freeways and highways, including traffic management technologies like cameras, message signs, and adaptive signals.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing which ITS projects are planned for your commute route helps you anticipate future construction delays or improved traffic flow. If you're a trucking company planning deliveries between Las Vegas and Reno, this data shows where variable speed limits or ramp meters will be installed to reduce congestion. Nevada residents can see if their regular highway stretch will get new traffic cameras or dynamic message signs that provide real-time alerts.

## How to read this data  
**Title** — The highway name, like "FREEWAY" in the sample.  
**From_Loc/To_Loc** — The start and end points of the project segment (e.g., "I-15 at Tropicana Ave").  
**DMS** — Dynamic Message Signs (1 = planned installation, 0 = none).  
**Adapt_Sig** — Adaptive traffic signals (1 = planned, 0 = none) that adjust timing based on real-time traffic.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular Nevada highway commute (like US-95 or I-80) has planned ITS improvements by searching the dataset for your route number in the **Route** field.
