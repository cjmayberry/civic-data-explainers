## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and types of Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) devices along Nevada highways, such as ramp meters and traffic cameras.  

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where ITS devices are located helps you plan your commute by showing where traffic flow is actively managed. For example, seeing a ramp meter on your route means you may encounter metered merging during peak hours. Nevada drivers can also use this data to report malfunctioning devices by referencing the exact **RID** (Route ID) when contacting NDOT.  

## How to read this data  
**ITS_Device** — The type of traffic device (e.g., "Ramp Meter").  
**Route_Name** — The highway where the device is installed (e.g., "IR515" for Interstate 515).  
**Direction** — The compass direction of travel the device monitors (e.g., "Southbound").  
**MEAS** — The mile marker where the device is located (e.g., 0.21290454).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular highway on-ramp has a ramp meter by searching the dataset for your route (e.g., "IR515") and direction ("Northbound" or "Southbound").
