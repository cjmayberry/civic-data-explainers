## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the locations and ranges of Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) Automated Traffic Monitoring (ATM) devices in Nevada.

## Why it matters to you  
These sensors help manage traffic flow and detect congestion. For Nevada drivers, knowing where these devices are located can help you anticipate areas with real-time traffic monitoring — like near the UNLV Thomas & Mack Center — where you might encounter variable speed limits or incident alerts. Businesses planning delivery routes through Clark County can use this to identify stretches where traffic data is actively collected.

## How to read this data  
**Description** — The landmark or area where the device is located (e.g., "UNLV Thomas-Mack Center").  
**Range** — How far the device monitors traffic in miles (e.g., "25" miles).  
**Lat/Lon** — Exact device coordinates (e.g., 36.105, -115.14333 for the UNLV sensor).
