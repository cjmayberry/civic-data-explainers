## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nevada's linear road segments (LRS) with their identifiers, jurisdictions, and maintenance details.

## Why it matters to you  
When reporting a pothole on B ST, this data tells you whether Nevada's state DOT (RouteID 100002LY) or local government (JurisdictionCode 040) handles repairs. Business owners can verify which agency maintains roads near their storefront before investing in sidewalk improvements that might conflict with upcoming roadwork.

## How to read this data  
**RouteID** — A unique code combining the road number and county (e.g., 100002LY means route 100002 in Lyon County).  
**RouteNameFull** — The complete street name (e.g., "B ST").  
**JurisdictionCode** — Shows which government entity manages the road (e.g., 040 indicates state maintenance).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your street's RouteID in Nevada's GeoHUB to confirm whether road maintenance requests should go to NDOT or your local public works department.
