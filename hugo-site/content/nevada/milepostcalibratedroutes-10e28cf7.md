## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nevada's state-maintained road segments with their official milepost measurements for Linear Referencing System (LRS) mapping.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing exact milepost locations helps you pinpoint road conditions in official reports. If you're a trucker planning a route through Nevada, this data confirms which stretches of "B ST" are state-maintained versus local roads. Residents can use mileposts to reference pothole complaints or construction zones when contacting NDOT, ensuring crews locate issues faster.

## How to read this data  
**RouteNameFull** — The official road name (e.g., "B ST") as recognized by Nevada DOT.  
**MPMin/MPMax** — The start and end milepost measurements for the segment (e.g., -3e-08 to 0.21191844 means this stretch covers just beyond milepost 0).  
**SystemType** — A code (like "12") indicating the road classification, which affects maintenance schedules.  

## Try it yourself  
Next time you see a NDOT work crew on your route, note the nearest milepost marker (small green signs) and cross-reference it with this dataset to verify the road's maintenance jurisdiction.
