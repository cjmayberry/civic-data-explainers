## What this tracks  
This dataset records the location and accessibility features of pedestrian push buttons at crosswalks in Nevada, NV.

## Why it matters to you  
If you use a wheelchair or have limited mobility, this data tells you which crosswalk buttons meet ADA standards for height, reach, and activation force before you plan your route. Parents with strollers can check if buttons are positioned correctly for easy access. Business owners near problematic intersections (like those with "non-compliant" status in the Notes field) can advocate for faster repairs to improve customer access.

## How to read this data  
**ComplianceStatus** — Whether the button meets ADA standards, with values like "Compliant" or blank for non-compliant.  
**Button_Height** — How high the button is mounted (in inches), with sample value 40.75 (ADA requires ≤48").  
**Lateral_Reach** — Horizontal distance from the sidewalk edge to the button (in inches), with 0.0 meaning it's directly adjacent.  
**cross_time** — How many seconds pedestrians get to cross (sample: 63), helping you gauge if you have enough time.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your regular walking route in Nevada's online map to see if the crosswalk buttons have "Compliant" status and check their cross_time values to plan safer crossings.
