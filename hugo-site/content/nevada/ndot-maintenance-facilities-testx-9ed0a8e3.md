## What this tracks  
This dataset tracks Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT) maintenance facilities across the state, including their locations, types, and operational details.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where NDOT maintenance facilities are located helps Nevada residents understand winter road maintenance capabilities near their commute routes. If you regularly drive US-95 near Trinity, this data tells you there's a salt-sand storage facility at milepost 58.9 that services that stretch of highway. Trucking companies can check fuel availability at these facilities when planning long-haul routes through rural Nevada counties like Churchill.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — Facility name like "TRINITY" shown on maps and signs.  
**TYPE** — What the facility stores or handles, such as "SALT-SAND" for winter road treatment materials.  
**ROUTE_NUM** & **MILEPOST** — Exact highway location (e.g., "95" at "58.9" means 58.9 miles along US-95).  
**NDOT_STATUS** — Whether the facility is currently "OPEN" or closed to the public.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if there's an open NDOT maintenance facility within 10 miles of your regular winter driving route by searching the milepost numbers along highways like US-95 or I-80.
