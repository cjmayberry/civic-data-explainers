## What this tracks  
This dataset identifies roads maintained by the Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT) across the state.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a business along a state route or reporting a pothole, this tells you whether NDOT (not your local government) handles maintenance for that road. For example, knowing NDOT maintains IR11N means you'd contact them directly about repaving delays instead of the county. Snowplow schedules, construction alerts, and road condition updates all depend on who owns the stretch of road you use.

## How to read this data  
**RouteID** — A unique identifier like "10006CL" where "CL" stands for Churchill County.  
**RouteNameFull** — The official road description, such as "IR11N TO SR599S INT90A" (Interstate 11 North connecting to State Route 599 South near Interstate 90A).  
**JurisdictionCode** — A three-digit code like "044" indicating which NDOT district manages the route.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your commute route is NDOT-maintained by searching [NDOT's public map](https://www.dot.nv.gov/maps) for the RouteID or street name.
