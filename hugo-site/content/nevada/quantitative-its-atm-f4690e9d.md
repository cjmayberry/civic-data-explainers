## What this tracks  
This dataset records planned intelligent transportation system (ITS) projects across Nevada's roadways, including traffic monitoring devices and infrastructure upgrades.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing what traffic monitoring devices are planned for your commute route helps you anticipate future travel delays in Nevada. If you see "Dynamic Speed" devices coming to US-395, you'll know to expect variable speed limit signs that may slow your morning drive. Business owners near planned CCTV camera installations can prepare for potential construction impacts on customer access. The data reveals where NDOT is prioritizing traffic flow improvements across urban and rural areas.

## How to read this data  
**Route** — The highway being upgraded (e.g., US-395).  
**ProjectPa** — The project type and scope (e.g., "Urban Lite - Permanent Full").  
**Dynamic_Sp** — Whether dynamic speed signs will be installed (1 = yes, 0 = no).  
**CCTV** — Count of planned traffic cameras (sample shows 0 meaning none planned for that segment).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular commute route has any planned ITS devices by searching the dataset for highways like US-395 or I-80 and looking at the device fields (CCTV, DMS, etc.).
