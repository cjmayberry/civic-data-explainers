## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all public community centers in Memphis, including their locations and contact information.  

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where your nearest community center is located helps you access free or low-cost programs like fitness classes, senior activities, and youth programs. If you're planning a family event or looking for a local meeting space, this data tells you which centers are closest to your neighborhood and how to contact them. For small businesses, sponsoring events at these centers can be a way to reach local customers.  

## How to read this data  
**Community Center** — The name of the facility (e.g., "Bert Ferguson Community Center").  
**street** — The full street address (e.g., "8505 Trinity Road").  
**phone** — The center's contact number (e.g., "901-636-0705").  
**website** — A link to more details about programs and hours (e.g., "https://memphisparks.com/park/bert-ferguson-park-community-center/").  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for centers in your ZIP code (like 38018) to find the closest one and check its website for upcoming events.
