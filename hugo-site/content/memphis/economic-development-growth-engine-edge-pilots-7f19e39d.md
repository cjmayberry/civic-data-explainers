## What this tracks  
This dataset records tax incentive agreements (PILOTs) granted to businesses through Memphis' Economic Development Growth Engine (EDGE) program.

## Why it matters to you  
When a local manufacturer gets tax breaks, your neighborhood might gain jobs but lose tax revenue for schools and roads. This data shows whether companies in your council district are creating the promised jobs (like the 119 positions at Morgan Steel) or just reducing their tax bills. A small business owner could check if competitors received incentives before negotiating their own lease or expansion plans.

## How to read this data  
**ProjectType** — The industry receiving incentives, like "Manufacturing" or "Warehousing."  
**ProjectedNewJobs** — How many positions the company promised to create (sample: "119").  
**ProjectedTaxesAbatedCity** — Estimated property taxes Memphis won't collect (sample: "$497,954.00").  
**BenefitCostRatio** — Whether the deal benefits the public (values above 1.0 are positive; sample: "6.18").  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your City Council district number in the dataset to see which companies near you are receiving tax breaks and what they promised in return.
