## What this tracks  
This dataset records loans issued by Memphis's Economic Development Growth Engine (EDGE) to local businesses and projects.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business in Memphis, this data shows where the city is investing in economic development—like facade improvements or equipment purchases—which could signal upcoming growth in specific neighborhoods. For residents, it reveals which areas might see new jobs or revitalized storefronts, helping you decide where to rent, buy property, or shop locally.  

## How to read this data  
**CompanyName** — The business receiving the loan, like "JTAP Kitchen, LLC."  
**LoanUse** — How the funds will be spent, such as "Facade Improvements" or equipment purchases.  
**ProjectedNewJobs** — The number of jobs the loan aims to create (e.g., "7").  
**CityCouncilDistrict** — The district where the project is located (e.g., "5").  

## Try it yourself  
Search for loans in your council district to see if nearby businesses are receiving funding for renovations or expansions that could impact your neighborhood.
