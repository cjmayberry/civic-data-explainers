## What this tracks  
This dataset records the percentage of Memphis Area Transit Authority (MATA) buses, MATAplus paratransit vehicles, and trolleys that arrived on time each month.

## Why it matters to you  
If you rely on MATA buses to commute to work, this data tells you how likely your route is to run late — helping you decide whether to leave earlier or explore alternatives. For small businesses near trolley stops, knowing on-time performance helps assess whether promoting trolley access will reliably bring customers. Parents can check if MATAplus meets its schedule before trusting it for children's medical appointments.

## How to read this data  
**MATABus** — The percentage of regular MATA buses that arrived on time that month (e.g., 50 means half were late).  
**MATAplus** — The on-time rate for wheelchair-accessible paratransit services (e.g., 79 means 79% arrived as scheduled).  
**Trolley** — The on-time percentage for downtown trolleys (e.g., "90" means 90% were punctual).
