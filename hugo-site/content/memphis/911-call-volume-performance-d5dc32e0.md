## What this tracks  
This dataset measures monthly 911 call volumes and response performance metrics in Memphis.

## Why it matters to you  
When seconds count, this data shows whether emergency calls are being answered quickly enough. If **Percent_Answered_in_20_sec** is low in your area, you might choose to program the non-emergency police line into your phone. The **Average_Total_Time** (39.6 seconds in the sample) tells you how long callers typically wait before speaking to a dispatcher — critical information when reporting fires or medical emergencies.

## How to read this data  
**Count_of_Calls** — Total 911 calls received that month (e.g., 67,391 in the sample). Higher numbers may indicate busier periods for emergencies.  
**Percent_Abandoned** — Calls disconnected before being answered (29.1% in the sample). High rates suggest system overload.  
**Average_Queue_Time** — Seconds callers wait in queue (35.3 in sample). Compare this to the **Total_Time_National_Goal** of 10 seconds.
