## What this tracks  
This dataset records economic development projects managed by the Downtown Memphis Commission, including renovations, new constructions, and major investments in the downtown area.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business downtown, this data shows where major developments are happening—helping you spot high-traffic areas or avoid locations near upcoming construction. Residents can use it to see what new amenities (like hotels or retail spaces) are coming to their neighborhood, or check if a property they're interested in is part of a larger redevelopment plan.  

## How to read this data  
**ProjectName** — The official name of the project, like "Bass Pro Shops at the Pyramid."  
**ProjectType** — Whether it's a renovation, new construction, or other category (sample: "Renovation").  
**Retail** — A "1" means the project includes retail space (sample: 1), while "0" means it doesn’t.  
**Status** — Tells you if the project is completed, in progress, or planned (sample: "Completed").  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your street or neighborhood in the [Downtown Memphis Commission Projects map](https://www.downtownmemphiscommission.com/) to see active developments near you.
