## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the seven City Council districts in Memphis, showing which voting precincts fall within each district's boundaries.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your council district helps you identify your representative for local issues like pothole repairs, zoning changes, or neighborhood safety initiatives. When Memphis residents need to voice concerns about city services, they must contact the council member representing their specific district. This data also shows which district boundaries changed in the 2023 redistricting, affecting who represents you.

## How to read this data  
**CD** — The council district number (1 through 7). If your address falls in CD 3, you'd contact Council Member XYZ for assistance.  
**Shape_Leng** — The perimeter length of the district in feet (sample: 327,719 ft for District 1), showing how boundaries wrap around neighborhoods.

## Try it yourself  
Visit Memphis' district lookup tool and enter your address to confirm which council member represents you after the 2023 boundary changes.
