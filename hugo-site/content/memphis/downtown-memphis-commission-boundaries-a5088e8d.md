## What this tracks  
This dataset defines the boundaries of areas managed by the Downtown Memphis Commission (DMC).

## Why it matters to you  
If you own a business or property in downtown Memphis, knowing whether you're inside a DMC boundary determines if you qualify for special grants, facade improvement programs, or business development services. Residents can check if their neighborhood falls under DMC initiatives like clean-and-safe patrols or streetscape projects.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The official designation of the DMC-managed area (e.g., "CBID" for Central Business Improvement District). This tells you which specific district's programs and services apply to a location.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your business address falls within a DMC boundary to see if you're eligible for their small business grants at [memphis.edu/downtown](https://www.memphis.edu/downtown).
