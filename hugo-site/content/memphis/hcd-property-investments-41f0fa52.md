## What this tracks  
This dataset records affordable housing investments in Memphis, including project details, funding sources, and property types.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're looking for affordable rental or homeownership opportunities in Memphis, this data shows where recent investments have been made. Small landlords can see which neighborhoods are receiving rehabilitation funds, while renters can identify newly available affordable units. Homebuyers can track developments in specific council districts like Frayser (District 7) where the "Pershing Park" project added 76 units.  

## How to read this data  
**Project_Type** — Describes the housing focus, like "Affordable Homeownership & Rental Housing" or specific development types.  
**Status** — Shows whether projects are "Complete" (like the sample) or in progress.  
**Units_HHs** — The number of housing units created (sample shows 76 units at Pershing Park).  
**Planning_District** — Neighborhood location (sample shows Frayser district).  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your council district (7 in the sample) to see active affordable housing projects near you.
