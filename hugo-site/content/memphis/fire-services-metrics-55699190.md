## What this tracks  
This dataset measures the Memphis Fire Department's monthly performance, including 911 call response times, incident counts, and public outreach efforts.

## Why it matters to you  
When choosing a home or business location in Memphis, this data shows how quickly fire crews typically reach different areas — a critical factor for property safety and insurance rates. The **Fire_response_320_sec** metric tells you whether crews meet the 5-minute target for residential fires in your neighborhood. The **Smoke_alarm_outreach** count reveals where the department is actively installing free detectors, which could save you from buying your own.

## How to read this data  
**F911_answer_time_15_sec** — Percentage of 911 calls answered within 15 seconds (sample: 92.05 means 92% of calls met this standard).  
**EMS_ALS_response_540_sec** — Percentage of advanced life support ambulance calls that arrived within 9 minutes (sample: 78.72 means 79% met this goal).  
**Public_outreach** — Total safety education events conducted (sample: 12,122 events in one month shows active community engagement).  

## Try it yourself  
Compare the **Fire_response_320_sec** percentage from recent months to see if response times in your ZIP code are improving or declining — this affects both emergency outcomes and property insurance premiums.
