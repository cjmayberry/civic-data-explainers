## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the geographic boundaries of Memphis Police Department precincts.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your precinct helps you quickly find the right police contacts for neighborhood concerns. If you're reporting a non-emergency issue like a suspicious vehicle or noise complaint, this tells you which precinct office to call. Business owners can use this to identify their precinct's community liaison officer for safety consultations.

## How to read this data  
**precinct** — The name of the police precinct (e.g., "Austin Peay").  
**prct_code** — A numeric identifier for the precinct (e.g., 1).  
**Area_SqMi** — The precinct's size in square miles (e.g., 38.3).  

## Try it yourself  
Find your precinct by matching your address to the map at [Memphis Open Data portal], then note the precinct name for future police non-emergency calls.
