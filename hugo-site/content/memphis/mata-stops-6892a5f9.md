## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and details of all Memphis Area Transit Authority (MATA) bus stops.

## Why it matters to you  
If you rely on public transit in Memphis, this data tells you exactly where to catch your bus and confirms whether a stop exists near your home or workplace. Small businesses can use it to verify if their location is accessible by transit, which may influence hiring decisions or customer outreach. Commuters can check if their regular stops have changed before heading out.

## How to read this data  
**stop_name** — The cross streets or landmark where the stop is located, like "DOUBLE TREE STREET at PARKROSE ROAD".  
**stop_desc** — Additional identifying information, often including the route number serving that stop, such as "no 81".  
**stop_lat** and **stop_lon** — The precise GPS coordinates (e.g., 35.025435, -90.103444) for mapping the stop's location.  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your nearest stop by entering your address in [MATA's trip planner](https://www.matatransit.com/plan-your-trip/) and cross-checking with this dataset's coordinates.
