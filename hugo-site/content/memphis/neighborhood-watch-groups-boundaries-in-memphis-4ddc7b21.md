## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the boundaries of active Neighborhood Watch groups in Memphis, TN.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing whether your block has an active Neighborhood Watch helps you assess local safety efforts and decide whether to join or start one. If you're considering moving to a new area, this data reveals which communities have organized crime prevention programs. For small businesses, it highlights areas where commercial districts partner with residents on safety initiatives.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The official name of the Neighborhood Watch group, like "Appletree Apartments" (from sample data).  
**FolderPath** — Shows the organizational structure, such as "Neighborhood Watch Groups, Memphis TN/Austin Peay NWG Boundaries" (from sample data), indicating which police precinct or area the group falls under.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your apartment complex or street has an active Neighborhood Watch by searching the map for your address at Memphis' open data portal.
