## What this tracks  
This dataset records all building permits issued by Memphis for construction projects, including renovations and new builds.

## Why it matters to you  
Before buying a home in Memphis, you can check if recent permits were issued for major renovations — like the "Vinyl Siding Installation" example — to assess neighborhood investment. Small contractors can see typical project valuations ($13,733 for an alteration in the sample) to price competitively. Homeowners near Millington can spot if adjacent lots have new construction permits that might affect property values.

## How to read this data  
**Sub_Type** — Whether the permit is for Residential or Commercial work (sample: "Residential").  
**Construction_Type** — The kind of project, like "Alteration" (sample) or "New Construction".  
**Valuation** — The estimated cost of work in dollars (sample: 13733.89).  
**Description** — Specifics like "Vinyl Siding Installation" (sample) or roof repairs.  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your street address in the [Memphis permit portal](https://www.memphistn.gov/) to see if neighbors have recent renovation permits that could signal upcoming construction noise or property value changes.
