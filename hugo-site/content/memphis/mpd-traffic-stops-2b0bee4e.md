## What this is  
A daily-updated record of traffic stops made by the Metropolitan Police Department (MPD).

## Why it matters to you  
This data helps residents understand where and why police are stopping drivers in their city. If you drive, bike, or walk near high-stop areas, you might see more police presence. Neighborhood groups use this to discuss policing priorities with local precincts. Businesses near frequent stop locations may notice impacts on customer parking or foot traffic.

## How to read this data  
Since no data dictionary is provided, we can only confirm this dataset tracks:  
- **Location**: Where stops occur (though exact address precision isn’t specified)  
- **Timing**: Daily updates suggest dates/times may be included  
- **Separate citations**: Traffic tickets are recorded elsewhere (per the description)  

## Where this leaves you  
While this dataset hints at traffic enforcement patterns, the lack of detailed fields means you can’t yet look up stops by your block or see reasons for stops. For official reports, contact MPD directly.
