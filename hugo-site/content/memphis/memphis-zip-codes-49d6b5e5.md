## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the geographic boundaries of all ZIP codes in Memphis, TN.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing exact ZIP code boundaries helps you verify which services and tax rates apply to your property. A business owner near a ZIP code line could discover they're actually in a different sales tax district than they assumed. Residents can confirm which post office serves their address when tracking packages or voting by mail.

## How to read this data  
**GEOID** — The 5-digit ZIP code (e.g., 38002 for parts of Cordova).  
**INTPTLAT/INTPTLON** — The latitude/longitude of the ZIP code's center point (e.g., +35.2863553, -89.7002741 for 38002).  
**ALAND** — Total land area in square meters (e.g., 301,517,800 for 38002 - about 116 square miles).  

## Try it yourself  
Check which ZIP code contains your home by matching your address's coordinates to these boundaries at [Memphis Open Data Portal](https://data.memphistn.gov).
