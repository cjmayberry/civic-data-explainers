## What this tracks  
This dataset records all public parks in Memphis, including their names, sizes, ownership, and locations.

## Why it matters to you  
When choosing a neighborhood to live in, this data shows which parks are nearby and how large they are. For parents, it reveals which parks are maintained by Memphis Parks versus other entities, helping you know where to report maintenance issues. Small businesses near parks can use this to assess foot traffic potential from park visitors.

## How to read this data  
**Park_Name** — Official name of the park, like "Oakley Park".  
**Park_Size_Acres** — Physical size of the park in acres (sample: 689.63).  
**Park_Local_Manager** — Which organization maintains the park (sample: "MEMPHIS PARKS").  
**Park_Status** — Whether the park is currently open or closed (sample: "Open").  

## Try it yourself  
Search for parks within your ZIP code (like 38128) to find recreational spaces near your home or business.
