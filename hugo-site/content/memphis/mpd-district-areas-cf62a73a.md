## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries of Memphis Police Department's 9 precinct stations across 2 districts.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your MPD precinct helps you identify the right station to contact for non-emergency police services like filing reports or checking crime trends. If your business is considering a new location, this data shows which precinct would respond to calls there. Residents can also use this to understand which community policing initiatives serve their neighborhood.

## How to read this data  
**district** — The main division (1 or 2) containing multiple precincts. Sample value: 1  
**shape_leng** — The perimeter measurement of the district boundary in feet. Sample value: 359219.477428 shows how large the geographic area is  

## Try it yourself  
Visit [Memphis Open Data](https://data.memphistn.gov/) and overlay this dataset with crime reports to see which precinct handles incidents near your home or workplace.
