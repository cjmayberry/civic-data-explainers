## What this is  
This dataset tracks police ward boundaries in the city.

## Why it matters to you  
Police wards determine which officers patrol your neighborhood and where emergency calls are routed. If you report a crime or need urgent assistance, the responding unit typically comes from your assigned ward. Residents often use this information to:  
- Find their local community policing meetings  
- Compare response times between wards  
- Understand which station handles their neighborhood's safety concerns  

## How to read this data  
Since no data dictionary is provided, this dataset likely contains geographic boundaries (like ward shapes or IDs) but no detailed statistics about crime or response times. You'd typically see:  
- Ward numbers or names identifying patrol areas  
- Geographic coordinates or boundaries marking ward edges  

## Where this leaves you  
While you can't look up ward-specific crime data here, knowing your ward helps direct safety concerns to the right police station. Check the city's crime data portal separately for statistics tied to these boundaries.
