## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the two super districts that divide Memphis for city council representation, each made up of multiple voting precincts.  

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your super district helps you understand which council members represent you at the city level. If you're advocating for neighborhood improvements or reporting issues like potholes, this tells you which council members to contact. It also clarifies which district-level decisions (like zoning changes or infrastructure projects) directly affect your area of Memphis.  

## How to read this data  
**SD** — The super district number (sample value: 8). Memphis has two super districts, so this will be either 1 or 2.  
**Shape_Leng** — The perimeter length of the district in feet (sample: 519206.9377), used for mapping but not critical for residents.  

## Try it yourself  
Check which super district contains your home by entering your address on [Memphis’ council district lookup tool](https://www.memphistn.gov/government/city-council/council-districts/).
