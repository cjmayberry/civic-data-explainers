## What this tracks  
This dataset records all public safety incidents reported to the Memphis Police Department, including crimes like theft, assault, and homicide.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering moving to a new neighborhood in Memphis, this data shows you which areas have higher crime rates—helping you choose a safer location for your family. Business owners can use it to assess security risks before leasing a storefront or hiring additional security.  

## How to read this data  
**UCR_Category** — The type of crime, such as "HOMICIDE" or "THEFT."  
**Street_Address** — Where the incident occurred, like "3800 KELLEY CIR."  
**Offense_Datetime** — When the crime happened, stored as a timestamp (e.g., 1771047900000).  
**Ward** — The city ward where the incident took place (e.g., "322").  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your street name or ZIP code in the dataset to see recent incidents near your home or business.
