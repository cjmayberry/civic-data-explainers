## What this tracks  
This dataset records all existing and planned bike facilities (like lanes and paths) across Memphis.

## Why it matters to you  
If you bike to work or for errands, this data shows which streets already have bike lanes and which will get them soon. A small business owner could check if their storefront will gain bike access, helping them decide whether to add bike parking. Cyclists can plan safer routes by seeing where protected lanes exist versus shared roads.

## How to read this data  
**facility_n** — The street name with bike facilities, like "Fields Rd".  
**facility_c** — Type of bike facility, such as "Bike Lane" or (likely) "Shared Roadway".  
**status** — Whether the facility is "Existing" or likely "Programmed" (planned).  
**yr_constr** — The year a facility was built (e.g., 2014) or will be built for planned routes.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your street in the dataset to see if it has a bike lane or is scheduled to get one—this affects how safely you can cycle to nearby shops or transit.
