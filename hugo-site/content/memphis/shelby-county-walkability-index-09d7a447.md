## What this is  
A dataset measuring how walkable different areas of Shelby County are.

## Why it matters to you  
Walkability affects daily decisions like whether you can safely walk to school, transit stops, or local businesses instead of driving. Families might use this to compare neighborhoods when moving, while small businesses could gauge foot traffic potential. Poor walkability scores often correlate with higher transportation costs and fewer spontaneous community interactions.

- **Amenity proximity**: How close are shops, schools, or parks?

This dataset doesn’t let you look up walkability for a specific address, but it could help advocate for pedestrian improvements in your area by showing countywide patterns. Check the full dataset for broader trends.
