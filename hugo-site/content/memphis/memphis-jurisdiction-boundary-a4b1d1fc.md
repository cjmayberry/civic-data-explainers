## What this tracks  
This dataset defines the official boundary of Memphis city limits as established by local ordinances.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing whether a property falls inside Memphis city limits determines which services you receive (like trash pickup) and which taxes you pay. A South Cordova business owner could use this to verify if they need a Memphis business license or Shelby County permit. Residents near Windyke could check if their home falls under city police jurisdiction or county sheriff patrols.

## How to read this data  
**CITY** — Always "Memphis" in this dataset, confirming these boundaries apply to Memphis specifically.  
**SQUARE_MIL** — Shows total area in square miles (sample value 0 indicates this calculation needs updating from the geometry fields).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your address falls within Memphis city limits by comparing your location to the boundary map at [Memphis Open Data Portal], especially if you live near Southwind or Rocky Point areas affected by recent deannexations.
