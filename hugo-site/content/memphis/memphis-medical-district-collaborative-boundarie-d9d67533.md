## What this tracks  
This dataset defines the geographic boundaries of the Memphis Medical District Collaborative (MMDC) area.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a healthcare-related business in Memphis, knowing whether your location falls within the MMDC boundaries determines your eligibility for district-specific grants, tax incentives, and business support programs. Residents can use this to verify if their neighborhood is included in special improvement initiatives like streetscape projects or safety patrols funded by the collaborative.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — Identifies the district (sample value: "MMDC").  
**sq_miles** — Shows the area's size in square miles (sample value: 2.61276), helping you understand the district's scale.  
**shape_leng** — The perimeter length in feet (sample value: 41880.1085382), useful for understanding the district's boundaries.

## Try it yourself  
Check if your business address falls within the MMDC boundaries by comparing your location to the district map at memphismedicaldistrict.com.
