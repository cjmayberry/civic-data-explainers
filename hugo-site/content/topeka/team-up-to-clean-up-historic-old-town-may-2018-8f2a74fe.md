## What this tracks  
This dataset records a one-time community cleanup event in Topeka's Historic Old Town in May 2018, including staff hours spent and associated costs.

## Why it matters to you  
Seeing the city's investment in neighborhood cleanup (over 35 staff hours and $1,500 in labor costs for this single event) shows how seriously Topeka takes maintaining historic districts. If you're considering opening a business in Old Town, this demonstrates city support for keeping the area attractive. Residents can use this as a benchmark when requesting similar cleanup efforts for their neighborhoods.

## How to read this data  
**Location** — Specific area cleaned (always "Historic Old Town" in this dataset).  
**Est__Staff_Hours_to_Date** — Total hours city staff worked during cleanup (sample shows 35 hours).  
**Cost_of_Est__Staff_Hours_to_Date** — Dollar value of staff time spent (sample shows $1,552).  
**Cost_of_Actions_Taken_to_Date** — Additional non-labor expenses (sample shows $555 for materials/supplies).

## Try it yourself  
Contact Topeka's Community Engagement division to inquire about organizing a similar cleanup in your neighborhood, using these numbers as a reference for likely staffing and costs.
