## What this is  
This dataset tracks past, current, and planned bike lane and trail projects across Oklahoma City.

## Why it matters to you  
Bike infrastructure affects your commute, recreation, and neighborhood safety. Knowing which projects are finished (like a new protected lane on your route) or coming soon (like a trail connecting to a park) helps you:  
- Choose safer biking routes today  
- Advocate for gaps in your area  
- Anticipate construction delays near you  

## How to read this data  
Key details in each project listing:  
- **Project Name**: Usually the street or area (e.g., "NW 23rd St Bike Lanes")  
- **Status**: "Completed," "Ongoing," or "Planned" tells you if it’s ready to use  
- **Description**: Specific locations (e.g., "from Classen Blvd to Western Ave")  
- **Length**: How long the lane/trail is in feet (helpful for judging scope)  

## Where this leaves you  
While you can’t look up projects by your exact address here, scanning the full list helps spot nearby work. Check the map view (if available) to see routes visually.
