## What this is  
This dataset shows recycling pickup zones in Oklahoma City, including who collects your recycling and which day they come.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your recycling zone helps you:  
- Put bins out on the correct day to avoid missed pickups  
- Contact the right service provider if your recycling isn’t collected  
- Plan ahead if you’re moving and want to check pickup schedules in a new neighborhood  

## How to read this data  
Key fields include:  
- **Route**: The alphanumeric code for your pickup zone (e.g., "R7")  
- **Pickup Day**: The weekday or monthly schedule for your area (e.g., "Every other Tuesday")  
- **Service Provider**: Which company handles your recycling (useful for complaints or questions)  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your address in the [interactive map](https://data.okc.gov/) to find:  
1. Your exact pickup day  
2. Your route number  
3. The phone number for your service provider
