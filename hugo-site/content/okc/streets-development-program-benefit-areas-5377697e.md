## What this is  
A dataset showing where street development projects are planned in the city.

## Why it matters to you  
This data helps you understand if your street or neighborhood will see construction, repaving, or other infrastructure work soon. Knowing this can affect when you schedule home repairs (to avoid overlapping with roadwork), where you choose to open a business (to avoid construction zones), or even which routes to take during your commute. Municipal budgets prioritize certain areas over others—this shows where those dollars are going.

## How to read this data  
Since no field descriptions are available, look for:  
- **Location boundaries**: Likely shows streets or zones marked for development  
- **Project types**: May include repaving, utility work, or sidewalk upgrades  
- **Timelines**: If present, would show estimated start/end dates  

## Where this leaves you  
Without address-specific details, you can’t look up your exact block, but this dataset still reveals which general areas will see changes. Check the raw data or city announcements for project lists by neighborhood.
