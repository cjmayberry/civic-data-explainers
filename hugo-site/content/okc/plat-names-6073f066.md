## What this is  
A list of neighborhood plat names and their corresponding codes used in Oklahoma City.

## Why it matters to you  
Plats are the official recorded maps of neighborhoods and subdivisions. This data helps when:  
- Checking property records to see which plat your home is in  
- Understanding which neighborhood rules might apply to your property  
- Researching historical development patterns in your area  

## How to read this data  
- **Plat Code**: A number assigned to each plat (like 12345)  
- **Plat Name**: The official recorded name of the neighborhood or subdivision (like "Maplewood Heights Addition")  

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset won't tell you which plat contains a specific address, but it does provide the master list of all recognized neighborhood plats in the city. You'll need to cross-reference with other property records to find your exact plat.
