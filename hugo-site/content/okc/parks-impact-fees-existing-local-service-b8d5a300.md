## What this is  
This dataset tracks areas in Oklahoma City that are exempt from paying park development fees.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're developing property or building in Oklahoma City, these exemptions could save you money. The data helps determine whether your project falls in an area where park impact fees don't apply—affecting your construction budget and planning. Developers, small businesses expanding their lots, or homeowners adding structures all need to check this before finalizing plans.  

## How to read this data  
- **Park Served**: A number used to calculate whether fees apply to your location.  
- **Calculator**: A code that helps determine fee exemptions (like a category label).  
- **Area**: The size of the exempt zone in square feet—bigger areas mean more potential development sites are covered.  

## Where this leaves you  
While this data doesn’t let you look up exemptions by address directly, it shows where fee-free zones exist. For specific projects, you’d need to cross-reference this map with your site’s location (or consult city planning staff). More details on fees are at [okc.gov](https://www.okc.gov).
