## What this is  
A list of pedestrian trail projects in Oklahoma City, including past, current, and future work.  

## Why it matters to you  
Trails affect where you can walk, bike, or run safely—and which routes might be closed for construction. If you're planning a commute, exercise route, or neighborhood event, this data shows where paths are being added, upgraded, or repaired. Delays or detours near you could mean adjusting your routine.  

## How to read this data  
Key details in each record:  
- **Project Name**: Usually the street or area (e.g., "Riverside Trail Extension").  
- **Status**: Tells you if it’s planned, underway, or finished.  
- **Length**: How long the trail segment is (in feet).  
- **Description**: Specifics like cross-streets or landmarks.  

## Where this leaves you  
This data doesn’t let you search by your address, but scanning the list helps spot projects near places you frequent. Check the "Status" field to anticipate closures or new routes.
