## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Missouri alcohol licenses that have not been renewed for the upcoming license year, which begins July 1.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you own a bar, restaurant, or liquor store in Missouri, this data tells you which competitors or suppliers may lose their license to operate after July 1. For example, if you're negotiating a wholesale contract with DIAGEO AMERICAS INC., seeing their unrenewed license could signal delivery disruptions. Residents planning events can also check if their preferred vendors risk losing serving rights.  

## How to read this data  
**licensee_name** — The legal business name holding the license (e.g., "DIAGEO AMERICAS INC.").  
**dba_name** — The "doing business as" name if different (e.g., "DIAGEO AMERICAS").  
**license_type** — The license category (e.g., "Liquor Solicitor - Out-State" for wholesalers operating outside Missouri).  
**county** — Where the business is based ("00 - OUT OF STATE" for non-Missouri entities).
