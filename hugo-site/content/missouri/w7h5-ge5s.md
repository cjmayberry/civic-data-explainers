## What this tracks  
This dataset lists locally owned businesses in Missouri that participate in the Buy Missouri program, which promotes shopping at local establishments.

## Why it matters to you  
When you need to hire a contractor or buy supplies, this data helps you find Missouri-owned alternatives to national chains — keeping your money in the local economy. For example, before hiring an IT consultant from a nationwide firm, you could check this list for qualified local tech companies like ZSI-Foster in Springfield. Supporting these businesses directly strengthens job growth in your community.

## How to read this data  
**business** — The name of the local business (e.g., "ZSI-Foster").  
**county** — Where the business operates (e.g., "Greene").  
**location_1** — Contains the city (e.g., "Springfield") and exact coordinates if available.  
**website** — Direct link to the business's online presence (e.g., "https://www.zsi-inc.com/").  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for businesses in your county by filtering the "county" field, then visit their websites to compare services or products before your next purchase.
