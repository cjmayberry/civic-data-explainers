## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the average financial assistance received by households in Missouri cities through energy aid programs.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're struggling to pay utility bills in Missouri, this data tells you whether your city's residents typically qualify for meaningful assistance — and how much help to expect. A small business owner might use this to gauge whether customers in their area likely have disposable income after covering basic utilities. Seeing Adrian residents receive $670 on average could prompt you to apply before winter.

## How to read this data  
**geocoded_city_name** — Your city (e.g., "Adrian"), confirming where the benefits were distributed.  
**average_benefit_received** — The typical annual amount per household (e.g., "$670.76"), helping you budget for energy costs.  
**benefit_recipient_count** — How many households received aid (e.g., "34"), indicating program reach in your community.
