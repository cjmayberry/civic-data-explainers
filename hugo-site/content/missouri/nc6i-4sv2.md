## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Missouri-certified business incubators that help startups and small businesses grow, including their contact details and locations.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're launching a small business in Missouri, these incubators offer free or low-cost workspace, mentoring, and networking. Knowing which ones are nearby in St. Louis or other cities helps you tap into resources faster than searching blindly. For example, a bakery startup could use this to find incubators with commercial kitchen access instead of leasing expensive private space.

## How to read this data  
**incubator** — Official name of the program (e.g., "BEGIN New Venture Center").  
**sponsor** — Parent organization backing the incubator (e.g., "St. Patrick’s Center").  
**street_address** / **city** — Physical location (e.g., "800 N. Tucker Blvd, St. Louis").  
**contact_person** / **phone_number** — Direct liaison for applicants (e.g., "Susie Wildman, (314) 802-0995").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if there’s a certified incubator near your ZIP code by searching the [Missouri Business Portal](https://business.mo.gov/) with your address.
