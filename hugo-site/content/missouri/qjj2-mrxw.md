## What this tracks  
This dataset lists licensed ambulatory surgery centers (ASCs) in Missouri, including their contact details and accreditation status.

## Why it matters to you  
If you need outpatient surgery, this data helps you verify whether a facility near you is properly licensed and accredited before scheduling a procedure. For small healthcare businesses, it shows competitor locations and their certification statuses — critical when choosing where to establish a new clinic in Kansas City or evaluating partnership opportunities.

## How to read this data  
**facility_name** — The legal name of the surgery center (e.g., "Creekwood Surgery Center, LP").  
**accredited** — Whether the facility meets national standards (sample value: "True").  
**state_license_expiration_date** — When the facility's Missouri license expires (e.g., "06/30/2027").  
**county** — The county where the facility operates (sample: "CLAY").
