## What this tracks  
This dataset lists locations in Missouri that provide Missouri Works Assistance (MWA) services, including their addresses, hours, and contact information.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're looking for job training, career counseling, or employment support services in Missouri, this data helps you find the nearest assistance center. Knowing the hours (**Mon - Fri 8:00 am to 5:00 pm**) prevents wasted trips to closed locations, while the phone number lets you confirm services before visiting. Small businesses can refer employees or job seekers to these verified resources.

## How to read this data  
**center** — The name of the assistance location (e.g., *LINC-MWA*).  
**additional_information** — Operating hours (e.g., *Mon - Fri 8:00 am to 5:00 pm*).  
**phone_number** — Direct contact for the center (e.g., *816-303-0660*).  
**location_1** — Full street address, city, and ZIP (e.g., *3325 Prospect, Kansas City, MO 64128*), plus GPS coordinates.  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your ZIP code in the [human_address](#) field to find the closest Missouri Works Assistance center. Call the listed **phone_number** to ask about walk-in availability.
