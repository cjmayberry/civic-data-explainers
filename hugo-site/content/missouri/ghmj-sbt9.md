## What this tracks  
This dataset lists pregnancy assistance providers in Missouri, including their locations, contact details, and service areas.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're expecting a child or supporting someone who is, this data helps you find local clinics, health centers, and support services near you. For example, a resident in Dent County could use this to confirm whether the Dent County Health Center offers prenatal classes before making an appointment. Small businesses offering family-friendly services might also use this to identify nearby providers for partnerships or referrals.  

## How to read this data  
**facility_name** — The name of the provider, like "Dent County Health Center."  
**physical_address** — The street address where services are offered, such as "1010 E. Scenic Rivers Blvd."  
**counties_served** — Which counties the provider assists, like "Dent."  
**days_and_hours_of_operations** — When the facility is open, e.g., "Monday,Tuesday,Wednesday,Thursday,Friday."  

## Try it yourself  
Check if there’s a pregnancy assistance provider near you by searching for your county in the **counties_served** field.
