## What this tracks  
This dataset lists electric and telephone utility providers across Missouri cities and counties.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're moving to a new home in Missouri, this tells you whether you'll need to call a rural electric cooperative or a major provider to set up power. For small businesses, knowing whether AT&T or another provider serves an area could determine your internet speed options before signing a lease. When comparing properties in different counties, this data reveals whether you'll face higher cooperative rates versus municipal electricity.

## How to read this data  
**city** — The municipality where utilities are available, like "ADAIR"  
**e_type** — Ownership type of electric provider, with values like "COOP" (cooperative) or "MUNI" (municipal)  
**electric** — Name of power company, such as "TRI-COUNTY ELECTRIC COOPERATIVE ASSN."  
**telephone** — Landline provider for the area, like "AT&T MISSOURI"
