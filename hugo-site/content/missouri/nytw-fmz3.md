## What this tracks  
This dataset records alcohol licenses for businesses in Missouri that have permanently closed.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a bar, restaurant, or liquor store in Missouri, this data helps you identify locations where similar businesses recently failed. You can cross-reference these addresses with foot traffic patterns or local demographics to avoid risky locations. For residents, it reveals which neighborhood establishments serving alcohol have closed—useful when tracking changes in local business districts.  

## How to read this data  
**licensee_name** — The legal name of the business (e.g., "MADE IN KC LLC").  
**dba_name** — The "doing business as" name visible to customers (e.g., "MADE IN KC").  
**status_effective_date** — When the business was marked "Out of Business" (e.g., "06/16/2026").  
**street_name** and **street_number** — The exact address of the closed business (e.g., "325 E 31ST STREET STE 105").  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your ZIP code (e.g., "64108") to see which bars or liquor stores near you have recently closed.
