## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all licensed long-term care facilities in Missouri, including nursing homes, assisted living facilities, and residential care facilities.

## Why it matters to you  
When choosing care for an aging parent, this data helps you compare facilities by location, capacity, and services offered. It tells you whether a facility near your Missouri home specializes in Alzheimer's care or accepts Medicare/Medicaid. You can also check how many beds a facility has before scheduling a tour.

## How to read this data  
**facility_name** — The official name of the care facility, like "BAPTIST HOMES OF ADRIAN".  
**level_of_care** — The type of services provided, such as "SNF" (Skilled Nursing Facility) or "ALF" (Assisted Living Facility).  
**capacity** — How many residents the facility can accommodate, shown as a number like "38".  
**alzheimer_s_scu** — Whether the facility has a special Alzheimer's care unit, marked "True" or "False".

## Try it yourself  
Search for facilities near your Missouri ZIP code using the address field to find options within driving distance of your family.
