## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all licensed lodging establishments (hotels, motels, etc.) in Missouri, including their locations and contact information.

## Why it matters to you  
When planning a trip within Missouri, this data helps you verify legitimate lodging options and avoid unlicensed operators. Small business owners in tourism can use it to research competitors' locations or identify underserved areas for new lodging ventures. Event planners can quickly find accommodations near venues by cross-referencing this list with local maps.

## How to read this data  
**establishment_name** — The business name (e.g., "INDEPENDENCE MY PLACE").  
**establishment_address_2** — The street address (e.g., "20105 EAST VALLEY PARKWAY").  
**establishment_city** and **establishment_zip_code** — Location details (e.g., "INDEPENDENCE", "64057").  
**telephone_number** — Direct contact (e.g., "816-491-2598").  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your zip code to see all licensed lodging options near your home or workplace.
