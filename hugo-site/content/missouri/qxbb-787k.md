## What this tracks  
This dataset lists locations of Excel Centers in Missouri — public high schools specifically designed for adult learners to earn diplomas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you or someone you know dropped out of high school, this data helps find the nearest adult education center to complete a diploma. A St. Louis resident working night shifts could compare locations to find a center with evening classes along their commute route. Local employers hiring for positions requiring diplomas can refer applicants to these verified resources.

## How to read this data  
**center** — Name of the adult high school location (e.g., "The Excel Center (Adult High School)-St. Louis").  
**address_1** — Street address where classes are held (e.g., "1727 Locust St.").  
**zip_code** — Shows which neighborhoods each center serves (e.g., "63103" for downtown St. Louis).  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for centers near your ZIP code to find the most convenient location, then visit the school's website for class schedules and enrollment details.
