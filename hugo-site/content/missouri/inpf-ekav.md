## What this tracks  
This dataset lists businesses in Missouri licensed to sell cigarettes and other tobacco products.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a convenience store or smoke shop, this tells you which competitors already sell tobacco nearby. Parents can check whether a store near their child's school holds a tobacco license. Missouri residents can verify if a seller displaying tobacco products is properly licensed before reporting violations.

## How to read this data  
**licensee_name** — Business name (e.g., "ST. JOSEPH TOBACCO COMPANY DBA SAINT JOE DISTRIBUTING")  
**cigarette** — "CIG" means licensed for cigarette sales  
**other_tobacco_products** — "OTP" means licensed for vaping/e-cigarettes, cigars, etc.  
**retailer** — "NO" indicates wholesale distributors (not storefronts)  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your ZIP code to see all licensed tobacco sellers near your home or business.
