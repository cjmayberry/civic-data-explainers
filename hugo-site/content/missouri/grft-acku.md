## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all Medicare-certified hospice providers licensed by the state of Missouri.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you or a family member needs end-of-life care, this data helps you compare nearby hospice providers by location, service area, and ownership type. Knowing which providers serve your county (like Franklin or St. Louis) saves time when researching options under time-sensitive circumstances. The license expiration dates also show whether a provider’s certification is current.  

## How to read this data  
**facname** — The provider’s name, like *AccentCare Hospice & Palliative Care of Missouri*.  
**address** — Physical location, including street and suite (e.g., *3660 S. Geyer Road - Suite 120*).  
**servicearea_county** — Which counties the provider serves (e.g., *FRANKLIN* means they don’t cover St. Louis).  
**licyrexpires** — When the license expires (e.g., *2026-11-30* means this provider is certified for 3 more years).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if any providers in this list serve your county by matching the *servicearea_county* field to your location.
