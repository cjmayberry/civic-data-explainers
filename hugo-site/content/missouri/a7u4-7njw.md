## What this tracks  
This dataset lists outpatient physical therapy providers in Missouri, including their locations and contact information.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're recovering from surgery or managing chronic pain, this data helps you find nearby physical therapy clinics without relying solely on doctor referrals. A small business owner opening a medical office could use this to identify service gaps in specific counties like St. Charles. Knowing whether a provider is for-profit (marked with ✓) helps residents make informed choices about their healthcare spending.

## How to read this data  
**facname** — The clinic's name, like "Associated Rehabilitation Services, Inc."  
**address** — Full street address including city (e.g., "11210 SCHUETZ ROAD, St Louis")  
**ownership4profit** — A checkmark (✓) means the clinic operates for profit  

## Try it yourself  
Search for clinics near your ZIP code by filtering the "zip" field to find options within your insurance network.
