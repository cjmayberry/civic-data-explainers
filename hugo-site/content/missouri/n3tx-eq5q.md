## What this tracks  
This dataset records expiration dates for temporary alcohol licenses issued to businesses in Missouri.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning an event at a Missouri venue, this tells you whether their temporary alcohol license will still be valid on your event date. For business owners, checking nearby competitors' license expirations helps time your own license renewals strategically. In Branson, where many resorts hold temporary licenses, this data prevents last-minute surprises about alcohol service availability.

## How to read this data  
**licensee_name** — The legal business name (e.g., "THE BOWL SPOT LLC").  
**license_type** — The specific permit type (e.g., "Retail by Drink - Resort Temporary").  
**expiration_date** — When the license becomes invalid (e.g., "09/07/2026").  
**city** — Where the business operates (e.g., "BRANSON").
