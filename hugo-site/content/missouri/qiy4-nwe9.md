## What this tracks  
This dataset lists contact information and locations for all local public health agencies across Missouri.

## Why it matters to you  
When your child needs immunization records for school or you suspect a foodborne illness outbreak at a local restaurant, this data tells you exactly which health department serves your county and how to reach them. For small business owners, knowing your district's health agency contact helps with food service permits and sanitation inspections. In Missouri, public health responses start at these local agencies.

## How to read this data  
**lpha** — The official name of the health department (e.g., "Adair County Health Department").  
**street_address** — Physical location (e.g., "1001 South Jamison" in Kirksville).  
**phone** — Direct line to the agency (formatted like "660/665-8491").  
**district** — Two-letter region code (like "NE" for Northeast District).  

## Try it yourself  
Find your county's health department by searching the dataset for your county name, then save their phone number and address for future public health needs.
