## What this tracks  
This dataset lists licensed facilities in Missouri that provide fetal ultrasound services, including their contact details and service areas.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're pregnant and need an ultrasound, this data helps you find nearby providers, including whether they offer free services. For example, a Shelbyville resident could check if the Shelby County Health Department provides free ultrasounds before scheduling an appointment elsewhere. Small healthcare businesses can also use this to identify service gaps or potential referral partners in their area.  

## How to read this data  
**facility_name** — The provider's name (e.g., "SHELBY COUNTY HEALTH DEPT").  
**free_ultrasounds** — Whether the facility offers no-cost ultrasounds (values like "No" or potentially "Yes").  
**services_counties** — Which counties the facility serves (e.g., "Shelby").  
**days_and_hours_of_operations** — When the facility is open (e.g., "Monday,Tuesday,Wednesday,Thursday,Friday").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your county has a provider offering free ultrasounds by filtering the dataset for your county name in **services_counties** and "Yes" in **free_ultrasounds**.
