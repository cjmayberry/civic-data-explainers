## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all licensed adult day care (ADC) programs in Missouri, including their locations, operating hours, and capacities.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're helping an aging parent or relative find daytime care in Missouri, this data shows which facilities operate near their home and whether they offer specialized Alzheimer's care. A small business owner could use it to verify a competitor's operating hours when planning a new ADC location in Columbia or another Missouri city.  

## How to read this data  
**adcname** — The facility's name, like "CLOSE TO HOME ADULT DAY CARE."  
**adcaddresscenter** — Street address where care is provided, such as "2400 E BUSINESS LOOP 70."  
**maxcap** — Maximum participants allowed, shown as a number (e.g., 20).  
**alzunit** — "True" or "False" to indicate whether the facility has an Alzheimer's/dementia unit.  

## Try it yourself  
Search for facilities near your Missouri ZIP code using the `adczip` field to compare operating hours (`adcopen` and `adcclose`) for morning drop-off options.
