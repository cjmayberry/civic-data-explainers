## What this tracks  
This dataset records upcoming public meetings held by Missouri government bodies, including schedules, locations, and contact information.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you want to voice concerns about a local policy or track decisions affecting your neighborhood, this data tells you when and where to attend key meetings—like the Missouri Technology Corporation’s Investment Committee sessions. For small businesses, it reveals opportunities to bid on projects or advocate for industry changes. Knowing whether a meeting is virtual (e.g., "Via Teleconference") helps you plan how to participate.  

## How to read this data  
**meeting_body_name** — The organization hosting the meeting, like "Missouri Technology Corporation."  
**begin_date_time** — When the meeting starts, formatted as "2026-07-08T09:30:00.000" (July 8, 2026, at 9:30 AM).  
**building_name** — The physical or virtual location, such as "Via Teleconference."  
**contact_first_name** and **contact_last_name** — Who to reach for details, e.g., "Jenni Rabenau" at "(636) 686-0304."  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for meetings near you by checking the **latlng** field (e.g., latitude 37.9642529, longitude -91.8318334) against your area’s coordinates.
