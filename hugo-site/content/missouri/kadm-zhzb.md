## What this tracks  
This dataset counts workplace injury reports in Missouri that involve lower body parts like legs, knees, and feet.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a small business owner in Missouri, this data shows which lower-body injuries happen most often in workplaces like yours. A roofing contractor might see that ankle injuries are common and invest in better boots, while a warehouse manager noticing frequent knee injuries could prioritize anti-fatigue mats. Employees can check if their workplace has above-average injury rates for specific body parts before accepting physically demanding jobs.

## How to read this data  
**knee_s** — Number of knee injuries reported (sample value: 518 in one month).  
**ankle_s** — Ankle injury counts (sample: 253). Higher numbers mean greater risk.  
**foot** — Total foot injuries excluding toes (sample: 207). Compare this to toe-specific counts.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your industry has unusually high knee or ankle injuries by comparing your workplace's safety reports to these statewide monthly averages.
