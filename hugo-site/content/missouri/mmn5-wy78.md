## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all current beer, wine, and liquor solicitors licensed in Missouri, along with their business names, locations, and license types.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you own a bar, restaurant, or retail store in Missouri, this list helps you verify whether a salesperson pitching alcohol products is properly licensed. For example, before agreeing to stock a new craft beer from a distributor, you can check this dataset to confirm their license is active. It also helps residents report unlicensed alcohol sales by identifying legitimate solicitors in their zip code.  

## How to read this data  
**business_name** — The legal name of the licensed solicitor (e.g., "EGGHEAD TOO, LLC").  
**license_type** — The specific permit held, such as "Liquor Solicitor - Out-State" for distributors based outside Missouri.  
**zip_code** — The solicitor's operational zip code (e.g., "32174"), useful for finding local representatives.
