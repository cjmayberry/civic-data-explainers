## What this tracks  
This dataset lists licensed onsite soil evaluators in Missouri who assess soil conditions for septic systems and other land-use projects.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're building a home, adding a septic system, or developing land in rural Missouri, state law requires a certified soil evaluator's report. This list helps you find local professionals approved to conduct these assessments. Choosing an evaluator who serves your county ensures your project meets regulations and avoids costly delays. For small businesses like excavation contractors, verifying an evaluator's active license status prevents working with expired credentials.

## How to read this data  
**business** — The company name providing soil evaluation services (e.g., "McKee Soils and Farm").  
**counties_served** — Where the evaluator is authorized to work (e.g., "BOLLINGER, BUTLER, CAPE GIRARDEAU").  
**date_of_expiration** — When the evaluator's license expires (e.g., "2028-08-31").  
**business_phone** — Direct contact number for scheduling (e.g., "(417) 252-0966").  

## Try it yourself  
Search the list for evaluators serving your county (like "PHELPS" or "STONE") to find local professionals when planning a septic system installation.
