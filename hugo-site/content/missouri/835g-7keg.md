## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all Missouri state-approved offices where residents can get driver’s licenses and motor vehicle services.

## Why it matters to you  
If you need to renew your license or register a car, this data tells you the closest office, its hours, and even holiday closures—so you don’t waste a trip. For example, the Potosi office closes on 12/24/2026 (Christmas Eve), which you’d want to know before driving there. Small businesses like SEMO Tags LLC, which operate these offices, can also use this to verify their listed contact details are correct for customers.

## How to read this data  
**name** — The office’s public name, like "Potosi."  
**address1** — Street address (e.g., "301 E Jefferson Street, Unit 1").  
**daysopen** — Operating hours (e.g., "Monday - Friday 8:30-4:30").  
**additionaldaysclosed** — Specific closure dates (e.g., "3/6/2026, 11/27/2026").  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your city (like "Potosi") to find the nearest office’s phone number and hours before heading out.
