## What this tracks  
This dataset lists new liquor licenses issued in Missouri (primarily Jackson County) over the past three weeks.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a bar or restaurant in Kansas City, this shows where competitors are getting licensed nearby. Residents can check if new nightlife venues are opening on their block — useful for noise concerns or property value changes. Neighborhood associations can track liquor license density to advocate for zoning changes.

## How to read this data  
**licensee** — The legal business entity (e.g., "HULA HOOP LLC").  
**dbaname** — The public-facing business name (e.g., "HULA HOOP").  
**license_type** — Specific permissions (e.g., "Sunday by Drink" means alcohol sales permitted on Sundays).  
**street** — Exact location with suite numbers (e.g., "4247 WALNUT ST STE A").

Search the dataset for your ZIP code (like 64111) to see all new liquor-licensed businesses near you.
