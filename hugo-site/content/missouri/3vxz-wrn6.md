## What this tracks  
This dataset estimates the number of deaf and hard of hearing residents in Missouri based on 2010 census data.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a business with public-facing services in Missouri, this tells you roughly how many potential customers may need accommodations like sign language interpreters or assistive listening devices. For residents, seeing these numbers helps advocate for better accessibility in local schools, hospitals, and government offices. The data shows deaf and hard of hearing Missourians represent a significant population – about 10% of the state when combining both groups.

## How to read this data  
**est_deaf_1** — Estimated residents who are deaf (sample: 59,889, or ~1% of population).  
**est_hoh_9** — Estimated residents with some hearing difficulty (sample: 539,003, or ~9% of population).  
**est_deaf_hoh_10** — Combined total of both groups (sample: 598,893, or ~10% of population).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your Missouri county has local accessibility services by searching "[Your County] MO deaf services" – the statewide numbers suggest demand exists even in rural areas.
