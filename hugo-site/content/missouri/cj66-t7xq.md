## What this tracks  
This dataset records monthly unemployment claims across different industries in Missouri.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering a career change or starting a business in Missouri, this data shows which industries are experiencing the most job instability. For example, seeing high claims in retail trade might make you reconsider opening a brick-and-mortar store, while stable numbers in healthcare could confirm it as a reliable field. Small business owners can use this to anticipate labor market shifts when hiring.

## How to read this data  
**accomodation_food_services** — Shows claims from hotels and restaurants (sample: 2,458 claims in June 2026).  
**health_care_social_assistance** — Tracks claims in hospitals and care facilities (sample: 2,608 claims).  
**construction** — Indicates layoffs in building trades (sample: 837 claims).  
**manufacturing** — Reflects factory job losses (sample: 1,417 claims).
