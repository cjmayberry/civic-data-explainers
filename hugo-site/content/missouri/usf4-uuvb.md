## What this tracks  
This dataset lists certified stretcher van services in Missouri that provide non-emergency medical transportation.

## Why it matters to you  
If you or a family member needs wheelchair-accessible transport to medical appointments, this data helps you find licensed providers near you. Knowing a service's certification number (like 8019 for Advanced Mobility Solutions) lets you verify their legitimacy with the state before booking. For small medical transport businesses, checking nearby competitors' locations (like Dongola, IL) helps identify service gaps in rural areas like Union County.

## How to read this data  
**service_name** — The company name, like "Advanced Mobility Solutions, Inc."  
**service_street_1** — Physical address (e.g., "6615 US Hwy 51 South") where vehicles are based.  
**service_phone** — Direct contact number (format: 618-751-1428) for scheduling rides.  
**service_user_position** — Role of the listed contact (e.g., "EMS Administrator").  

## Try it yourself  
Call 618-751-1428 to ask Advanced Mobility Solutions about their rates for trips from your Missouri address to the nearest dialysis center.
