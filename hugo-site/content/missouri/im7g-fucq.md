## What this tracks  
This dataset maps which Missouri zip codes belong to which counties and cities.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're mailing tax documents or applying for a business license in Missouri, this data confirms whether your zip code falls under Springfield city limits or Greene County jurisdiction. A small business owner in 65899 could use this to verify they're filing paperwork with the correct municipal office instead of the county clerk. Parents registering for school districts can cross-check whether their zip code aligns with their expected city boundaries.  

## How to read this data  
**county** — The county where the zip code is located, like "Greene".  
**zip_code** — The 5-digit postal code, such as "65899".  
**city** — The primary municipality for that zip code, for example "Springfield".  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your own zip code to confirm which city and county handles your local services like trash collection or property tax assessments.
