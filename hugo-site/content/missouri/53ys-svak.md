## What this tracks  
This dataset records the names, positions, agencies, and gross pay of Missouri state employees in 2020.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering a job with the state, this data shows what similar roles actually pay — not just the posted salary range. A small business owner bidding on state contracts could check whether the agency they're working with has budget for new hires, which might signal upcoming projects. Missouri residents can see how tax dollars fund positions across agencies like Agriculture or Corrections.  

## How to read this data  
**agency_name** — Which state department the employee works for, like "AGRICULTURE" or "TRANSPORTATION".  
**position_title** — The employee's job, such as "ACCOUNTANT II" or "CORRECTIONAL OFFICER I".  
**ytd_gross_pay** — The employee's total earnings before deductions in 2020, like 23769.77 (dollar amount).  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your own name if you worked for Missouri in 2020, or look up a job title you're considering to compare pay across agencies.
