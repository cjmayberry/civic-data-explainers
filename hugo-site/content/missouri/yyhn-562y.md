## What this tracks  
This dataset records active alcohol licenses for businesses involved in manufacturing, shipping, or selling alcohol in Missouri.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a bar or liquor store in Missouri, this data tells you how many existing alcohol sellers operate near your planned location. For residents, checking **primary_type** reveals whether a business is a brewery, distributor, or retailer—helpful when reporting suspicious sales near schools. The **county** field flags out-of-state sellers, which matters if you're a Missouri retailer tracking competition.

## How to read this data  
**dbaname** — The "doing business as" name (e.g., "SCHAEFER-REICHART SELECTIONS INC."), which may differ from the legal licensee name.  
**primary_type** — The license category (e.g., "22% Solicitor - Out-State"), showing if the business is a manufacturer, retailer, or out-of-state entity.  
**street + city** — The physical location (e.g., "4500 SALISBURY RD STE 530, JACKSONVILLE"), useful for verifying a seller's address.
