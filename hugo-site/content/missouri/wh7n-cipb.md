## What this tracks  
This dataset lists retail businesses registered with the Buy Missouri program, which promotes locally owned businesses in the state.  

## Why it matters to you  
When shopping for home goods, gifts, or specialty items, this data helps you find and support Missouri-owned businesses instead of national chains. For small business owners, it reveals potential local partners or competitors in your county. A Cape Girardeau resident could use this to discover nearby shops like Double D Supply before driving across town.  

## How to read this data  
**business_name** — The registered name of the business (e.g., "Double D Supply").  
**city**/**county** — Where the business operates (e.g., "Cape Girardeau" city and county).  
**website** — Some listings include a direct link to the business (e.g., `{"url": "https://smokinbrothers.com"}`).  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your ZIP code (like 63701) to find participating businesses within 10 miles of your home.
