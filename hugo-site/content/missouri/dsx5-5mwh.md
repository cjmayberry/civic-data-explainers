## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Missouri's Family Support Division Resource Centers, including their locations, hours, and services offered.

## Why it matters to you  
If your family needs food assistance (SNAP), health care coverage, or child support services, this data tells you exactly where to go in Missouri and when they're open. Knowing the **hours_of_operation** prevents wasted trips when centers are closed for lunch or have special Wednesday hours. The **drop_box_information** field shows whether you can submit paperwork after hours.

## How to read this data  
**resource_center** — The official name of the assistance office (e.g., "Osceola Resource Center").  
**hours_of_operation** — Specific open times (e.g., "8 am to 5 pm M, T, TH, F", with lunch closures).  
**location_1** — Physical address with GPS coordinates (e.g., 230 Business Hwy 13, Osceola, MO 64776).  
**phone_number** — Direct contact line (e.g., (855) 373-4636).  

## Try it yourself  
Find your nearest center by searching the dataset for your county name in the **resource_center** field, then check its **hours_of_operation** before visiting.
