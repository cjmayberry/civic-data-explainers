## What this tracks  
This dataset lists farmers' markets and local farm vendors across Missouri, including their locations, contact details, and product offerings.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're looking for fresh, locally grown produce or meat in Missouri, this data helps you find nearby farmers' markets and farm-direct sellers. For example, a family in Greentop could use this to compare beef suppliers like Ertel Cattle Company before stocking their freezer. Small restaurants or grocers can also source seasonal ingredients directly from nearby farms instead of relying on distant distributors.

## How to read this data  
**business_name** — The vendor's name, like "Ertel Cattle Company."  
**address1** + **city** — The physical location (e.g., "26694 Anchor Way, Greentop").  
**company_description** — Details about the farm’s products (e.g., "premium, locally raised beef").  
**website** — A link to the vendor’s site (e.g., http://www.ertelcattle.com).  

## Try it yourself  
Search for vendors near your ZIP code using the [Missouri Farmers' Market Directory](https://agriculture.mo.gov/abd/farmersmarket/) to plan your next grocery trip or bulk meat purchase.
