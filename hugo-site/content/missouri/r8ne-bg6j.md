## What this tracks  
This dataset counts workplace injuries to upper extremities (arms, hands, shoulders) reported to Missouri each month.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a small business owner in Missouri, this data shows which body parts get injured most often in your industry. A restaurant owner seeing high finger_s injuries might invest in cut-resistant gloves. Construction companies with frequent shoulder_s reports could prioritize ergonomic training. Workers can check if their injury type is common before filing claims.  

## How to read this data  
**finger_s** — Number of finger injuries reported (e.g., 739 in one month).  
**shoulder_s** — Shoulder injuries (sample: 480), useful for physical labor businesses.  
**wrist_s_hand_s** — Combined wrist and hand injuries (sample: 81), indicating repetitive strain risks.

Search your industry's injury reports by comparing recent months' finger_s and hand_s counts to assess equipment or training needs.
