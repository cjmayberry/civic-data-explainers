## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all Medicare-certified End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) dialysis facilities in Missouri.

## Why it matters to you  
If you or a family member needs regular dialysis treatment, this data helps you find nearby facilities and compare options in St. Louis and across Missouri. Knowing the administrator's contact information lets you ask specific questions about treatment capacity or insurance acceptance before committing to a facility. For small healthcare businesses, it reveals where dialysis services are concentrated or underserved.

## How to read this data  
**facility_name** — The official name of the dialysis center (e.g., "RTC - Hampton Avenue Dialysis").  
**address** — Street location (e.g., "1425 Hampton Avenue") with city and ZIP code.  
**phone** — Direct contact number (e.g., "(314) 781-4022") for scheduling or questions.  
**administrator_full_name** — Who manages the facility (e.g., "Monica Atkins"), useful for personalized inquiries.

Search the dataset for facilities near your ZIP code to compare travel times from your home or workplace.
