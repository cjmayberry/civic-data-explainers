## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Community Action Agencies in Missouri that provide Skill Up job training and support services.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're looking to gain new job skills or need career counseling in rural Missouri, this data helps you find the nearest assistance center without calling multiple county offices. For example, a Paris, MO resident could confirm their local NECAC office offers workforce training before making a 30-mile trip to a different county. Small businesses can also refer employees to these verified training resources when upskilling staff.

## How to read this data  
**location** — The name of the service center, like "Monroe County Service Center (NECAC)".  
**phone_1** — Direct contact number for the center, such as "660-327-4110".  
**location_1** — Physical address with coordinates, formatted as:  
`{"address": "314 North Washington", "city": "Paris", "state": "MO", "zip": "65275"}`  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your nearest center offers weekend classes by calling the **phone_1** number listed for locations in your county.
