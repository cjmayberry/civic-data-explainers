## What this tracks  
This dataset lists upcoming COVID-19 vaccination events in Missouri, including locations, schedules, and vaccine types available.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you or a family member still needs a COVID-19 vaccine or booster, this data shows where to get one near Bates County without calling multiple clinics. It tells you whether a Butler location offers walk-in appointments (like Compass Health Network) or requires advance registration, and whether they have the specific vaccine type you prefer.  

## How to read this data  
**location** — The facility hosting the event, like "Compass Health Network-Butler".  
**address_1** — Street address for the event, such as "805 N Orange Street" in Butler.  
**days** — When the clinic operates, e.g., "Monday - Friday".  
**vaccine_types** — Which vaccines are offered (sample: "Moderna").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if there’s a walk-in clinic within 20 miles of Butler by filtering for "registration_type: Walk-In" and your county name.
