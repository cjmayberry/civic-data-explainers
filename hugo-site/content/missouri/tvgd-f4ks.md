## What this tracks  
This dataset counts workplace head injuries reported to Missouri by specific body part affected.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering jobs in construction, manufacturing, or other high-risk fields in Missouri, this shows which head injuries happen most often. A parent might check eye injury rates before letting a teen work at a metal shop. Employers can compare their safety records to statewide trends for specific injury types like skull fractures or brain trauma.

## How to read this data  
**eye_s** — Total eye injuries per month (e.g., 225 in one sample month).  
**head_soft_tissue** — Cuts/scrapes to the scalp (165 cases).  
**skull** — Fractures or other bone injuries (59 cases).  
**brain** — Serious traumatic brain injuries (23 cases).
