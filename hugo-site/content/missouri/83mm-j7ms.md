## What this tracks  
This dataset lists current job openings across Missouri state government agencies, including position details and application information.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're job hunting in Missouri, this data shows actual vacancies with state benefits — like a $2,750/month Publications Specialist role in Jefferson City. Small businesses can check for state contract opportunities by seeing which agencies are hiring for specific skills. Teachers transitioning to administrative work might find relevant openings at the Department of Elementary Education.  

## How to read this data  
**title** — The official job name (e.g., "Publications Specialist I").  
**salarystart** — Base pay (e.g., "$2,750.00" monthly).  
**organization** — Which state agency is hiring (e.g., "House of Representatives").  
**joburl** — Direct link to apply (e.g., `https://mocareers.mo.gov/Details?id=20452`).  

## Try it yourself  
Search for jobs near your ZIP code by filtering the `workzip` field to your area (like 65101 for Jefferson City). Check the `jobtype` field if you need "Full-Time" or "Part-Time" work.
