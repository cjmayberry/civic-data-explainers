## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the locations and details of parking lots in Missouri's Capitol Complex.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're visiting Jefferson City for government business or tours, this tells you exactly where to park near the State Capitol. Knowing which lots are closest to your destination (like the Supreme Court or Governor's Office) saves time circling the complex. Business owners hosting clients at state offices can share precise parking instructions.

## How to read this data  
**name** — Identifies the parking lot (e.g., "Lot #1A").  
**the_geom** — Geographic coordinates of the lot (example: -92.172287 longitude, 38.579819 latitude).  
**descriptio** — Contains a photo of the lot (e.g., an image link showing Lot 1A's layout).
