## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Excel Centers in Missouri, which are adult education centers offering high school diploma programs.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're an adult in Missouri needing to finish your high school education, this data helps you find the nearest Excel Center location and contact information. Knowing these centers exist could help you decide to enroll in a flexible program that fits your work schedule rather than assuming traditional night school is your only option. For small businesses, this data shows where potential employees might be completing their education nearby.

## How to read this data  
**location** — The name of the Excel Center, like "Poplar Bluff Excel Center".  
**address** — The street address where the center is located, such as "2233 N. Westwood Blvd".  
**phone** — The direct contact number for the center, like "573-686-6004".  
**latlong** — Geographic coordinates you can use in mapping apps, formatted as {'type': 'Point', 'coordinates': [-90.425383, 36.780559]}.
