## What this tracks  
This dataset lists clinics in Missouri that participate in the Vaccines for Children (VFC) program, which provides free vaccines to eligible kids.  

## Why it matters to you  
If your child qualifies for the VFC program (based on Medicaid eligibility, being uninsured, or other criteria), this data helps you find nearby clinics offering free vaccines instead of paying out-of-pocket elsewhere. For small healthcare providers, it shows where to refer patients who need low-cost immunizations. In Missouri, where rural areas often have fewer options, knowing these locations can save families long drives to distant health departments.  

## How to read this data  
**primary_name** — The clinic’s name (e.g., "BOWLING GREEN MEDICAL GROUP").  
**street** and **city** — The physical location (e.g., "905 HIGHWAY 161, BOWLING GREEN").  
**provider_phone** — Contact number to confirm vaccine availability (e.g., "573-324-3333").  

## Try it yourself  
Search this dataset for clinics near your ZIP code to compare locations and call ahead for vaccine availability if your child qualifies for VFC.
