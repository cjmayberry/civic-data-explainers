## What this tracks  
This dataset lists providers in Missouri who offer Show-Me Healthy Women (SMHW) services, including cancer screenings and preventive health care.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a Missouri woman without insurance or with limited coverage, this data helps you find free breast and cervical cancer screenings near you. A Kirksville resident could see that Northeast Missouri Health Council is just 10 minutes away at 1416 Crown Drive instead of assuming they'd need to travel to Columbia. Small business owners offering health benefits can reference nearby SMHW providers when explaining supplemental care options to employees.

## How to read this data  
**provider_name** — Organization offering services, like "Northeast Missouri Health Council".  
**address** — Physical location with street address, city, and ZIP (e.g., "1416 Crown Drive, Kirksville, MO 63501").  
**smhw_provider** — Confirms participation in the program (value will always be "Yes" in this dataset).  

## Try it yourself  
Call (660) 627-5757 to ask Northeast Missouri Health Council about free screenings if you live near Kirksville – or find your closest provider by checking the city/zip fields for your area.
