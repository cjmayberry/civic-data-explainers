## What this tracks  
This dataset lists licensed ground ambulance services operating in Missouri, including their contact information and locations.

## Why it matters to you  
When choosing emergency medical transport for a family member or arranging workplace medical coverage, this data helps you verify licensed providers near your home or business in Missouri. Knowing which ambulance companies serve your specific county (like St. Louis County) ensures faster response times during emergencies. Small healthcare businesses can also use this to identify potential partners for patient transport services.

## How to read this data  
**service_name** — The legal name of the ambulance company (e.g., "Abbott Ambulance Inc").  
**service_street_1** — Physical address where the service operates (e.g., "2500 ABBOTT PLACE").  
**service_county** — Which county the ambulance primarily serves (e.g., "St. Louis").  
**service_phone** — Direct contact number for the service (e.g., "314-768-1000").  

## Try it yourself  
Look up ambulance services in your county by filtering the dataset for your county name in the **service_county** field, then compare their locations and contact information.
