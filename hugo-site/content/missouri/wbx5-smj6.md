## What this tracks  
This dataset defines the boundaries of the Kansas City, MO-KS Metropolitan Statistical Area as designated by federal agencies for statistical reporting.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing whether your business or home falls within this metro area helps determine eligibility for certain federal grants, small business loans, or workforce development programs. For example, a Kansas City restaurant owner could check if they qualify for urban revitalization tax credits by confirming their location is within these official boundaries.

## How to read this data  
**namelsad** — The full legal name of the statistical area: "Kansas City, MO-KS Metropolitan Statistical Area".  
**aland** — Total land area in square meters (sample: 20,269,414,580 m²).  
**intptlat/intptlon** — The latitude (+38.9086649) and longitude (-94.5086766) of the area's central point.
