## What this tracks  
This dataset records vehicles with active child support liens filed by the state of Missouri.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying a used car in Missouri, this data tells you whether the vehicle has an unpaid child support lien that could prevent you from getting clear title. Checking this list before purchase could save you from legal complications or unexpected costs. Business owners who deal in used vehicles should check this list during inventory acquisition to avoid inheriting someone else's debt.

## How to read this data  
**year** — The vehicle's model year (e.g., 2021).  
**make** — The manufacturer (e.g., "HMDE").  
**vin_number** — The unique vehicle identification number (e.g., "DRXMVB000367589MO").  
**child_support_lien_date** — When the lien was filed (e.g., "2026-06-18").

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the city or state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
