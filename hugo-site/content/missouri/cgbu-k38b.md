## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all active law enforcement agencies in Missouri, including sheriffs' offices, municipal police departments, university police, and court marshals.

## Why it matters to you  
If you need to report a crime or request police assistance, this data helps you identify the correct agency for your location in Missouri. For example, if you live near a university campus, you'll see whether campus police or city police handle calls in your area. Business owners can use this to verify which agency responds to their address before filing a security-related complaint.

## How to read this data  
**name** — The official name of the agency, like "13th Judicial Court Marshal's Office."  
**ceo_title** + **ceo_name** — Who leads the agency, such as "Marshal Michael Franks."  
**street_address** + **city** — Physical location, like "705 East Walnut, Columbia."  
**agency_type** — Whether it's a Sheriff, Municipal, Court, or University agency.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your local law enforcement agency by searching the dataset for your city or county name, then save their non-emergency phone number (**voice_number**) for future reference.
