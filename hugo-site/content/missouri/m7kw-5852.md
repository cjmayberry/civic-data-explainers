## What this tracks  
This dataset records upcoming COVID-19 vaccination events in Missouri, including location, time, vaccine types offered, and registration details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you or a family member still needs a COVID-19 vaccine or booster, this data helps you find nearby options without calling multiple clinics. For Webster County residents, it shows whether the Marshfield health unit offers your preferred vaccine type (like Johnson & Johnson) on days you're available. Small businesses can check if employees have convenient after-work vaccination times nearby.

## How to read this data  
**date** — When the event occurs, shown as 2021-07-26 for July 26, 2021.  
**start_time/end_time** — Clinic hours, like 15:00:00 (3 PM) to 16:00:00 (4 PM).  
**type** — Vaccines offered, such as "Moderna and Johnson & Johnson".  
**address_1** — Street address of the event, like "233 E. Washington Street" in Marshfield.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if there's a vaccine event this week at the Webster County Health Unit by matching the "county" field to your location. Call 417-859-2532 to confirm walk-in availability.
