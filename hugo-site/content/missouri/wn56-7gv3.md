## What this tracks  
This dataset lists contact information, locations, and services provided by Community Action Agencies in Missouri.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're struggling to pay utility bills in Clay County, this data tells you exactly where to get energy assistance before your power gets shut off. Kansas City residents can check if their neighborhood food pantry offers senior-specific programs like health fairs. Small landlords in Platte County can see if weatherization services are available to improve their rental properties' energy efficiency.

## How to read this data  
**community_action_agency** — The organization name, like "Community Action Agency of Greater Kansas City (CAAGKC)".  
**serving_counties** — Which counties the agency helps, such as "Clay, Jackson, Platte".  
**services_provided** — Specific assistance programs offered, like "Energy Assistance, Family Support, Food Pantry".  
**location_1** — Physical address with coordinates, including street ("6323 Manchester Avenue") and city ("Kansas City").
