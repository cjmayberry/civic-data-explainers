## What this tracks  
This dataset records active alcohol licenses issued in Missouri counties, including business details and license expiration dates.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a bar or restaurant in Jackson County, this tells you whether nearby competitors have Sunday liquor licenses before signing a lease. Event planners can verify if a venue's alcohol license is active before booking. Neighbors concerned about new liquor establishments can check if a problematic business's license is nearing expiration.

## How to read this data  
**license_type** — The specific alcohol permission (e.g., "Sunday by Drink" allows Sunday alcohol sales).  
**current_status** — Shows if the license is "Active" or expired/inactive.  
**expiration_date** — When the license renewals are due (sample format: 06/30/2027).  
**dba_name** — The public-facing business name (e.g., "HULA HOOP" instead of the legal entity name).
