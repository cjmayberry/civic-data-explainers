## What this tracks  
This dataset lists organizations in Missouri that distribute free Baby Boxes (safe sleep kits for newborns) and their contact information.  

## Why it matters to you  
Expecting parents in Missouri can use this data to find local Baby Box providers before their due date. These boxes typically include a safe sleep surface, diapers, and newborn essentials. Knowing where to get one could save families $100+ on starter supplies and provide crucial safe sleep education to prevent SIDS.  

## How to read this data  
**service_name** — The organization distributing boxes, like "Big River Ambulance District".  
**service_street_1** — Their physical address (e.g., "6327 LORENS LN").  
**service_phone** — Direct contact number (e.g., "636-274-3964") to ask about eligibility requirements.  

## Try it yourself  
Call the provider nearest to your Missouri address (like Big River Ambulance at 636-274-3964) to ask how to claim your free Baby Box before delivery.
