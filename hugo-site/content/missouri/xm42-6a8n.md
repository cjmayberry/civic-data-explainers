## What this tracks  
This dataset records the monthly number of unemployment claims in Missouri, broken down by Hispanic/Latino and non-Hispanic/Latino ethnicity.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a small business owner in Missouri, this data helps you understand which demographic groups are disproportionately affected by job losses in your area. A community organization advocating for workforce development could use these trends to target retraining programs where they're needed most. Seeing spikes in claims for a specific group may also prompt local policymakers to investigate potential discrimination in layoffs.

## How to read this data  
**date** — The month being reported, formatted as year-month-day (e.g., 2011-08-01 shows August 2011 data).  
**hispanic_or_latino** — The number of unemployment claims filed by people identifying as Hispanic/Latino (e.g., 942 claims in the sample).  
**not_hispanic_or_latino** — The number of claims filed by people not identifying as Hispanic/Latino (e.g., 31,311 claims in the sample).  

## Try it yourself  
Compare the most recent month's Hispanic/Latino claim percentage to the overall Missouri population percentage (about 4.9% as of 2022) to spot potential disparities.
