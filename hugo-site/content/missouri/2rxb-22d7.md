## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Missouri's child support offices, including their locations, contact details, and hours of operation.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're navigating child support payments or custody arrangements in Missouri, this data helps you quickly find your nearest office without calling a general helpline. Knowing the exact hours (like "8 am to 5 pm M-F") prevents wasted trips, and the address details confirm whether you need to visit in person or can mail documents to the Jefferson City PO Box. For parents with tight schedules, it clarifies if a lunchtime visit is possible.

## How to read this data  
**location_1** — The office's physical address (e.g., "471 Siemers Drive, Cape Girardeau, MO 63701") with latitude/longitude for mapping.  
**hours_of_operation** — When staff are available (e.g., "8 am to 5 pm M-F").  
**phone** — Direct contact number for questions (e.g., "866-313-9960").

Search the dataset for your city (like "Cape Girardeau") to find your closest office's address and hours before your next visit.
