## What this tracks  
This dataset lists licensed inspectors and evaluators who can assess onsite wastewater treatment systems (septic systems) in Missouri.

## Why it matters to you  
If your home or business relies on a septic system, Missouri law requires inspections by licensed professionals. This list helps you find qualified inspectors in your county when buying a property, selling your home, or maintaining your system. Knowing which counties each inspector serves prevents wasted calls to professionals who don't operate in your area.

## How to read this data  
**business** — The company name providing inspection services (e.g., "Accurate Septic & Well Inspections").  
**counties_served** — Where the inspector is authorized to work (e.g., "FRANKLIN,JEFFERSON,LINCOLN").  
**date_of_expiration** — When the inspector's license expires (e.g., "2028-08-31"), so you can verify current credentials.  
**business_phone** — Direct contact number for scheduling (e.g., "636-262-1303").  

## Try it yourself  
Find inspectors serving your county by searching the dataset for your county name (like "STLOUIS") in the "counties_served" field.
