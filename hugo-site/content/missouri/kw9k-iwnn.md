## What this tracks  
This dataset records the real-time GPS locations of three bison living in Missouri State Park.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a hike or picnic in the park, this data tells you whether bison are near your planned route—helping you avoid unexpected encounters. For photographers or wildlife enthusiasts, it shows where to find these animals without disturbing their natural movements. Parents can check if bison are grazing near playgrounds or picnic areas before visiting with children.

## How to read this data  
**name** — Identifies individual bison like "Miller Bison 3" from the park's small herd.  
**latlon** — Shows exact coordinates like {'latitude': '37.525435', 'longitude': '-94.570591'} for mapping their position.  
**moving** — Indicates if the bison is currently active (Y/N), with "N" meaning they're stationary.  
**gpsfixdatetime** — Timestamp like "2015-10-25T03:22:00.000" showing when the location was last updated.

## Try it yourself  
Check the current location of all three bison on a park map before your next visit to plan a safe viewing distance.
