## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all fire departments registered with Missouri's Division of Fire Safety, including their locations and contact details.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying a home in Missouri, this data tells you which fire department serves that address and how to reach them in an emergency. Business owners can verify response times by checking how far the nearest station is from their property. Renters can confirm their building's fire protection coverage before signing a lease.  

## How to read this data  
**departmentname** — The official name of the fire department, like "Saline Valley Fire Protection District."  
**addressphysical** — The street address where the fire station is located, such as "1771 SPRINGDALE BLVD."  
**emergencyphone** — The direct line to report fires, shown as a 10-digit number like 6363439300.  
**disasterregion** — Which emergency coordination zone the department operates in (e.g., "C" for Central Missouri).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your nearest fire station by comparing your address to the **addressphysical** fields in the dataset, then save the **emergencyphone** in your contacts.
