## What this tracks  
This dataset counts how many Missouri residents are currently receiving unemployment benefits, broken down by payment type.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're job hunting in Missouri, this data shows whether unemployment claims are rising or falling in your area — a strong indicator of how hard it may be to find work. Small business owners can use the trend to anticipate labor market changes when hiring. The "regular" vs "emergency" benefit breakdown reveals whether layoffs are routine or driven by economic crises.

## How to read this data  
**date** — The month being reported (e.g., 2013-01-01 shows January 2013 data).  
**regular** — Standard unemployment claims (sample value: 65,742 recipients).  
**euc** — Emergency Unemployment Compensation during recessions (sample: 29,532 recipients).  
**total** — All beneficiaries combined (sample: 95,274 people).  

## Try it yourself  
Compare the most recent "total" figure to older dates — if numbers are rising sharply in 2024, consider broadening your job search to neighboring counties.
