## What this tracks  
This dataset records water quality test results and open/closed status for public beaches in Missouri state parks.

## Why it matters to you  
Before driving to a beach at Cuivre River State Park or other Missouri locations, this data tells you whether the water is safe for swimming that day. If **open_closed** shows "Closed," high bacteria levels could make you or your kids sick. Checking the **geomean** score helps you compare water cleanliness across beaches—lower numbers mean cleaner water.

## How to read this data  
**park_name** — Which state park and specific beach was tested (e.g., "Cuivre River SP - Lake Lincoln Beach").  
**sample_taken** — When the water was last tested (e.g., "2026-08-03").  
**geomean** — Average bacteria level (e.g., 7.9); under 8 is generally safe.  
**open_closed** — Current swimming status (e.g., "Open" or "Closed").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your favorite Missouri state park beach is open for swimming today by searching [the dataset](https://data.mo.gov/) for the park name.
