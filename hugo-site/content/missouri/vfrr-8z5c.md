## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all licensed financial institutions operating in Missouri, including banks and credit unions.  

## Why it matters to you  
When choosing where to open a business checking account in Missouri, this data lets you verify a bank’s official state license before depositing funds. It also helps small business owners compare local institutions by size (assets) when seeking loans—a $33 million-asset community bank like Canton State Bank may offer more personalized service than larger chains.  

## How to read this data  
**name** — The official institution name, like "Canton State Bank".  
**assets** — Total holdings in thousands of dollars (e.g., "33038" = $33 million).  
**type** — Institution category: "CB" for commercial banks, "CU" for credit unions.  
**ceo** — Leadership details, such as "Carolyn Schaller, Pres/CEO".  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for banks in your ZIP code to find locally headquartered options with Missouri-based decision-makers.
