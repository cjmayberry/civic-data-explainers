## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Missouri Career Centers that provide job search assistance and employer hiring services.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're looking for work in Missouri, these centers offer free resume help, interview coaching, and job listings you won't find online. For business owners, they can screen applicants and host recruitment events. Knowing the Sedalia Job Center exists (with staff reachable at 660-530-5627) means you don't have to navigate unemployment paperwork alone.

## How to read this data  
**career_center_name** — Official name of the assistance center, like "Sedalia Job Center".  
**address1** + **city** — Physical location (e.g., "515 S. Kentucky, Sedalia").  
**phone**/ **email** — Direct contacts (e.g., "660-530-5627", "sedalia@dhewd.mo.gov") for scheduling appointments.  

## Try it yourself  
Call the Sedalia Job Center at 660-530-5627 to ask about their next resume workshop — walk-ins are often welcome.
