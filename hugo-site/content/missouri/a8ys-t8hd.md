## What this tracks  
This dataset lists public access points along the Missouri River Water Trail, including amenities and ownership details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a river trip in Missouri, this data helps you find safe launch points and know what facilities to expect. It tells you whether an area offers camping (like primitive sites at Deroin Bend) or requires portaging your boat. Knowing who manages each site (like the Missouri Department of Conservation) helps you check for seasonal closures or special rules before loading your gear.

## How to read this data  
**river_mile** — The exact mile marker along the Missouri River (e.g., 520.50 means 520.5 miles from the river's start).  
**camping** — Available overnight options (e.g., "Primitive" means basic campsites without facilities).  
**rvr_access** — How you reach the water (e.g., "Portage" means carrying boats from parking to river).  
**location** — GPS coordinates for navigation (e.g., latitude 40.280047, longitude -95.550208 for Deroin Bend).

## Try it yourself  
Check if there's a river access point with camping near your next planned float trip by searching [the Missouri Department of Conservation's atlas](http://mdc4.mdc.mo.gov/applications/moatlas/) for sites along your route.
