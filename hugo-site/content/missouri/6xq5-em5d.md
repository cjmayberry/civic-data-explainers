## What this tracks  
This dataset lists air ambulance services operating in Missouri, including their contact details and locations.

## Why it matters to you  
If you live in rural Missouri or frequently travel through areas with limited hospital access, this data helps you identify nearby emergency air transport options before an urgent need arises. Knowing which services operate in your county could inform decisions about emergency preparedness plans or medical insurance coverage that includes air ambulance benefits. For small healthcare providers, it reveals potential partners for patient transfers.

## How to read this data  
**service_name** — The company name (e.g., "Air Evac EMS Inc").  
**service_city** and **service_county** — Where the service is based (e.g., "O'Fallon" in "St. Charles" County).  
**service_phone** — Direct contact number (e.g., "636-695-5432") for inquiries or emergencies.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your Missouri county has an air ambulance service by filtering the dataset for your county name in the **service_county** field.
