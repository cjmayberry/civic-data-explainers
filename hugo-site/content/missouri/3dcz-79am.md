## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all Medicare-certified home health providers licensed to operate in Missouri.

## Why it matters to you  
When a family member needs in-home nursing care or therapy after hospitalization, this data helps you compare nearby providers by location and license status. Knowing which agencies serve your county prevents wasted calls to providers who can't assist you. Missouri residents can verify if a provider's license is current before arranging care.

## How to read this data  
**facname** — The agency's name (e.g., "Adoration Home Health").  
**servicearea_county** — Where the provider is authorized to serve patients (e.g., "DUNKLIN" means they cover Dunklin County).  
**licyrexpires** — The license expiration date (e.g., "2026-11-30" shows this provider's approval runs through November 2026).  
**ownership4profit** — A checkmark (✓) indicates a for-profit agency, while its absence means nonprofit.  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for providers in your county (like "Scott") to find home health agencies near your address.
