## What this tracks  
This dataset lists job centers in Missouri that provide employment resources and services to residents.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're looking for work or career training in the St. Louis area, this data helps you find nearby assistance without searching multiple websites. Knowing these locations could save you hours of phone calls when you need resume help, job listings, or interview coaching. The addresses and phone numbers are verified and kept current by the city.

## How to read this data  
**job_center** — Name of the organization providing services, like "ARCHS/Better Family Life - St. Ann".  
**phone_1** — Direct contact number for inquiries, formatted as "314-615-6050".  
**location** — Physical address with GPS coordinates and full street details: "715 NW. Plaza Dr., St. Ann, MO 63074".  

## Try it yourself  
Call the job center nearest your ZIP code (found in the location field) to ask about their free job-search workshops this month.
