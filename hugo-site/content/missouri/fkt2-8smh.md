## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all currently licensed beer, wine, and liquor wholesalers in Missouri, including their locations and license types.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own a bar, restaurant, or retail store in Missouri, this list helps you find legal alcohol suppliers near you. Knowing which wholesalers operate in your zip code lets you compare delivery times and pricing. For event planners, it shows which companies can legally provide bulk alcohol for weddings or festivals. The daily updates mean you’re seeing the most current licensing status.

## How to read this data  
**business_name** — The legal name of the wholesaler (e.g., SNI VALLEY VINEYARDS & WINERY LLC).  
**license_type** — The wholesaler’s authorization level (e.g., "22% Wholesale Solicitor" for mid-strength wine/spirits).  
**zip_code** — Where the business operates (e.g., 64067 for Lexington).
