## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Missouri-certified professionals who conduct home energy audits to assess a property's energy efficiency.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a homeowner in Missouri considering insulation upgrades, new windows, or HVAC replacements, hiring a certified auditor ensures you qualify for state rebates and federal tax credits. Knowing which auditors serve your county helps you avoid last-minute searches when scheduling pre-weatherization inspections. Businesses offering energy services can also use this to verify competitor credentials or find subcontractors.  

## How to read this data  
**mocert_number** — The auditor's state license ID (e.g., "2015-BR-0014-26").  
**expiration_date** — When the certification expires (e.g., "2026-10-01"). Always check this before hiring.  
**county** — Where the auditor operates (e.g., "Boone"). Filter by your county to find local providers.  
**location** — GPS coordinates of the auditor's business address (e.g., latitude "38.96548").  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for auditors in your ZIP code by filtering the "zip_code" field to find professionals near you.
