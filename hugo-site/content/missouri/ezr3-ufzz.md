## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all Missouri businesses that have renewed their alcohol licenses for the upcoming license year beginning July 1st.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a bar or restaurant in Missouri, this data tells you which competitors in your county have already secured their alcohol licenses for the year. For residents, checking this list confirms whether a favorite local spot has renewed its license to serve alcohol — useful when planning events or nights out.  

## How to read this data  
**license_type** — The category of alcohol service permitted, like "Retail by Drink - Resort" (sample value) or other classifications.  
**dba_name** — The "Doing Business As" name displayed to customers, which may differ from the legal business name (e.g., "POPIS" instead of "POPI'S LLC").  
**renewal_date** — When the license was renewed (format: MM/DD/YYYY), helping identify recently approved venues (sample: "04/14/2026").  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your Missouri city or ZIP code to see which bars, restaurants, and stores near you are licensed to serve alcohol this year.
