## What this tracks  
A statistical count of on-street parking spaces in Lisbon — how many spaces each parish (**freguesia**) has, split by **tipologia** (type of space: ZEDL limited-duration, ZAAC restricted-access, loading bays, EV charging spaces, and more).

## Why it matters to you  
Numbers like this are how residents and councils argue about parking policy. If your parish shows few EV-charging spaces (Alvalade: 34 in the sample) or few loading bays (Alvalade: 153), you have a concrete figure for asking for more — or for deciding where to look for a resident permit. It's the aggregate view that the boundary datasets (zones, areas) break down into individual rules.

## How to read this data  
**freguesia** — the parish (e.g. "Alvalade").  \n**tipologia** — the type of space (e.g. "Carga Veículos Eléctricos" = EV charging, "Cargas e Descargas" = loading/unloading).  \n**lugaresQTD** — the number of spaces of that type in that parish (e.g. 34 EV spaces in Alvalade).

## Try it yourself  
Open the [parking statistics in EMEL's data portal](https://dados.emel.pt/dataset/statisticsplacesbytypologyandparish) and compare your parish's **lugaresQTD** across **tipologia** types. A parish with many loading bays but few EV spaces tells you where the policy attention is — and isn't.
