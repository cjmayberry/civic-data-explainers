## What this tracks  
Every station of the GIRA shared-bike system — 154 in the live service — with its location, hours, tariff options, payment methods, and support contact.

## Why it matters to you  
If you rely on GIRA to get around, this is the authoritative list of where the bikes live. **estacaolocalizacao** names the station (e.g. "105 - CC Vasco da Gama"), **horariofuncionamento** tells you when it's open ("6h00 - 2h00" in the sample), and **tarifario** shows the actual prices (annual €25 / monthly €15 / daily €2 in the sample) before you commit to a pass. **wifi** marks stations with connectivity.

## How to read this data  
**estacaolocalizacao** — station name and location (e.g. "105 - CC Vasco da Gama").  \n**latitude / longitude** — precise coordinates (38.768548, -9.096222 in the sample) for mapping.  \n**horariofuncionamento** — when the station is operational (e.g. "6h00 - 2h00").  \n**tarifario** — pricing by pass type; **formaspagto** — payment methods (MB, PayPal, credit cards; occasional users may need a €300 deposit).  \n**contatoservassistencia** — help contact (gira@emel.pt, (+351) 211 163 060).

## Try it yourself  
Open the [GIRA stations in EMEL's data portal](https://dados.emel.pt/dataset/girastations), find the station nearest your home or office by name, and check its **horariofuncionamento** and **tarifario** before you buy a pass — the annual rate is a fraction of daily passes if you ride regularly.
