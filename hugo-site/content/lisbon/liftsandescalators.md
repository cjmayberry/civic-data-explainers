## What this tracks  
The elevators and escalators that support walking in Lisbon's hilly public spaces — each one's location, parish, postcode, contact, and operating hours.

## Why it matters to you  
Lisbon's hills are the reason this dataset exists. If you have limited mobility, push a stroller, or carry heavy loads, the **localizacao** field (e.g. "Largo do Chão do Loureiro; Calçada do Marquês de Tancos") tells you exactly where the infrastructure is, and **freguesia** tells you which parish covers it. **horario** shows when it runs — an elevator that closes at night may force a long detour.

## How to read this data  
**localizacao** — the public space where the lift/escalator sits (e.g. "Largo do Chão do Loureiro").  \n**freguesia** — Lisbon's parish (e.g. "Santa Maria Maior", "Areeiro").  \n**cP7** — the 7-digit postcode (e.g. "1100-340") for precise navigation.  \n**contacto** — who to call if it's out of service (e.g. "351 252 637 619").  \n**horario** — operating hours (blank in the sample — check the source for updates).

## Try it yourself  
Open the [lifts and escalators in EMEL's data portal](https://dados.emel.pt/dataset/liftsandescalators) and search your parish (**freguesia**). Knowing where the nearest public elevator is — before you need it — is exactly the kind of civic data that makes the city usable.
