## What this is  
This dataset tracks park facilities like playgrounds, pools, and picnic areas across Oklahoma City parks.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing what amenities exist in nearby parks helps you plan outings, find accessible features, or reserve spaces for events. For example, this data lets you:  
- Find the closest playground with shade structures for summer playdates  
- Check if a park shelter can be reserved for a family reunion  
- Locate ADA-accessible restrooms near walking trails  

## How to read this data  
Key fields to look for:  
- **Park**: Which park contains the facility  
- **Facility Type**: What the item is (e.g., "playground", "picnic shelter")  
- **Accessibility**: Whether it meets ADA standards (helpful for strollers/wheelchairs)  
- **Reservation**: If you need to book it in advance
