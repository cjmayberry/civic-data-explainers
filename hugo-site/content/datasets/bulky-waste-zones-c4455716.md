## What this is  
This dataset shows where and when bulky waste (like furniture or appliances) gets picked up in Oklahoma City.

## Why it matters to you  
If you need to toss a couch, mattress, or other large item, this data tells you:  
- Which day to put it out for collection  
- Who’ll actually pick it up (city crew or private contractor)  
- Whether your address falls in a service zone  

Putting items out on the wrong day could mean they sit for weeks or you get fined.

## How to read this data  
Key details in the dataset:  
- **Route**: The zone name (like "NW1") for your area.  
- **Pickup Day**: When crews typically collect (e.g., "1st Monday of month").  
- **Service Provider**: Whether it’s the city or a company like "Waste Management".  

## Try it yourself  
1. [Open the map version](https://data.okc.gov/) (if available).  
2. Type your address to see your zone’s pickup day and provider.  
3. Mark your calendar so you don’t miss the next collection.
