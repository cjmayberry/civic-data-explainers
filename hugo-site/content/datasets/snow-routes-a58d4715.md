## What this is  
This dataset tracks which streets Oklahoma City prioritizes for snow and ice treatment during winter storms.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your nearest snow route helps you plan safer winter travel. If you live near or commute along these roads, they'll be cleared first after a storm. This affects decisions like which route to take to work, where to park overnight during snow forecasts, or whether your child's school bus route will run on time.

## How to read this data  
- **Quadrant**: Shows which general area of the city the route serves (e.g., NW, SE)  
- **Location 1**: The main street name that gets treated (e.g., "N Western Ave")  
- **Location 2**: Cross streets or additional details about the treated stretch  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your street is a snow route by searching the dataset for your nearest major road. If it's listed, expect quicker plowing after storms. If not, plan for longer delays or consider alternative routes.
