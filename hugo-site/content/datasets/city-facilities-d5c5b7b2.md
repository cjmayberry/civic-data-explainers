## What this is  
A list of Oklahoma City-owned buildings and facilities with their locations and types.

## Why it matters to you  
This data helps you find city services near you—like where to pay a water bill, drop off hazardous waste, or attend a public meeting. If you're starting a business, it shows where to get permits. Parents can locate nearby rec centers, and community groups can find meeting spaces to rent.

## How to read this data  
Key fields include:  
- **Address**: Where the facility is located (e.g., "420 W Main St")  
- **Facility Type**: What the building is used for (e.g., "Library," "Fire Station")  
- **Facility Name**: Official name (e.g., "Downtown Public Library")  
- **Facility Information**: Extra details like hours or services offered  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your ZIP code or street name to find the closest city facility. Need a parking permit? Look for "Public Works" buildings. Want to vote early? Check for "Election Board" locations.
