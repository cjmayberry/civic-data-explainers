## What this is  
This dataset shows different park benefit areas in Oklahoma City where development fees apply.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're building a new home, business, or development, these zones determine whether you'll pay extra fees to fund nearby park improvements. The fees help maintain green spaces, trails, and recreation areas that serve your new development. Knowing your benefit area category could add thousands to your project costs or exempt you entirely.

## How to read this data  
- **Benefit Area**: The general neighborhood or region (like "NW 23rd Street Corridor")  
- **Category**: Fee classification (likely tiers like high/medium/low impact)  
- **Area**: Size of the benefit zone in square feet - bigger areas may mean more shared costs  

## Where this leaves you  
While you can't look up specific addresses here, this data explains why similar projects in different parts of town might face different park fees. Check okc.gov's development fee pages for exact rates tied to these zones.
