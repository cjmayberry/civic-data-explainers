## What this is  
A collection of official construction records for roads, drainage systems, and storm sewers in Oklahoma City.  

## Why it matters to you  
These "as-built" documents show what was actually constructed (not just planned) for infrastructure near you. When a street floods repeatedly, these records help crews trace whether drains were built correctly. Before digging on your property, checking these can reveal hidden sewer lines. Developers use them to verify lot drainage meets code before building.  

## How to read this data  
- **AsBuilt ID**: A reference number linking to scanned construction plans (like "PW-2021-0456")  
- **AsBuilt Type**: Labels the infrastructure type (e.g., "Paving", "Storm Sewer")  
- **Description**: May note project dates, contractors, or special features (like "24-inch reinforced concrete pipe")  

## Where this leaves you  
While you can’t look up records by address here, these documents exist for specific projects. Contact Public Works with a street name or project ID if you need construction details.
