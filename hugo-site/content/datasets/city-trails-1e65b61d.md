## What this is  
A list of walking and multi-use trails in Oklahoma City parks.

## Why it matters to you  
This data helps you choose where to walk, bike, or ride based on trail rules and features. Need a stroller-friendly path? Looking for horse trails? Want to avoid construction zones? This shows which trails match your needs before you go.

## How to read this data  
Key fields to check:  
- **Status**: "Open" means ready to use, while "Under construction" means delays  
- **Rating**: Difficulty level (easy/moderate/hard) for planning hikes  
- **Accessible**: "Yes" means ADA-compliant for wheelchairs/strollers  
- **Motorized/Hike/Bike/Equestrian**: Shows what activities each trail allows  

## Where this leaves you  
While you can't look up trails by address here, this helps compare options across the city for your next outdoor trip. Check the "Trail Name" and "Design Use" to find ones near you.
