## What this is  
This dataset shows where police briefing stations are located in Oklahoma City.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where your nearest police station is helps you:  
- Report crimes in person when needed  
- Find safe places to meet officers for exchanges (like online purchases)  
- Understand response times if you call 911 (stations closer to you may mean faster help)  

## How to read this data  
Key fields include:  
- **Facility Name**: The official name of the police station (e.g., "Hefner Division")  
- **Facility Address**: The exact street address of the station  

## Try it yourself  
Look up the police station nearest to your home or workplace using the dataset’s map or address list. Note its name and how long it takes to drive there—this could matter in an emergency.
