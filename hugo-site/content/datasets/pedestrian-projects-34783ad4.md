## What this is  
A list of past, current, and future sidewalk and street projects focused on pedestrian safety in Oklahoma City.

## Why it matters to you  
This data helps you see where walking infrastructure is being improved—or neglected—in your area. If you're choosing a walking route to school, pushing for sidewalk repairs near your business, or advocating for safer crosswalks, this shows what projects are planned, underway, or already finished. Delayed projects might mean longer waits for safer streets.

## How to read this data  
Key fields to check:  
- **Project Name**: Often includes the street name (e.g., "NW 23rd St Sidewalks").  
- **Project Type**: Tells you if it’s a sidewalk repair, new crossing signal, etc.  
- **Status**: Reveals if work is "Completed," "In Progress," or "Planned."  
- **Description**: May specify exact blocks or intersections affected.  

## Where this leaves you  
While you can’t look up projects by your exact address, scanning the full dataset (sorted by "Status") helps spot trends—like whether your neighborhood has fewer completed projects than others.
