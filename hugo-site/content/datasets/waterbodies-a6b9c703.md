## What this is  
This dataset shows the locations of lakes, ponds, and other waterbodies in Oklahoma City.  

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where waterbodies are helps with decisions like flood risk when buying property, planning recreational activities, or understanding local wildlife habitats. For example, if you're considering a home near a pond, this data helps you check for potential drainage issues or mosquito control needs.  

## How to read this data  
- **Name**: The official name of the waterbody (if it has one).  
- **Type**: What kind of waterbody it is (lake, pond, etc.).  
- **Area**: Size in square feet—useful for comparing different waterbodies.  
- **Length**: The distance around the waterbody’s edge in feet.  

## Where this leaves you  
This data doesn’t let you search by address, but you can still explore waterbody locations on a map to see what’s nearby.
