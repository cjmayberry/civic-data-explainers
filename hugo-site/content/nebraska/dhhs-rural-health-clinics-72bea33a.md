## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all state-licensed Rural Health Clinics (RHCs) in Nebraska, including their locations and contact information.

## Why it matters to you  
If you live in rural Nebraska, this data helps you find the nearest clinic that accepts Medicare/Medicaid patients when choosing healthcare providers. Small medical practices can use it to identify potential referral partners in underserved areas. The roster dates tell you whether a clinic is currently operational, preventing wasted trips to closed facilities.

## How to read this data  
**LEGALNAME** — Official clinic name, like "ORCHARD MEDICAL CLINIC".  
**MDSDBA_FACILITY_ADDRESS** — Physical street address (e.g., "103 WASHINGTON ST").  
**FAC_CITY/CNTYNAME** — Location details showing towns (e.g., "ORCHARD") and counties (e.g., "ANTELOPE").  
**GIS_Status** — Confirms if the clinic is active ("on current roster") or inactive.  

## Try it yourself  
Search for clinics within 30 miles of your ZIP code using the coordinates (X/Y fields) on Nebraska's public health maps.
