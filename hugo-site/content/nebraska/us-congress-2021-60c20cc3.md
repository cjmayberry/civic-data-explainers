## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nebraska's three U.S. Congressional District boundaries as established by the state legislature in 2021.  

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your district tells you which representative votes on federal laws affecting Nebraska’s farms, roads, and healthcare funding. If you’re advocating for rural broadband grants or disputing a federal agency decision, this determines whom to contact. The boundaries also impact where candidates campaign, so local business owners might see more political ads or events in their area during election years.  

## How to read this data  
**DISTRICT** — The district number (1, 2, or 3), like the sample value "1" for eastern Nebraska including Lincoln.  
**POPULATION** — Total residents in the district (e.g., "653822.0"), showing how evenly districts are divided.  
**BOUND_AREA** — Geographic size in square units (e.g., "6044.91455"), highlighting urban/rural splits (District 2 is compact Omaha, while District 3 covers most of the state).  

## Try it yourself  
Check which district your address falls in using Nebraska’s [official district lookup tool](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/redistricting/2021/congressional-map.php).
