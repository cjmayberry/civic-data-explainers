## What this tracks  
This dataset identifies Nebraska's biologically unique ecoregions and priority watersheds for conservation efforts.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying rural property in the Sandhills, this data shows whether your land falls within a protected priority watershed — which could affect well drilling permits or grazing rights. Farmers can check if their fields border conservation areas that might qualify for USDA stewardship programs. Hunters and anglers can locate ecologically rich areas likely to support game species.

## How to read this data  
**ECOREGION** — Names distinct ecological zones like "Sandhills" or "Loess Hills," indicating landscapes with unique plants and wildlife.  
**Shape_Leng** — Measures boundary length in feet (e.g., 1,787,574.6487), showing how extensive a conservation area is.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your hunting grounds or ranch land overlaps with a priority watershed by searching the Nebraska Natural Legacy Project map for your county.
