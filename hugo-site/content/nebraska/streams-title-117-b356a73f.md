## What this tracks  
This dataset records detailed information about streams in Nebraska, including their water quality classifications, designated uses, and key ecological features.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying property near a Nebraska stream, this data tells you whether the water supports fishing (Aquatic_Life = "WA") or has recreational value (Recreation = "PCR"). Farmers can check the Agricultural field to see if a stream is approved for irrigation use. Anglers can identify stretches with sensitive species like the Key_Species field's "26,33,i,j" codes before planning fishing trips.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — Official stream segment name, like "Big Blue River - Turkey Creek to Nebraska-Kansas border".  
**Aquatic_Life** — Water quality for fish/wildlife: "WA" means fully supported, other codes indicate impairments.  
**Length_miles** — Segment length (e.g., 50.1 miles) helps gauge the scale of water features.  
**Key_Species** — Lists numbered species codes (e.g., "26,33") for ecologically important aquatic life.

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for streams near your Nebraska property using the Subbasin field (e.g., "BB1" for Big Blue River) to check water quality classifications that could affect land use decisions.
