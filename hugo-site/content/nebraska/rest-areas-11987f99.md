## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all official Nebraska Department of Transportation rest areas along state highways.

## Why it matters to you  
Planning a road trip across Nebraska? This data helps you locate clean rest stops with amenities like bathrooms and picnic areas before you hit the road. Truckers can identify safe overnight parking spots along their routes. Families can find designated break areas during long drives through the state.

## How to read this data  
**Display_Name** — The visible name of the rest area (e.g., "Ogallala Rest Area WB" for westbound I-80).  
**Ph_Address** — The highway location using mile markers (e.g., "I-80 MM 132.55" means it's at mile marker 132.55 on Interstate 80).  
**City** — The nearest town (e.g., "OGALLALA"), useful for estimating drive times between stops.  
**Site_Type** — Confirms the location is an official rest area (not just a gas station or parking lot).  

## Try it yourself  
Before your next Nebraska road trip, check for rest areas along your route by searching this dataset for cities you'll pass through or highway numbers you'll travel (like I-80 or Highway 83).
