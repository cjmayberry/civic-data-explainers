## What this tracks  
This dataset defines official county boundaries in Nebraska and includes demographic data like population counts, racial breakdowns, and housing statistics for each county.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business in York County, this data shows you that 90% of residents identify as White Alone, helping you assess cultural representation in your marketing. The 657 vacant housing units could indicate rental opportunities for property investors. Parents can see that 480 people live in institutional facilities (like nursing homes), which may inform elder care choices for aging relatives.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — Official county name (e.g., "York County").  
**Total_Population** — Resident count (e.g., 14,125 in York County).  
**Hispanic_or_Latino** — Self-identified Hispanic/Latino residents (e.g., 839 people).  
**Housing_Vacant** — Unoccupied homes (e.g., 657 units available for potential renters/buyers).
