## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nebraska's Supreme Court judicial district boundaries as established by the 2021 redistricting process.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're involved in a court case, this tells you which Supreme Court district handles appeals from your county. Business owners challenging state regulations need to know which district's precedents apply to their case. Voters can verify their judicial district when evaluating retention elections for Supreme Court justices.

## How to read this data  
**DISTRICT** — The numbered judicial district (e.g., "6" for the 6th District). Nebraska has seven Supreme Court districts.  
**POPULATION** — Total residents in each district (e.g., 327,892), showing how redistricting balanced representation.  
**BOUND_AREA** — Geographic size in square units (e.g., 49,762.375), revealing urban/rural district proportions.

## Try it yourself  
Check which Supreme Court district contains your county using the Secretary of State's district lookup tool at https://sos.nebraska.gov/elections/electoral-maps.
