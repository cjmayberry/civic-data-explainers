## What this tracks  
This dataset maps which fire department serves each area of Nebraska in emergency situations.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your fire response area helps you verify if your home or business falls under the coverage you expect. If you're considering a property purchase in Nebraska, this data confirms whether the local fire department meets your safety standards. Renters can also check if their apartment's emergency response matches their landlord's claims.

## How to read this data  
**DsplayName** — The identifier for your fire district (e.g., "T504" in Douglas County).  
**ServiceURI** — Contact number for the responding agency (e.g., "tel:1123456789").  
**Agency_ID** — The official code for your local fire service provider (e.g., "gisdougco_ne_us").  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your Nebraska address on the state's GIS portal to see which fire district would respond to emergencies at your location.
