## What this tracks  
This dataset shows how deep you need to dig to reach groundwater at locations across Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing groundwater depth helps Nebraska farmers decide where to drill irrigation wells and what crops to plant — shallow-rooted crops may struggle where water is deep. Homeowners planning to install a well can estimate drilling costs, since deeper wells require more materials and labor. Ranchers can use this to identify areas where livestock ponds might naturally fill.

## How to read this data  
**DEPTH** — The vertical distance to reach water, shown as ranges like "0-50 ft." (shallow) or "100-150 ft." (deep). Lower numbers mean water is closer to the surface.
