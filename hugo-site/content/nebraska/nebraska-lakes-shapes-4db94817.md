## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the shoreline boundaries of Nebraska lakes and reservoirs that have undergone bathymetric (depth) surveys.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a fishing trip in Nebraska, this data tells you which lakes have detailed depth maps available — crucial for finding prime fishing spots. Property owners near these lakes can check the **ShorelineL** field to understand their water frontage, while boaters can look for lakes with **VolumeTabl** data to gauge water volume changes seasonally.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — Official lake name (e.g., "Blue Lake").  
**County** — Where the lake is located (e.g., "Garden").  
**Acres** — Lake surface area (e.g., 323.4 acres).  
**LakeMapPDF** — Link to downloadable depth map (e.g., "Blue Lake Map.pdf").  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your county in the dataset to find nearby surveyed lakes, then download their PDF maps to plan your next fishing trip or waterfront activity.
