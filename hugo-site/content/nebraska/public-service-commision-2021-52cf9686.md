## What this tracks  
This dataset records the boundaries and population data for Nebraska's Public Service Commission (PSC) districts as established after 2021 redistricting.  

## Why it matters to you  
The PSC regulates utilities like electricity, natural gas, and telecommunications in Nebraska. Knowing your district helps you identify which commissioner represents your area when filing complaints about service disruptions or rate changes. For small businesses, this data clarifies which district’s policies apply if you’re negotiating bulk utility contracts or disputing infrastructure fees.  

## How to read this data  
**DISTRICT** — The PSC district number (e.g., "2"). This determines which commissioner oversees utility regulations in your area.  
**POPULATION** — The estimated resident count per district (e.g., "409738"). Shows how representation is balanced across regions.  
**BOUND_AREA** — The geographic size of the district in square miles (e.g., "146.796326"). Larger areas may face different infrastructure challenges.  

## Try it yourself  
Check which PSC district you’re in by comparing your address to the [interactive district map](http://news.legislature.ne.gov/red/).
