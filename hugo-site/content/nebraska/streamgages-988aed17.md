## What this tracks  
This dataset provides real-time water level and flow measurements from monitoring stations along Nebraska's rivers and reservoirs.

## Why it matters to you  
Farmers near the Republican River can check current **Stage** (water height) to decide whether to irrigate crops this week. Homeowners downstream of Harlan County Reservoir can see the **FloodStatus** to know if they should move valuables to higher ground. Anglers planning a trip to McCook can check **Discharge** (flow rate) to predict fishing conditions on the Republican River.

## How to read this data  
**Stage** — Current water height in feet (sample: 2.41 ft at McCook station). Compare to **FloodStage** (9.0 ft at this location) to assess flood risk.  
**Discharge** — Water flow rate in cubic feet per second (sample: 0.58 ft³/s). Low values may indicate drought conditions.  
**FloodStatus** — Current flood risk level (sample: "Normal"). Values include Action, Flood, Moderate and Major stages.  
**StationName** — Specific waterway and location (sample: "Republican River at McCook").

## Try it yourself  
Check if your nearest river station (like Republican River at McCook) shows a **Stage** within 2 feet of its **FloodStage** — this could mean rising flood risk for nearby properties.
