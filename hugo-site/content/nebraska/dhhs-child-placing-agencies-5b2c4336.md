## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) licensed Child Placing Agencies (CPAs) that arrange foster care or adoptive placements.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering fostering or adopting a child in Nebraska, this data helps you verify licensed agencies near you. Parents can check an agency's official standing before committing to services, and prospective foster families can confirm which organizations are authorized to work with DHHS. Knowing an agency's license status and location helps families make informed decisions about who will guide them through foster care or adoption processes.

## How to read this data  
**Full_Name** — The official name of the licensed agency (e.g., "Father Flanagan's Boys' Home").  
**Address** / **City** / **County** — Physical location details (e.g., "213 MAHER DR", "Boys Town", "Douglas County").  
**License_Number** — The state's unique identifier for the agency (e.g., "CPA008").  
**Issue_Date** — When the agency's current license took effect (shown as a timestamp; convert to readable date).  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for agencies in your county by filtering the "County" field to compare options near you.
