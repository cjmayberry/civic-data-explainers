## What this tracks  
This dataset records fishing locations across Nebraska, including details about each site's amenities and available fish species.

## Why it matters to you  
Anglers in Nebraska can use this data to plan weekend fishing trips by finding nearby lakes with their preferred fish species. Families can identify locations with playgrounds and restrooms before loading up the car. The "Attributes" field tells you whether a spot has picnic areas or accessible piers—key details when planning an outing with children or mobility-limited family members.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — Official name of the fishing location, like "Sidney Legion Park Pond".  
**Species** — Fish available at the site, such as "Bluegill, Channel Catfish, Largemouth Bass, Rainbow Trout".  
**Attributes** — Amenities listed as phrases like "Family Friendly Location".  
**Comments** — Additional notes like "Fishing pier, playground, and restroom available".

## Try it yourself  
Search for lakes containing "Rainbow Trout" in the Species field to find winter fishing spots near your Nebraska town.
