## What this tracks  
This dataset records the names and locations of minor streams and ditches across Nebraska, as identified by the state's Department of Natural Resources.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own farmland or property near a waterway, this data tells you whether your land borders a named stream or ditch — crucial for flood risk assessment, irrigation planning, or permit applications. Developers can check if a construction site intersects protected water features before purchasing land. Hikers and anglers can verify local stream names for navigation or fishing regulations.

## How to read this data  
**GNIS_Name** — Official name of the waterway (e.g., "Central Cutoff Ditch").  
**LengthKM** — The stream's length in kilometers (e.g., 0.639 km).  
**GNIS_ID** — Unique identifier from the Geographic Names Information System (e.g., 00828053).  
**FlowDir** — Indicates flow direction (1.0 typically means downstream).  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your nearest named stream to see if it passes through your property or neighborhood — important for understanding drainage patterns during heavy rains.
