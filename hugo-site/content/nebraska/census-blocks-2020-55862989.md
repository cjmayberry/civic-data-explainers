## What this tracks  
This dataset records population and housing statistics for every census block in Nebraska from the 2020 Census.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a small business in Nebraska, this data shows whether a neighborhood has enough residents to support your shop. Homebuyers can check if a block has mostly families or single residents before moving. Community groups use it to advocate for services in areas with growing populations.

## How to read this data  
**Total_Population** — The number of people living in this block, like "2" in the sample.  
**White_Alone** — Counts residents identifying only as White, like "2" in the sample.  
**Total_Housing** — Shows all housing units, with sample value "1" meaning one home exists there.  
**Housing_Occupied** — Indicates lived-in homes versus vacant ones, like "1" occupied in the sample.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your Nebraska address on the Census Bureau's map to see your exact block's population mix and housing density.
