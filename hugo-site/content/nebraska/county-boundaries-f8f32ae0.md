## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the official boundaries of all 93 counties in Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing exact county lines helps when determining which county office handles your property taxes, voting precinct, or emergency services. If you're opening a business in Nebraska, this data confirms which county's zoning laws and business licenses apply to your location. Residents relocating near county borders can verify school districts and trash collection services.

## How to read this data  
**Cnty_Name** — The official county name (e.g., "Adams").  
**Cnty_No** — Nebraska's numeric county code (e.g., 1 for Adams County).  
**CountyFIPS** — The federal FIPS code used in national datasets (e.g., "001" for Adams County).

## Try it yourself  
Check which Nebraska county contains your address using the [state's interactive county map](https://dot.nebraska.gov/maps/county/).
