## What this tracks  
This dataset identifies Nebraska lakes that are 10 acres or larger, sourced from the National Hydrography Dataset.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a fishing trip, looking for waterfront property, or considering a lakeside business in Nebraska, this data shows which bodies of water are officially recognized as major lakes. Knowing a lake's size (like whether it's 10 acres or 500 acres) helps you gauge recreational potential or development opportunities. The dataset also helps verify if a waterbody you’re interested in is state-tracked, which matters for permitting or conservation efforts.  

## How to read this data  
**GNIS_Name** — Official lake name from the Geographic Names Information System (e.g., blank in samples, but populated entries might show "Lake McConaughy").  
**AreaSqKm** — Lake size in square kilometers (e.g., 0.133 in samples, roughly 33 acres).  
**Elevation** — Height above sea level in meters (e.g., 0.0 in samples, suggesting plains or low-lying areas).

Search the dataset for lakes near your Nebraska town to compare sizes—helpful if you’re scouting fishing spots or evaluating flood risks near larger water bodies.
