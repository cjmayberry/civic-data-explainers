## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's public school districts, including their boundaries, names, and student populations.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're moving to Nebraska or choosing where to rent/buy a home, this data shows which schools serve specific areas and how many students they educate. Parents can compare district sizes when considering class sizes, while businesses like tutoring centers use student population numbers to gauge potential demand. Realtors frequently need to verify school districts for property listings.

## How to read this data  
**DistrictName** — The official name of the school district (e.g., "Kenesaw Public Schools").  
**StudentPopulation** — Total enrolled students (e.g., 267), helping compare district scales.  
**AreaSqmile** — Geographic size in square miles (e.g., 111), indicating whether the district covers rural or compact urban areas.  

## Try it yourself  
Search your Nebraska address on the state's [school district boundary map](https://www.education.ne.gov/) to see which district serves your neighborhood.
