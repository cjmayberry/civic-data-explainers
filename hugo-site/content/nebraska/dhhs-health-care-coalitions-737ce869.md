## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's regional Health Care Coalitions (HCCs) that coordinate emergency medical responses across county lines.

## Why it matters to you  
When a blizzard hits or a hospital reaches capacity, your local HCC determines whether ambulances can reroute to neighboring counties or if emergency medical supplies will reach your area. Knowing your HCC region (like the Omaha Metropolitan Healthcare Coalition) tells you which hospitals, health departments, and EMS teams collaborate during crises. If you're considering relocating within Nebraska, this reveals how medical emergencies would be handled in different regions.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The full coalition name (e.g., "Omaha Metropolitan Healthcare Coalition") showing which counties work together for health emergencies.  
**ShortName** — An abbreviation like "OMHCC" used in official communications and emergency plans.  
**HCCID** — A unique code (e.g., "310089") for referencing the coalition in state records and grant applications.

## Try it yourself  
Check which Health Care Coalition covers your Nebraska county by matching its location to the regional map at [Nebraska DHHS emergency preparedness portal](https://dhhs.ne.gov/Pages/Emergency-Preparedness.aspx).
