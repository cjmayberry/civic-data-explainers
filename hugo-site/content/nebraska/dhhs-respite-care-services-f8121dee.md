## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Nebraska's licensed respite care facilities that provide temporary relief for caregivers of children or adults with special needs.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're caring for a family member with disabilities in Nebraska, this data helps you find nearby respite services to get a break while ensuring quality care. Knowing which facilities are licensed by DHHS means you can verify their legitimacy before contacting them. For example, a parent in Omaha could use this to compare locations and client capacities when planning weekend care for their child.

## How to read this data  
**LEGALNAME** — The official name of the facility (e.g., "Children's Respite Care Center").  
**MDSDBA_FACILITY_ADDRESS** — Physical location (e.g., "2010 N 88TH STREET" in Omaha).  
**BEDLICTOT** — Maximum clients served (e.g., "16.0" indicates capacity for 16 individuals).  
**TELEPHONE** — Direct contact number (e.g., "4024961000" shown with no formatting).  

## Try it yourself  
Search for facilities near your ZIP code using the [DHHS public portal](https://dhhs.ne.gov/Pages/default.aspx) to find respite options within driving distance.
