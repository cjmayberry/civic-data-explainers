## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all licensed adult day service facilities in Nebraska, including their locations and contact information.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're caring for an aging parent or a family member with disabilities in Nebraska, this data helps you find nearby professional daytime care options. Knowing which facilities exist in your county could save hours of web searches when you need respite care or specialized support. The verified license numbers also let you confirm a facility's legitimacy before visiting.

## How to read this data  
**LEGALNAME** — The official name of the facility, like "MidPointe".  
**ADDRESS** + **FAC_CITY** — Physical location details, such as "101 N 30TH ST" in "LINCOLN".  
**TELEPHONE** — Direct contact number formatted as 10 digits (e.g., 4024758717).  

## Try it yourself  
Search for facilities in your Nebraska county by filtering the CNTYNAME field (e.g., "LANCASTER") to see options near you.
