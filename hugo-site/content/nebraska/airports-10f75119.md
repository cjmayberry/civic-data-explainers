## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all airports in Nebraska, including their locations, facilities, and operational details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a business trip or shipping goods, this data helps you choose the nearest airport with the services you need—like jet fuel availability or runway lighting for night flights. Small aviation businesses can scout locations by comparing airport amenities, such as fixed-base operators (FBOs) or fuel types. For rural residents, it clarifies which nearby airports offer emergency medical flights or cargo services.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — Official airport name, like *Ainsworth Regional Airport*.  
**Code** — Three-letter airport identifier (e.g., *ANW* for Ainsworth).  
**Fuel** — Available fuel types, such as *100 LL & Jet A*.  
**Runway1** — Primary runway designation and orientation (e.g., *17 / 35* means runways align northeast/southwest).  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for airports near your Nebraska town using the **City** field (e.g., "Ainsworth") to check runway surfaces or fuel options before booking a flight.
