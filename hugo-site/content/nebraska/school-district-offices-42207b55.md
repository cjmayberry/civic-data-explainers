## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and contact details of all school district offices in Nebraska, including both public and private accredited schools.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're moving to a new area in Nebraska and need to enroll your child, this data tells you exactly where to find your local school district office for registration. Business owners planning after-school programs can use this to identify which districts to partner with based on location. Parents comparing school options can quickly call district offices listed here to ask about transfer policies or special programs.

## How to read this data  
**District_N** — The official name of the school district (e.g., "AINSWORTH COMMUNITY SCHOOLS").  
**Address** — Physical location of the district office (e.g., "520 E 2ND ST").  
**Phone** — Direct contact number for the office (e.g., "(402)387-2333").  
**Type_of_Sc** — Accreditation status (e.g., "ACCREDITED"), important for verifying school quality.
