## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Nebraska school districts with their names, numbers, sizes, and student populations.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're moving to Nebraska or choosing where to enroll your child, this data shows which districts serve specific areas and how many students they have. A smaller district like Kenesaw Public Schools (267 students) may offer different resources than a larger one. Parents can compare districts before house-hunting or transferring schools.  

## How to read this data  
**DistrictName** — The official name of the school district, like "Kenesaw Public Schools."  
**DistrictNumber** — A unique code for the district (e.g., "01-0003"), often used in official forms.  
**StudentPopulation** — Total enrolled students (e.g., 267), indicating district size.  
**AreaSqmile** — Geographic size in square miles (e.g., 111), useful for understanding bus routes.  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for your street address or ZIP code to confirm which school district serves your neighborhood.
