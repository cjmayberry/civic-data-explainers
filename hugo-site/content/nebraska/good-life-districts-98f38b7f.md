## What this tracks  
This dataset tracks Nebraska's designated Good Life Districts (GLDs), special zones where sales tax rates may differ to support economic development.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a retail shop or restaurant in Nebraska, buying in a GLD could mean charging customers a different sales tax rate than neighboring blocks. For residents, this affects where you'll pay more or less sales tax on everyday purchases — a $1,000 appliance could cost $50 less just by crossing into a GLD.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The official district name, like "GLD Avenue One".  
**DateEffective** — When the district's tax rules start applying (sample value converts to April 1, 2024).  
**Established** — Confirms if the district is active (sample value: "yes").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your business address or favorite shopping area falls within a GLD by searching the district names at Nebraska's economic development portal.
