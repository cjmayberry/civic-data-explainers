## What this tracks  
This dataset defines the official boundaries of incorporated cities in Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing exact city boundaries helps you determine which municipality provides services to your property. If you're opening a business in Dunning, this data confirms whether you'll pay taxes to the city or just Blaine County. Homebuyers can verify if a property qualifies for city water/sewer by checking if it falls inside these boundaries.

## How to read this data  
**City_Name** — The official name of an incorporated city (e.g., "Dunning").  
**County_Nam** — The county where the city is located (e.g., "Blaine").  

## Try it yourself  
Compare the city boundary map with your property address to confirm which government handles your trash collection and local ordinances.
