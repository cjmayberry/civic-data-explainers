## What this tracks  
This dataset identifies Nebraska waterways that fail to meet water quality standards under the Clean Water Act, along with their specific pollutants and restoration plans.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you fish in the Big Blue River, this data tells you whether it’s safe to eat your catch (the "Aquatic_Life" field shows contamination risks). Farmers can check the "Agriculture_Water_Supply" field to see if irrigation water meets safety standards. Homebuyers near affected streams (like those marked "I" for impaired) might reconsider properties relying on well water.  

## How to read this data  
**Waterbody_Name** — Official name of the stream (e.g., "Big Blue River").  
**Aquatic_Life** — "I" means impaired (unsafe for fish/wildlife), "S" means threatened. Sample shows "I" due to atrazine and aluminum.  
**Impairments_Causes** — Lists pollutants (e.g., "E. coli") and affected seasons (e.g., "May-June Atrazine").  

## Try it yourself  
Search your local river’s name in the dataset to see if it has active contamination warnings before fishing or swimming.
