## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all hospitals licensed by Nebraska's Department of Health and Human Services, including their locations and capacities.

## Why it matters to you  
When choosing emergency care for your family, this data shows which hospitals are nearest to your home or workplace in Nebraska. For small healthcare businesses, it reveals where existing facilities are concentrated (like seeing that O'Neill has a 25-bed hospital) when planning new services or partnerships.

## How to read this data  
**LEGALNAME** — The hospital's official name, like "Avera St. Anthony's Hospital".  
**MDSDBA_FACILITY_ADDRESS** — Physical location (e.g., "300 N 2ND ST") where patients visit.  
**BEDLICTOT** — Total licensed beds, indicating facility size (sample shows "25.0" for a rural hospital).  
**FAC_CITY** — The city where the hospital operates (e.g., "O'NEILL"), helping locate nearby care.
