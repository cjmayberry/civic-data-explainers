## What this tracks  
This dataset contains verified 911 emergency service addresses for every location in Nebraska, including street names, numbers, and geographic coordinates.

## Why it matters to you  
When you call 911 from a landline or mobile phone in Nebraska, dispatchers use this data to pinpoint your exact location. Knowing your address is correctly registered helps ensure first responders can find you during emergencies. For businesses, this confirms your official address matches what appears in emergency systems and postal services — critical for receiving deliveries and emergency services.

## How to read this data  
**FULL_ADDR** — The complete street address (e.g., "12924 JOSEPHINE ST").  
**COUNTY** — Two-letter county code (e.g., "SC" for Sarpy County).  
**ZIP** — The 5-digit postal code (e.g., "68505" for Lincoln).  
**LAT/LONG** — Geographic coordinates (e.g., 40.83235642, -96.6414176) used to map the location.  

## Try it yourself  
Verify your home or business address appears correctly by searching for your street name in the **ST_NAME** field (e.g., "JOSEPHINE") and checking the **FULL_ADDR** matches your expectation.
