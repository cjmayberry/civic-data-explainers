## What this tracks  
This dataset records population, housing, and demographic details for each census tract in Nebraska as of the 2020 Census.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business in Nebraska, this data reveals whether a neighborhood has enough residents in your target demographic to support it. Homebuyers can check if an area has families (via age data) or high vacancy rates (which might signal investment opportunities). Community groups use race/ethnicity data to advocate for culturally appropriate services in their tract.

## How to read this data  
**Total_Population** — The number of people living in the tract (e.g., 2,527). Compare this to **Housing_Occupied** (e.g., 1,005) to estimate average household size.  
**White_Alone_18YrsAndOver** — Adults identifying solely as White (e.g., 1,839). Contrast with **Hispanic_or_Latino_18YrsAndOver** (e.g., 18) to understand cultural diversity.  
**NursingFacilities_SkilledNursingFacilities** — Institutionalized elderly population (e.g., 38), useful for healthcare businesses or family planning.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your census tract using [Nebraska's address search tool](https://nebraska.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=8bfa4e637a1e4e8f929a5e5b0e018b6b) to see your neighborhood's exact population mix and housing stats.
