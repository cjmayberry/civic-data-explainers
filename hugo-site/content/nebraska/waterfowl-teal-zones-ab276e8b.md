## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's waterfowl hunting zones specifically for teal species, including season dates and bag limits.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a teal hunting trip in Nebraska, this data tells you exactly when and where you can legally hunt, how many birds you can take per day (**Low Plains** zone allows 6 teal daily), and what hours you're permitted to shoot (from 30 minutes before sunrise to sunset). Knowing your zone's specific season dates (like **5 – 13 Sept**) helps avoid fines for hunting outside regulated periods. The possession limit of **18 birds** prevents accidental over-harvesting when storing your game.

## How to read this data  
**Zone** — Geographic area designation like "Low Plains" where regulations apply.  
**Season** — Exact hunting dates (e.g., "5 – 13 Sept (9 days)").  
**DailyBagLi** — Maximum teal allowed per day ("6 in aggregate" for three species combined).  
**ShootingHo** — Legal hunting times ("30 minutes before sunrise to sunset").
