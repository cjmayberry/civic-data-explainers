## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's designated bighorn sheep hunting units where permits are required.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a bighorn sheep hunt in Nebraska, this data shows exactly where you're allowed to hunt with a valid permit. Knowing the unit boundaries helps you scout legal hunting areas before applying for limited permits through Nebraska Game and Parks Commission. The **Links** field directly connects you to official regulations.  

## How to read this data  
**UnitName** — The hunting zone's official name (e.g., "PINE RIDGE").  
**Links** — URL to Nebraska's hunting guidelines (e.g., "http://outdoornebraska.gov/guides/").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your preferred hunting spot falls within a bighorn sheep unit by cross-referencing the UnitName with Nebraska's interactive hunting maps.
