## What this tracks  
This dataset contains the official geographic boundary of the state of Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a road trip across Nebraska or need to verify whether a location falls within state lines for tax or regulatory purposes, this boundary data provides the definitive answer. Businesses shipping goods can use it to confirm when deliveries cross into or out of Nebraska, which affects tax calculations and shipping regulations. Residents disputing property boundaries near state borders may reference this data in legal matters.

## How to read this data  
**ID1** — A simple identifier for the boundary (always "1" since Nebraska only has one state boundary).  
**Shape__Area** — The total square measurement of Nebraska's territory (value not provided in samples).  
**Shape__Length** — The combined length of all boundary lines around Nebraska (value not provided in samples).  

## Try it yourself  
While this dataset doesn't support address-level lookups, you can overlay it with county or city boundary maps to see exactly where Nebraska ends and neighboring states begin. Many free online mapping tools allow this type of comparison.
