## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's designated elk hunting units, showing where specific hunting regulations apply.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning an elk hunt in Nebraska, this data tells you exactly which unit you'll be hunting in—critical because permits, season dates, and harvest limits vary by unit. Knowing your unit helps you apply for the correct permit and avoid fines for hunting in restricted areas. The linked regulations show whether you can use rifles or bows in your chosen unit.

## How to read this data  
**UnitName** — Identifies the hunting unit (e.g., "Unit 1"), which determines your permit requirements.  
**Hunting_Li** — Direct link to the official Nebraska hunting guide (e.g., "https://outdoornebraska.gov/guides/") with unit-specific rules.  

## Try it yourself  
Visit the Hunting_Li URL for your unit to check this year's elk season dates before applying for a permit.
