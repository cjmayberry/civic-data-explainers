## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's Deer Management Units, which are zones regulating hunting seasons and permit requirements.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning to hunt deer in Nebraska, this tells you exactly which unit your preferred hunting area falls under—critical for buying the correct permit. Knowing your unit's boundaries helps avoid accidental trespassing or illegal harvests. The linked hunting regulations show season dates and bag limits specific to each unit.

## How to read this data  
**UnitName** — The designated hunting area (e.g., "Blue Northwest").  
**Hunting_Li** — Direct URL to official regulations (e.g., `https://outdoornebraska.gov/guides/`).  
**Shape_Area** — Relative size of the unit in square degrees (e.g., 0.78), useful for comparing zone coverage.  

## Try it yourself  
Check which Deer Management Unit contains your hunting spot by cross-referencing the map at [outdoornebraska.gov/guides](https://outdoornebraska.gov/guides/) with landmarks or county lines.
