## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and contact information for Nebraska's 17 Educational Service Unit (ESU) headquarters.

## Why it matters to you  
If your child attends a Nebraska public school, your district partners with one of these ESUs for special education services, professional development, and shared resources. Knowing your ESU's headquarters helps you find training workshops for parents, inquire about special needs programs, or locate regional education resources. Small businesses that provide school supplies or tech services can also use this to identify regional education offices for contract opportunities.

## How to read this data  
**ESU** — The numbered unit (1-17), like "1" for ESU 1 covering northeast Nebraska.  
**Headquarters** — The city where the main office is located, such as "Wakefield."  
**Address** — Physical location like "211 TENTH ST" where you could visit in person.  
**Phone** — Direct contact number formatted as "(402)287-2061" for immediate inquiries.

## Try it yourself  
Call your local ESU office using the phone number in this dataset to ask about upcoming parent workshops or special education resources in your school district.
