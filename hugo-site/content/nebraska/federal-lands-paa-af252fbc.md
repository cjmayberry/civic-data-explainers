## What this tracks  
This dataset maps federal lands in Nebraska, including wildlife areas managed by agencies like the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service.

## Why it matters to you  
If you hunt or hike, this data tells you where public lands are located near you and what rules apply—like whether you can use lead shot or target-shoot. For example, knowing that Theesen WPA in Clay County allows hunting but prohibits rifles helps you plan a compliant trip. Farmers can also check if nearby federal lands might affect drainage or land use.

## How to read this data  
**AreaName** — The name of the federal land parcel (e.g., "Theesen WPA").  
**OwnerAgency** — Which government agency manages the land (e.g., "U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service").  
**SpeciesLikelyPresent** — Wildlife you might encounter (e.g., "Dove, Pheasant, Waterfowl").  
**NontoxicShotOnly** — Whether lead ammunition is banned (e.g., "Yes").  

## Try it yourself  
Search for federal lands near your town using the **County** or **LocationInfo** fields (e.g., "Clay County; ½N of Glenvil") to find hunting or recreation spots. Call the **DistrictManagerPhone** listed for specific questions.
