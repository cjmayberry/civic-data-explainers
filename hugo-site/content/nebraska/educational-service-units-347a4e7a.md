## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the locations and contact information for Nebraska's 17 Educational Service Units (ESUs), which support local school districts.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a parent or educator in Nebraska, this data tells you which ESU serves your school district and how to contact them for resources like special education support, professional development, or technology services. Small businesses that provide educational supplies or services can use this to identify regional ESU offices for partnerships or outreach.  

## How to read this data  
**ESU** — The numbered unit (e.g., "1" for ESU 1).  
**Headquarters** — The city where the ESU office is located (e.g., "Wakefield").  
**Address** / **City** / **St** — The physical location (e.g., "211 TENTH ST", "Wakefield", "NE").  
**Phone** — Direct contact number (e.g., "(402)287-2061").  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your nearest ESU office by checking the "City" field to find the closest headquarters location to you.
