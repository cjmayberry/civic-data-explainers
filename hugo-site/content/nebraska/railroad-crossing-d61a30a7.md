## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and details of railroad crossings across Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a delivery route or commute that crosses train tracks, this data tells you exactly where crossings are and which railroad company maintains them. For truck drivers or school bus operators, knowing the precise GPS coordinates helps avoid last-minute turns onto tracks. Homebuyers near crossings can check who's responsible for maintenance before purchasing.

## How to read this data  
**NationXingNumber** — The federal crossing identifier, like "967438M".  
**RROwner** — Which railroad company operates the crossing, such as "BNSF Railway Company".  
**Latitude/Longitude** — Exact crossing location (sample: 40.58305359, -98.40190887).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular commute crosses tracks by entering your route's coordinates and comparing them to the dataset's latitude/longitude values.
