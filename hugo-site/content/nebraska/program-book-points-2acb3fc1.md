## What this tracks  
This dataset records planned transportation improvement projects across Nebraska, including road and bridge maintenance, safety upgrades, and intersection improvements.

## Why it matters to you  
This data shows when major road work will happen near your home or business. If you're planning to open a store in Knox County, seeing that Highway 012 will have intersection upgrades in 2028-2032 helps you anticipate construction delays. Trucking companies can check planned bridge repairs along their routes before bidding on contracts. Homebuyers can avoid purchasing near roads scheduled for multi-year construction.

## How to read this data  
**Hwy** — The highway number where work will occur (e.g., "012" for Highway 12).  
**ProjectName** — Specific location and type of project (e.g., "District 2, 3, 5, 6, & 8 Beacons" for safety lighting installations).  
**TypeImprovement** — The kind of work planned (e.g., "Intersection" for traffic light or lane changes).  
**ProgramYear** — When work is scheduled (e.g., "2028-2032" means planning for those fiscal years).  

## Try it yourself  
Check the Program Book Application map for projects along your commute route by searching your county name (like "Knox") and highway number.
