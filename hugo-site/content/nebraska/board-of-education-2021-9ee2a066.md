## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's Board of Education districts as redrawn in 2021 following legislative approval.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you’re a parent, educator, or voter, this data tells you which Board of Education district your address falls under—determining who represents your schools’ policies until 2031. Knowing your district helps you contact the correct representative about curriculum changes, funding decisions, or school board elections. Nebraska residents can also verify if redistricting shifted their representation since the last census.  

## How to read this data  
**DISTRICT** — The numbered district (e.g., "4"), which correlates to a specific geographic area and elected board member.  
**POPULATION** — The approximate resident count per district (e.g., "245713.0"), showing how evenly distributed representation is across the state.  
**BOUND_AREA** — The geographic size of the district in square miles (e.g., "80.25618"), useful for comparing urban vs. rural coverage.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the [Secretary of State’s redistricting map](http://news.legislature.ne.gov/red/) to find your Board of Education district by entering your address.
