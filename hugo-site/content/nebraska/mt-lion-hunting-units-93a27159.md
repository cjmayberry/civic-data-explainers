## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's mountain lion hunting units where regulated hunting is permitted.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a hunter or outdoor enthusiast, this data shows exactly where mountain lion hunting is legally allowed in Nebraska. Knowing the boundaries of units like Pine Ridge helps you avoid trespassing or hunting in prohibited areas. Landowners near these units can also check if their property falls within a hunting zone, which may affect livestock management decisions.

## How to read this data  
**UnitName** — The official hunting area designation, like "Pine Ridge Unit."  
**Hunting_Li** — A direct link to Nebraska Game and Parks Commission's hunting guide for regulations (e.g., `https://outdoornebraska.gov/guides/`).  

## Try it yourself  
Visit the [NGPC Permits webpage](https://outdoornebraska.gov/guides/) to check if your preferred hunting location falls within a designated unit before applying for a permit.
