## What this tracks  
This dataset records population and housing statistics for small geographic areas (block groups) in Nebraska, based on the 2020 U.S. Census.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business in Nebraska, this data shows whether a neighborhood has enough residents to support it. For example, **White_Alone_18YrsAndOver: 858** in one sample block group tells you how many working-age adults live there. Homebuyers can check **Housing_Vacant: 38** to gauge housing availability, while community groups might use **Hispanic_or_Latino: 12** to identify translation needs for local services.

## How to read this data  
**Total_Population** — The number of people living here (sample: 1168).  
**Housing_Occupied** — How many homes are currently lived in (sample: 468).  
**NursingFacilities_SkilledNursingFacilities** — Count of residents in care homes (sample: 38), useful for health service planning.  
**INTPTLAT/INTPTLON** — The block group's center point (sample: +40.7927071, -097.7928431) to locate it on a map.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your Nebraska block group using the GEOID (like 311859699002) to see your neighborhood's exact population mix and housing stats.
