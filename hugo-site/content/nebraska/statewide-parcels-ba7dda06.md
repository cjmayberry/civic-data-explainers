## What this tracks  
This dataset records property boundaries and assessment details for every land parcel in Nebraska, maintained by county assessors.

## Why it matters to you  
Before buying land in Nebraska, this data shows you the exact acreage (GIS_Acres), zoning type, and whether neighboring parcels are residential or agricultural. It also reveals the school district serving a property—critical for families comparing homes in Burwell versus Elyria. The assessed values help spot discrepancies if your property tax bill seems unusually high.

## How to read this data  
**Ph_Full_Address** — Physical street address when available (e.g., "123 MAIN ST").  
**Total_Assessed_Value** — The county's valuation of land + structures (sample: $33,230).  
**School_District_Name** — Which district serves the parcel (e.g., "BURWELL PUBLIC SCHOOLS").  
**Legal_Description** — Survey details like "ELYRIA-CIEMNY ADD/996 L1-4 6.9 AC".  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your Nebraska address in the [state’s parcel viewer](https://nebraska.maps.arcgis.com/) to see its boundaries, assessed value, and school district.
