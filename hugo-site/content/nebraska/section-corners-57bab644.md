## What this tracks  
This dataset records the precise geographic coordinates of section corners across Nebraska, which mark the boundaries of land survey sections.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying rural property in Nebraska, this data confirms the exact legal boundaries of your land parcel before finalizing the purchase. Farmers can verify fence lines or irrigation rights match their property deeds. Developers planning wind farms or solar arrays use these points to ensure infrastructure stays within leased land boundaries.

## How to read this data  
**TOWNSHIP** — The north-south position in the Public Land Survey System (PLSS), like "19" meaning Township 19 North.  
**RANGE** — The east-west position in PLSS, such as "4" meaning Range 4 East.  
**SECTION_** — Identifies a specific 1-square-mile section within the township, with values from 1-36 (sample shows "7" for Section 7).  
**DIR1** — Compass direction modifier for the range, like "E" indicating east of the principal meridian.

## Try it yourself  
Check where your property's section corner falls by matching the Township (e.g., 19N), Range (e.g., 4E), and Section numbers from your deed to this dataset.
