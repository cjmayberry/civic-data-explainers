## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nebraska's official state boundary and its demographic, housing, and institutional population statistics from the U.S. Census.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business in Nebraska, this data tells you how many adults live in different racial or ethnic groups statewide—helping you gauge potential customer bases. For example, knowing that 234,715 residents identify as Hispanic or Latino could inform bilingual marketing strategies. Community organizers can also use the housing vacancy rate (70,966 units) to advocate for affordable housing programs.

## How to read this data  
**Total_Population** — Nebraska's entire population (sample: 1,961,504).  
**Hispanic_or_Latino** — Residents identifying as Hispanic/Latino (sample: 234,715).  
**Housing_Vacant** — Unoccupied housing units (sample: 70,966).  
**CollegeUniversity_StudentHousing** — Students living in campus housing (sample: 20,831).
