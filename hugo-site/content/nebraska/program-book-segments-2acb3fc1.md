## What this tracks  
This dataset records planned highway and bridge improvement projects across Nebraska, including their locations, types, costs, and timelines.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a major purchase like farmland near Highway 12 in Knox County, this data shows when nearby roadwork might affect property access or values. Trucking companies can check when their regular routes (like District 3 corridors) will have construction delays. Residents can see if problematic intersections on their commute (like the beacon upgrades listed) will be improved before winter.

## How to read this data  
**Hwy** — The highway number where work will occur, shown with leading zeros (e.g., "012" means Highway 12).  
**TypeImprovement** — The kind of upgrade planned, like "Intersection" changes or bridge repairs.  
**ProgramYear** — When work is scheduled, formatted as a range (e.g., "2028-2032" means sometime in those five years).  
**Estimate** — Project cost in thousands (e.g., "688" means $688,000 budgeted).

## Try it yourself  
Check the Program Book Application map for projects along your most-used highway by searching the Hwy field with your route number (like "012" for Highway 12).
