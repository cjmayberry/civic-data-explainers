## What this tracks  
This dataset records permitted groundwater wells in Nebraska, including their locations, uses, and technical specifications.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a Nebraska farmer considering drilling a new irrigation well, this data shows existing wells in your area and their permitted pumping rates — critical for understanding local water availability. Landowners can check whether neighboring wells might affect their property's water table. Small towns can identify industrial water users that might impact municipal supplies during droughts.

## How to read this data  
**WellUseDescription** — The well's primary purpose, like "Irrigation" or "Industrial".  
**PumpRate** — Maximum allowed water withdrawal in gallons per minute (sample: 1250 GPM).  
**StaticWaterLevel** — Depth to water when not pumping (sample: 91 feet below surface).  
**CountyName** — Where the well is located (sample: Fillmore County).  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset by your county name to see all permitted wells near your property and their water usage rates.
