## What this tracks  
This dataset records Nebraska's state park areas, including their locations, sizes, recreational features, and management details.

## Why it matters to you  
Planning a weekend camping trip? This data tells you whether Alexandria SRA allows hunting in specific zones (like its northern portion) before you pack your gear. If you're a fishing enthusiast in Jefferson County, you can verify which areas have active boating IDs to launch your boat. Small businesses near parks can use the acreage and visitor status to gauge potential customer traffic during peak seasons.

## How to read this data  
**AreaName** — The park's name, like "Alexandria SRA".  
**Acres** — Size of the park in acres (e.g., 101 acres).  
**Notes** — Specific rules or features, such as "Hunting allowed only in northern portion."  
**AreaType** — Classification like "SRA" (State Recreation Area) or other designations.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your nearest park (by county) has hunting or boating access by searching the dataset for your county name in the **County** field.
