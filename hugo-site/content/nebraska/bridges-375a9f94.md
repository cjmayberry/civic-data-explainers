## What this tracks  
This dataset records the location, structural details, and inspection history of bridges and bridge-sized structures across Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a trucking route through Nebraska, this data tells you whether a bridge can handle your vehicle's weight by checking its **Posting** (weight limit) and **DeckWidth**. Residents can see when their local bridge was last inspected (**DateInspected**) and what creek or road it crosses (**FeatureIntersected**), helping anticipate maintenance delays. Farmers can verify detour lengths (**Detour_Length**) if a rural bridge closes during harvest season.

## How to read this data  
**Location** — The street address or route where the bridge is located (e.g., "1695 S. PRIMROSE AVE").  
**YearBuilt** — Construction year (e.g., 1935), indicating aging infrastructure.  
**StructureType** — Building material (e.g., "Concrete Slab"), hinting at durability.  
**ADT** — Average daily traffic (e.g., 20 vehicles), showing how heavily a bridge is used.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the **Posting** and **DeckWidth** of bridges along your regular commute by searching for your street name in the **Facility_Carried** field.
