## What this tracks  
This dataset records planned Nebraska Department of Transportation projects for maintaining and improving highways and bridges across the state.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a major purchase like farmland or a trucking business along Highway 12 in Knox County, this tells you whether road construction will disrupt access during your first years of operation. Nebraska residents can check if their regular commute routes have scheduled improvements like intersection upgrades that may cause delays. Business owners near project sites can anticipate increased traffic during construction periods.

## How to read this data  
**Hwy** — The highway number where work will occur, like "012" for Highway 12.  
**ProjectName** — Specific location details, such as "District 2, 3, 5, 6, & 8 Beacons" for safety improvements across multiple regions.  
**ProgramYear** — When work is scheduled, shown as ranges like "2028-2032" for long-term projects.  
**TypeImprovement** — The kind of work planned, such as "Intersection" upgrades or bridge repairs.

## Try it yourself  
Search the Program Book Application for your county name to see all planned highway projects near your property or business location within the next decade.
