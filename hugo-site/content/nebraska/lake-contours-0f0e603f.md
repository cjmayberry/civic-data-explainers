## What this tracks  
This dataset records depth and elevation measurements for lakes and reservoirs across Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a fishing trip or considering lakeside property, this data tells you whether Blue Lake is deep enough for bass fishing (20 feet) or if it's more suited for shallow-water activities. For waterfront homeowners, the elevation measurements (like 3764.8 feet) help understand flood risks during heavy rains. Nebraska residents can use this to compare lake conditions before driving hours to recreational sites.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — Official lake name, like "Blue Lake" in the sample.  
**Depth** — Maximum depth in feet (sample shows 20 feet).  
**Elevation** — Height above sea level in feet, important for flood planning (sample shows 3764.8).  

## Try it yourself  
Check the depth of your favorite Nebraska fishing spot by searching the dataset for its name — look for lakes with 15+ feet depth if you're targeting walleye or bass.
