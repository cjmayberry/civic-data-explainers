## What this tracks  
This dataset maps which emergency medical service (EMS) provider responds to 911 calls in different areas of Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
When you call 911 for a medical emergency, this data determines which ambulance service arrives at your door. Knowing your local EMS provider helps you verify response times and contact them directly for non-emergency questions. If you're considering moving within Nebraska, checking this data shows whether your new neighborhood has a different EMS provider than your current one.

## How to read this data  
**DsplayName** — The official name of the EMS provider (e.g., "Douglas County EMS").  
**ServiceURI** — The direct phone number for the provider (e.g., "tel:1123456789").  
**ES_NGUID** — A unique code identifying the response zone (e.g., "Z612").  

## Try it yourself  
Contact your county emergency management office to request the EMS response area map for your specific address.
