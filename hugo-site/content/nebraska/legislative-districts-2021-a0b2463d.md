## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's 49 legislative districts, showing their boundaries and current state senators.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your district helps you contact your state senator about local issues like school funding or road repairs. For example, if Gering residents want to voice concerns about a proposed state law, this data tells them Senator Brian Hardin represents them and provides his direct capitol phone number. Small business owners can use this to track which senator advocates for economic development in their area.

## How to read this data  
**DISTRICT** — The district number (e.g., "48") that identifies your geographic representation area.  
**Senator** — Your elected state legislator's name (e.g., "Hardin, Brian").  
**CapitolPhone** — The direct office line to reach your senator (e.g., "(402) 471-2802").  
**City** — Major municipalities within the district (e.g., "Gering").

## Try it yourself  
Look up your address on Nebraska's [legislative district finder](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/districts/) to see which senator represents you and save their contact information for future reference.
