## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's regional Health Care Coalitions that coordinate emergency medical responses across counties.

## Why it matters to you  
When a disaster hits Nebraska — like a flood, tornado, or disease outbreak — your local Health Care Coalition determines which hospitals, clinics, and EMS teams work together to provide care. Knowing your coalition helps you understand emergency medical resources in your region. For example, if you're choosing between two nursing homes for a relative, checking which coalition they're in reveals their backup support system during crises.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The full coalition name, like "Omaha Metropolitan Healthcare Coalition," showing which counties work together for emergencies.  
**ShortName** — An abbreviation like "OMHCC" used in official communications about the coalition.  
**HCCID** — A unique code (e.g., 310089) for technical tracking of the coalition region.  

## Try it yourself  
Find which Health Care Coalition covers your Nebraska county by searching [Nebraska's public health regions map](https://dhhs.ne.gov/Pages/Public-Health-Resources.aspx) and cross-referencing the coalition names in this dataset.
