## What this tracks  
This dataset records planned Nebraska Department of Transportation (NDOT) highway and bridge improvement projects across the state.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a long-term business location near a highway or regularly commute between Nebraska counties, this data tells you where future road construction will create delays or accessibility changes. A trucking company could avoid leasing warehouse space along Highway 12 in Knox County during 2028-2032, knowing intersection upgrades (Project 01079) will disrupt traffic. Residents can check if their regular routes appear in the Program Year listings before making car purchase decisions based on expected road conditions.

## How to read this data  
**Hwy** — The highway number where work will occur, like "012" for Highway 12.  
**ProjectName** — Specific location details, such as "District 2, 3, 5, 6, & 8 Beacons" showing which maintenance districts are involved.  
**TypeImprovement** — The kind of work planned, like "Intersection" upgrades versus bridge repairs or resurfacing.  
**ProgramYear** — When work is scheduled, formatted as ranges like "2028-2032" for multi-year projects.  

## Try it yourself  
Search the [Program Book Application](https://dot.nebraska.gov/media/13626/program-book-2024.pdf) (PDF) for your county or frequently traveled highway number to see upcoming projects in your area.
