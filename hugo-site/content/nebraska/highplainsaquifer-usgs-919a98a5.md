## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries of the High Plains aquifer, a critical groundwater source beneath parts of eight U.S. states including Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
If you rely on well water for your home, farm, or business in Nebraska, this data shows whether you're drawing from the High Plains aquifer—a resource that’s declining in some areas due to overuse. Knowing your location relative to the aquifer boundary helps you anticipate potential water restrictions, plan for well depth adjustments, or advocate for local conservation measures before shortages affect your property.

## How to read this data  
**AQUIFER** — Confirms the mapped area is part of the "High Plains aquifer" system (the only value in this dataset).  
**Shape__Area** and **Shape__Length** — Technical measurements of the aquifer's geographic footprint (values not provided in samples).

## Try it yourself  
Zoom to your Nebraska property on the [USGS Groundwater Atlas](https://www.usgs.gov/mission-areas/water-resources/science/aquifers-and-groundwater) to see if it overlaps with this aquifer boundary.
