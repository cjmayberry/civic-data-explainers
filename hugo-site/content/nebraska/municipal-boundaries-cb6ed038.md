## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the official boundaries of every city, town, and village in Nebraska.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a business in Nebraska, these boundaries tell you which municipality's zoning laws and business taxes apply to your location. Homeowners can check whether a property falls within a specific town's school district or emergency services coverage. For example, a coffee shop owner in Omaha would verify they're inside city limits to follow local signage rules.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The municipality's common name (e.g., "Danbury").  
**NAMELSAD** — The full legal name with community type (e.g., "Danbury village").  
**ALAND** — Total land area in square meters (e.g., 838,941m² for Danbury).  
**INTPTLAT/INTPTLON** — Geographic center coordinates (e.g., +40.0377063, -100.4051085).  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your town's name in the dataset to see its exact boundaries and compare land area with neighboring communities.
