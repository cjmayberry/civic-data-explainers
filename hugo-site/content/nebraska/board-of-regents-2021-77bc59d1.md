## What this tracks  
This dataset records the boundaries and population statistics for Nebraska's University Board of Regents districts following the 2021 redistricting.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a Nebraska resident, student, or work with the University system, this data tells you which regents represent your area—important when advocating for campus policies or funding decisions. Parents comparing university resources across regions can see if their district serves a larger or smaller population. Small businesses near campuses can identify which regent to contact about partnership opportunities.  

## How to read this data  
**DISTRICT** — The regent district number (e.g., "4"), matching elected representatives to geographic areas.  
**POPULATION** — Total residents per district (e.g., "237525.0"), showing how evenly distributed representation is.  
**BOUND_AREA** — Geographic size in square miles (e.g., "78.146935"), useful for identifying rural vs. urban districts.  

## Try it yourself  
Check which regent district contains your address using the Secretary of State’s redistricting tools at [nebraska.gov/sos](https://nebraska.gov/sos).
