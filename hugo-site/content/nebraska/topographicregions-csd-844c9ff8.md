## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's major topographic regions, like plains and rolling hills.  

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your region's topography helps anticipate drainage issues before buying land or building in rural areas. Farmers can compare their field elevations to nearby regions to assess flood risks. Developers in Lincoln might check whether a vacant lot sits in a flood-prone valley before planning construction.  

## How to read this data  
**DESCRIP** — Names the landform type (e.g., "Rolling Hills"), indicating whether an area has steep slopes or flat terrain.  

## Try it yourself  
Search your county in Nebraska’s [interactive map](https://snr.unl.edu/data/geologysoils/topographicregions.aspx) to see if your property lies in a floodplain or elevated area.
