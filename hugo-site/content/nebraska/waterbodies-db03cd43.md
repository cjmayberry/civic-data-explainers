## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's small waterbodies (10 acres or smaller) like ponds and small lakes, modified from the National Hydrography Dataset.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying property near a small pond, this data confirms whether that water feature is officially mapped—affecting flood risk assessments and insurance rates. Farmers planning irrigation systems can check for nearby small waterbodies that might influence drainage patterns. Hunters and anglers can verify if small fishing holes are documented public resources before planning trips.  

## How to read this data  
**GNIS_Name** — Official name from the Geographic Names Information System (sample: blank, meaning unnamed).  
**AreaSqKm** — Waterbody size in square kilometers (sample: 0.004 = ~1 acre).  
**FCode** — Classification code; 43613 indicates "lake/pond" (sample: 43613).  

## Try it yourself  
Check the [Nebraska Department of Natural Resources map viewer](https://dnr.nebraska.gov/) to see if your property borders any mapped small waterbodies affecting drainage or recreation rights.
