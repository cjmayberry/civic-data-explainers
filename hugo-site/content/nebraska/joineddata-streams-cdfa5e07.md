## What this tracks  
This dataset records fishing locations, regulations, and species availability across Nebraska's public lands and waterways.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a fishing trip in Nebraska, this data tells you exactly where to find trout streams, what species are present, and the specific daily catch limits before you drive out. A small business owner running a bait shop near Fort Robinson could use the StreamComments field to advise customers about nearby camping amenities that might increase foot traffic.

## How to read this data  
**StreamName** — Specific waterway name like "Soldier Creek" where fishing is permitted.  
**Species** — Fish types available (e.g., "Brook Trout, Brown Trout") at each location.  
**Regs** — Catch limits and rules like "Daily bag limit of 2 trout on the Wilderness Area".  
**StreamComments** — Extra details such as "Camping available at Fort Robinson State Park".
