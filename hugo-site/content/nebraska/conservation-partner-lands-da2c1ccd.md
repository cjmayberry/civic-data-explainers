## What this tracks  
This dataset records protected conservation lands across Nebraska, including recreation areas and wildlife habitats managed by partner organizations like Ducks Unlimited and Natural Resources Districts.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a hunting trip in Nebraska, this data tells you exactly where you can legally hunt, what species are present, and whether firearms are restricted. For families, it reveals nearby nature areas like the Oxbow Trail Reservoir Recreation Area where you can hike or observe wildlife. The regulations field warns you about prohibited activities like target shooting before you arrive.

## How to read this data  
**AreaName** — The official name of the conservation area, like "Oxbow Trail Reservoir Recreation Area (NRD)".  
**SpeciesLikelyPresent** — Lists animals you might encounter, such as "Dove, Furbearers, Pheasant, Quail, Rabbit, Squirrel, Waterfowl".  
**Regulations** — Specific rules like "Hunting is permitted in designated areas... Call 402-362-6601 for NRD regulations".  
**LocationInfo** — Directions like "Butler County; 1½E of Ulysses, N side of road".  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your county in the dataset to find the closest conservation area, then check its **SiteURL** (like upperbigblue.org) for visiting hours and activity permits.
