## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's most ecologically important landscapes and watersheds identified in the state's Natural Legacy Project conservation plan.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying rural property in the Sandhills, this data shows whether your land falls within a protected Biologically Unique Landscape — which affects development rights and conservation incentives. Farmers can check if their fields border priority watersheds to qualify for USDA conservation programs. Hunters and anglers can locate high-value habitats for game species like pheasants or trout.  

## How to read this data  
**ECOREGION** — The major ecological zone (e.g., "Sandhills"), indicating distinct wildlife habitats and conservation needs.  
**Shape_Leng** — The boundary length of the protected area in feet (e.g., 1,787,574.6), helping gauge the scale of nearby conservation zones.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your county contains any Biologically Unique Landscapes by searching [Nebraska's Natural Legacy Project map](https://outdoornebraska.gov/nebraskanaturallegacy/) for your location.
