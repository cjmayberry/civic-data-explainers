## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's Public Land Survey System (PLSS) grid, showing township and range boundaries across the state.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying rural property in Nebraska, this data tells you exactly which township and range your land falls under — critical for verifying legal descriptions in deeds. Farmers can confirm their section boundaries before installing fences or drainage systems. Hunters and outdoor enthusiasts use it to navigate public lands correctly, avoiding accidental trespass on private sections.

## How to read this data  
**TOWNSHIP** — The north-south position (e.g., "N35" means 35 townships north of baseline)  
**RANGE** — The east-west position (e.g., "W57" means 57 ranges west of the principal meridian)  
**TR** — Combined township/range identifier (e.g., "W3557" means Township 35 North, Range 57 West)  

## Try it yourself  
Find your property's township and range by matching its location to the grid at Nebraska's [DNR GIS Data Viewer](https://dnr.nebraska.gov/gis-mapping-data).
