## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Nebraska's official trauma response regions used by emergency health services.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your trauma region helps you understand where emergency responders would transport you during a critical injury. If you're comparing hospitals in different parts of Nebraska—for example, when choosing where to establish a business with high injury risks—this shows which facilities serve your location. Parents can also check whether their child's school trip destinations fall within their home region's emergency coverage.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The designated trauma region (e.g., "Region 3"), which determines which hospitals and specialists are responsible for emergency care in that area.  

## Try it yourself  
Check which trauma region covers your Nebraska county by matching your location to these regions—this determines where ambulances would take you in a life-threatening emergency.
