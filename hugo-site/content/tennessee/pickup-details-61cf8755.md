## What this tracks  
This dataset shows sections of Tennessee highways adopted by volunteer groups through the TDOT Adopt-A-Highway program, including their locations and maintenance status.

## Why it matters to you  
If you’re planning a road trip or commute, this data tells you which stretches of highway are regularly cleaned by volunteers—meaning you’re likely to encounter cleaner roadsides. For businesses, sponsoring a highway section (visible here by your location) can improve local aesthetics and showcase community involvement. Tennessee residents can also use this to thank nearby volunteer groups or join one in their county.

## How to read this data  
**NBR_RTE** — The highway identifier (e.g., "SR050" for State Route 50).  
**BEGIN_LOG_MILE** and **END_LOG_MILE** — The mile markers where the adopted section starts and ends (e.g., 17.7 to 20.0 means the group maintains that 2.3-mile stretch).  
**COUNTY** — The county where the adopted section is located (e.g., "41" for Sevier County).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular commute route (e.g., SR050 between miles 17–20) is maintained by a volunteer group by searching the dataset for your highway number.
