## What this tracks  
This dataset maps locations where trout are stocked in Tennessee's waterways, including details about species, access, and seasonal programs.

## Why it matters to you  
Anglers can plan fishing trips by checking where rainbow or other trout species were recently stocked, avoiding unproductive spots. The data reveals whether a stream has 24-hour access or sunrise/sunset restrictions, helping you time your visit. Small bait shops near stocked areas can anticipate customer demand by tracking seasonal stocking programs like "Spring."

## How to read this data  
**Site_Name** — Specific location name, like "Little Buffalo River."  
**Species** — Type of trout stocked (e.g., "rainbow").  
**HoursOpen** — Access times, such as "24 Hours" or daylight-only.  
**ParkingType** — Facilities available (e.g., "Gravel Pull Off").

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
