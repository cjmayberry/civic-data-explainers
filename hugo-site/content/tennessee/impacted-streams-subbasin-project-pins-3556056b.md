## What this tracks  
This dataset tracks Tennessee streams impacted by upcoming 10-year transportation projects, including mitigation credits and project details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you live near SR-386 or SR-174 in Wilson County, this tells you exactly where new road construction will affect local waterways and how the state plans to compensate for environmental impacts. Business owners near these routes can anticipate multi-year construction zones, while homeowners can check if their property borders a stream marked for "High" impact. The mitigation credits show how seriously Tennessee is taking environmental protection during these projects.

## How to read this data  
**COL_N** — Impact level on streams (sample value: "High") shows ecological disruption severity.  
**PROJECT_D2** — Specific project locations (sample: "SR-386 (Vietnam Veterans Blvd), From near Saint Blaise Road to SR-174") pinpoints construction zones.  
**VLLOKUP** — Project status (sample: "26850000 The environmental studies are underway") reveals current phase.  
**CORRECTION_AVERAGE** — Typical mitigation credits (sample: 1129.04) quantifies environmental compensation efforts.

## Try it yourself  
Check if your Wilson County property falls within 17.5 feet of impacted streams by searching the dataset for "SR-386" or "SR-174" route labels near your address.
