## What this tracks  
This dataset records traffic volume and vehicle type percentages on Tennessee roads, including daily traffic counts (AADT), truck traffic, and road classifications.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business along Highway 64, the **AADT** field shows whether 1,700 or 17,000 cars pass daily — crucial for estimating customer traffic. Homebuyers can check **VHCL_MU_TRK** to avoid streets with 10% semi-truck traffic near their potential property. Tennessee drivers can use the **K_FACTOR** (peak hour congestion percentage) to identify roads that bottleneck during rush hour.

## How to read this data  
**AADT** — Annual Average Daily Traffic (sample: 1,698 vehicles) measures total cars/trucks passing a road segment daily.  
**VHCL_MU_TRK** — Multi-unit truck percentage (sample: 1%) shows semi-trailer presence.  
**IS_URBAN** — "Y" indicates urban zones where traffic patterns differ from rural roads.  
**RAW_DATA_LINK** — Links to detailed traffic reports (sample: tdot.ms2soft.com/tcds).

## Try it yourself  
Search the [TDOT Traffic Data site](https://www.tn.gov/tdot/long-range-planning-home/long-range-planning/data-and-maps.html) with your street name to see truck traffic percentages near your home.
