## What this tracks  
This dataset measures the smoothness of Tennessee roadways using the International Roughness Index (IRI), where lower numbers indicate smoother pavement.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a road trip through Tennessee, this data tells you which highways might give you a bumpier ride. Business owners shipping fragile goods can check roughness ratings to avoid routes that could damage their cargo. Cyclists and motorcyclists can use it to find smoother roads for a more comfortable ride.

## How to read this data  
**COUNTY_NAME** — Which county the road section is in, like "Bledsoe".  
**IRI_AVERAGE** — The roughness score (55.7 = moderately rough; lower is smoother).  
**ROUGHNESS_RATING** — Plain-language rating like "Good" to quickly assess road quality.  
**ROUTE_NUMBER** — The numbered highway (30 = State Route 30).  

## Try it yourself  
Check the roughness rating for your regular commute route by searching for the route number (like 30) in your county's data.
