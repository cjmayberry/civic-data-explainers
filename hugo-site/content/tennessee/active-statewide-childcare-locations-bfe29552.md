## What this tracks  
This dataset lists all active childcare providers in Tennessee that are licensed by the Department of Human Services (excluding school-based programs).  

## Why it matters to you  
Parents in Tennessee can use this data to find verified childcare options near their home or workplace before touring facilities. Small business owners opening new childcare centers can identify service gaps by checking how many providers exist in their county. The "Child_Care_Type" field helps parents compare home-based daycares versus larger centers.  

## How to read this data  
**Provider_Name** — The official name of the childcare facility, like "21st Century Child Development and Learn".  
**Child_Care_Type** — Whether it's a "Child Care Center" (larger facility) or "Family Child Care Home" (smaller home-based care).  
**Street_Address** — Physical location (e.g., "2503 BAILEY AVENUE") with city and ZIP code for mapping directions.  
**County** — The Tennessee county where the provider operates (e.g., "Hamilton"), useful for regional searches.  

## Try it yourself  
Search this dataset for your ZIP code to see all licensed childcare options within 5 miles of your home.
