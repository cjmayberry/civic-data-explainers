## What this tracks  
This dataset maps fishing access sites across Tennessee, including details like boat ramps, parking availability, and amenities at each location.

## Why it matters to you  
Anglers can use this to plan weekend fishing trips by checking if a site has boat ramps (**Ramps**) or shoreline access (**Comments**). Small bait shops near popular waterways like Norris Reservoir (**Waterway**) can see where anglers gather to target their advertising. Families can avoid sites with no restrooms (**Restrooms**) or those closed seasonally (**ClosedMonths**).

## How to read this data  
**Waterway** — The body of water (e.g., "Norris Reservoir").  
**Parking** — Surface type and capacity (e.g., "Gravel" with "Less Than 25" **Spaces**).  
**Hours_Open** — Operating times (e.g., "24" for 24-hour access).  
**AccessFee** — Whether there’s a cost to enter (e.g., "No").

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
