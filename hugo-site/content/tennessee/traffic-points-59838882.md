## What this tracks  
This dataset records vehicle traffic volumes at monitoring stations across Tennessee, including total daily traffic and breakdowns by vehicle type (cars, trucks, motorcycles).

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business on SR-170 in Anderson County, the **AADT** value of 16,392 tells you how many potential customers drive past daily. The **TRUCK_PERCENTAGE** of 3.79% helps delivery-based businesses gauge freight traffic. Homebuyers near Minor Arterial roads can check **AADT_PASSCARS** (11,852 passenger cars/day) to anticipate neighborhood noise levels.

## How to read this data  
**AADT** — Annual Average Daily Traffic (16,392 vehicles/day at this station).  
**TRUCK_PERCENTAGE** — Percentage of heavy vehicles (3.79% means ~621 trucks/day).  
**FUNCTIONAL_CLASS** — Road importance ("Minor Arterial" connects smaller roads to highways).  
**AADTYEAR** — When counts were taken ("2025" means projected future traffic).

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
