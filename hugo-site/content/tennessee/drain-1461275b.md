## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and details of drains installed along Tennessee's interstates, state routes, and certain locally maintained roads.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a homeowner near a state highway, this data tells you whether a drain exists to handle stormwater runoff that might otherwise flood your property. Small businesses along truck routes can check if nearby drains are documented, helping assess flood risks for deliveries or parking areas. For drivers, knowing drain locations explains where standing water might pool during heavy rains on Tennessee roads.

## How to read this data  
**FEAT_DESC** — The type of drain, like "Catch Basin" (sample value shown).  
**COUNTY_NAME** — Where the drain is located, such as "Lawrence" (sample value).  
**LOC_DESC** — Position relative to the road, like "Right" (sample value) for drains on the right side when traveling the route's direction.  
**LOG_MILE** — Distance along the route in miles (sample: 0.012 means just past the start).  

## Try it yourself  
Check for drains near your regular commute by searching the route number (like "05694" from the sample) on TDOT's map viewer if available.
