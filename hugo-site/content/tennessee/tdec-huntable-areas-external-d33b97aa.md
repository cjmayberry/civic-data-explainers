## What this tracks  
This dataset maps huntable lands within Tennessee State Parks and State Natural Areas, including property types, acreage, and special rules for hunters.

## Why it matters to you  
If you hunt in Tennessee, this data tells you exactly which state-managed lands allow hunting and under what conditions. For example, knowing **Pickett SF & WMA** is a Wildlife Management Area (not a park with stricter rules) could determine where you buy a hunting license or plan your season. The special instructions field warns you about seasonal road closures, like Yellow Doors Road being closed to vehicles from December 20 to March 15—critical for accessing remote areas.

## How to read this data  
**PRIMARY_** — The official name of the huntable area (e.g., "Pickett SF & WMA").  
**TYPE** — Classification like "Wildlife Management Area" or "State Park," which determines hunting regulations.  
**CALC_ACRES** — Size of the area in acres (e.g., "9" for small parcels).  
**Instructions** — Rules like "Seasonal Limited Access for Motorized Use (Closed Dec. 20 to Mar. 15)."

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
