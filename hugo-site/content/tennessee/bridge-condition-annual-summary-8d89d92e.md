## What this tracks
This dataset records the structural condition of every bridge in Tennessee as of 2022, based on official field inspections.

## Why it matters to you
If you regularly drive across bridges in Tennessee, this data tells you which ones are in poor condition and may face weight restrictions or closures. Business owners who rely on freight transportation can identify potential route disruptions before they impact deliveries. Residents can also see how old their local bridges are and whether Tennessee is maintaining critical infrastructure in their area.

## How to read this data
**COUNTY** — The Tennessee county where the bridge is located, like "Anderson" or other county names. **ROUTE_NUMBER** — The specific road or highway number where the bridge carries traffic, such as "02444." **OVERALL_CONDITION** — The bridge's current structural status rated as "Good," "Fair," or "Poor" based on federal inspection standards. **YEAR_BUILT** — When the bridge was originally constructed, with sample bridges dating back to 1985, and **AGE** — how many years old the bridge is as of the 2022 reporting year.

## Try it yourself
Search for your Tennessee county name to see all bridges in your area and their conditions. If you have a regular commute route, look up that specific route number to check whether any bridges along your path are rated as "Poor" and might face future restrictions.
