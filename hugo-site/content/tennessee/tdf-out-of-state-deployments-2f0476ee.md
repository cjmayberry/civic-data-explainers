## What this tracks  
This dataset records Tennessee Division of Forestry (TDF) deployments of personnel and equipment to assist with wildfires in other states.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you own property near wildfire-prone areas in neighboring states (like Mississippi or Arkansas), this data shows when Tennessee’s firefighting resources are stretched thin elsewhere. For example, during peak fire season, knowing that TDF crews are deployed out-of-state could prompt you to take extra precautions—like clearing brush earlier or reviewing your insurance coverage—since local response capacity may be reduced.  

## How to read this data  
**STUSPS** — The two-letter state code where Tennessee crews are deployed (e.g., "MS" for Mississippi).  
**Personnel** — The number of TDF firefighters sent (e.g., "1" in the sample).  
**Equipment** — The number of firefighting assets (like trucks) deployed (e.g., "1" in the sample).

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
