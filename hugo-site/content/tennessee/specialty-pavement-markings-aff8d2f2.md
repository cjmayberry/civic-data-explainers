## What this tracks  
This dataset records specialty pavement markings like stop bars and lane indicators on Tennessee interstates, state routes, and select locally maintained roads.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a truck route through Tennessee, this data shows where critical road markings like stop bars exist—helping you anticipate sudden stops or lane merges. For residents, it reveals whether high-traffic exits (like I-75's Exit 129) have clear lane guidance markings, which could affect your morning commute timing. Businesses near ramps can check if delivery routes have adequate pavement markings for truck navigation.

## How to read this data  
**FEAT_DESC** — The type of marking: "Stop Bar" means painted lines where vehicles must halt.  
**ROUTE** — Highway identifier: "I0075" is I-75, while "0129" in **EXIT_** pinpoints Exit 129.  
**LOC_DESC** — Marking position: "Right" indicates it's on the right side of the lane.  
**DATE_COLL** — When recorded: Values like "1458086400000" (March 2016) show data freshness.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular highway exit (e.g., I-75 Exit 129) has stop bars by searching the dataset for "I0075" and your exit number in the **EXIT_** field.
