## What this tracks
This dataset records the precise location and address information for every property in Tennessee that can receive emergency services through the 911 system.

## Why it matters to you
When you call 911, dispatchers use this data to send help to your exact location, even if you can't clearly communicate your address. If your property isn't in this database or has incorrect information, emergency responders might be delayed reaching you during a crisis. For Tennessee businesses, this data helps verify that your location is properly registered for emergency services and can help you understand how your address appears in official county records.

## How to read this data
**Add_Number** — the street number of the address, like 930. **St_Name** — the street name without directional indicators, such as "TUCKER BEND." **St_PosTyp** — the street type that comes after the name, like "RD" for road. **County** — which Tennessee county the address is located in, such as "GILES." **Inc_Muni** — whether the address is within city limits or shows "Unincorporated" for rural areas. **Zip_Code** — the postal code, like 38477.

## Try it yourself
Search for your street name in the St_Name field to see all addresses on your road and verify your property is included in Tennessee's emergency services database. You can also filter by your county name to see how addresses are officially formatted in your area.
