## What this tracks  
This dataset shows locations of active material producer plants approved by the Tennessee Department of Transportation (TDOT) for supplying construction materials like metal culverts, asphalt, or concrete.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a contractor bidding on a TDOT road project in Tennessee, this data helps you find approved suppliers for required materials near your job site. Knowing which plants are active and what they produce can save time and reduce transportation costs. For residents, it shows where materials for local road projects are sourced.

## How to read this data  
**PRODUCER_SUPPLIER_NAME** — The company name, like "Southeast Culvert Inc".  
**PLANT_TYPE_DESCRIPTION** — What the plant produces, such as "Pipe - Metal Culvert".  
**STATUS** — Whether the plant is currently "Active" or inactive.  
**CITY/STATE** — Location details, with some plants located out-of-state (e.g., "Winder, GA").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if there's an active material producer plant near your Tennessee county by filtering for your region number in the **REGION** field (e.g., Region 5 covers parts of East Tennessee).
