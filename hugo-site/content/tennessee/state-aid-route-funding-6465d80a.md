## What this tracks  
This dataset records how many miles of state-aid roads were maintained and how much funding was spent per county each fiscal year in Tennessee.

## Why it matters to you  
If you’re considering buying property or opening a business in a rural county, this data shows where state road maintenance dollars are being invested—helping you gauge infrastructure quality. County officials can use it to compare their funding levels with neighboring areas when advocating for future budgets. For residents, it clarifies why certain routes get repaved sooner than others.

## How to read this data  
**COUNTY** — The Tennessee county where work occurred (e.g., "Anderson").  
**FISCAL_YEAR** — The budget year the spending covers (e.g., "2024").  
**TOTAL_MILES** — Miles of road maintained, like "15.33" in Anderson County.  
**TOTAL_FUNDS** — Dollars spent, formatted as "1349202.01" (over $1.3M in this example).

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
