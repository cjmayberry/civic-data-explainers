## What this tracks  
This dataset records where landscaping and flower installations exist along Tennessee interstates, state routes, and certain park roads.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a roadside business (like a cafe or gas station) near an interstate exit, this data shows whether the state maintains attractive landscaping nearby—a factor that could influence customer appeal. For residents, it reveals which highway stretches have beautification efforts, like I-124 in Hamilton County, helping you anticipate scenic drives or identify areas where wildflowers might bloom seasonally.

## How to read this data  
**ROUTE** — The highway identifier, like "I0124" for Interstate 124.  
**FEAT_DESC** — The type of feature, with values like "Landscaping" or (implied by codes) potential flower beds.  
**LOC_DESC** — Which side of the road the feature is on, such as "Left" for the left shoulder when traveling the route's direction.  
**DATE_COLL** — When the data was gathered (e.g., "1457049600000" translates to March 2016).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your nearest highway exit (like "001A" on I-124) has landscaping by searching the dataset for your **COUNTY_NAME** and **EXIT_** number.
