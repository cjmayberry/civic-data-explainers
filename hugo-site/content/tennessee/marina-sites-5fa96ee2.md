## What this tracks  
This dataset records detailed information about marinas and boat launch sites across Tennessee, including amenities, parking, and access details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a fishing trip or boating outing in Tennessee, this data helps you choose the right marina based on your needs. It tells you whether a site has 24-hour access, boat ramps for your vessel size, or amenities like electric hookups and restrooms. Business owners offering fishing charters can use it to identify marinas with adequate trailer parking and fuel availability for clients.

## How to read this data  
**Waterway** — Which lake or river the marina serves (e.g., "Douglas Reservoir").  
**Launchable** — Maximum boat length the ramp can handle (e.g., "Up to 26 ft.").  
**AccessFee** — Whether there's a charge to use the facility (e.g., "Yes").  
**Restrooms** — Onsite bathroom availability (e.g., "Yes").

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
