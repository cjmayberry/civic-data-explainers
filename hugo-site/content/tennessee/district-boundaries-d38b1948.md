## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the four official forestry district boundaries in Tennessee and their forest coverage statistics.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a landowner or timber business in Tennessee, knowing your forestry district determines which local TDF office handles your burn permits, wildfire assistance, and forest management programs. A farmer in West Tennessee would contact different forestry staff than a sawmill owner in East Tennessee for the same services. The forest coverage percentages also show which regions have more viable timber resources.

## How to read this data  
**District** — The forestry region name, like "West Tennessee" or "Highland Rim".  
**Forested_Acres** — Total wooded land in the district (e.g., 3,768,634 acres in West Tennessee).  
**PercentForested** — Percentage of land covered by forest (sample data shows ~58% for West Tennessee).

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
