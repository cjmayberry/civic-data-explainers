## What this tracks  
This dataset lists public recreation areas, parks, and forests across Tennessee, including their locations, amenities, and managing agencies.  

## Why it matters to you  
Planning a family outing in Tennessee? This data helps you find parks with specific features, like playgrounds or trails, near you. If you’re considering moving to a new neighborhood, checking nearby parks can reveal whether amenities like splash pads or sports fields are available. For small businesses, like outdoor gear rental shops, this data identifies high-traffic recreation spots to target for marketing.  

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The park's name, like "Lake Hills Park."  
**Open_After_Dark** — Whether the park stays open at night ("Yes" or "No").  
**Trails** — The number of trails in the park (e.g., "1").  
**Condition_of_Park** — General upkeep, such as "Good" or "Poor."  

## Try it yourself  
Search for parks in your county by filtering the dataset for your **County_Name**, then check which ones have the amenities you care about, like **Playground** or **Picnic_Tables**.
