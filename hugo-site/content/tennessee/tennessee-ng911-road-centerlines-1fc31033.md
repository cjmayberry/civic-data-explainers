## What this tracks
This dataset maps every road centerline across Tennessee with precise address ranges and geographic boundaries for the state's Next Generation 911 emergency response system.

## Why it matters to you
When you call 911 in Tennessee, this data helps dispatchers locate you instantly and send help to the right address. The dataset ensures emergency responders know exactly which county, city, and zip code jurisdiction covers your location, preventing delays when seconds count. It also helps delivery services, GPS navigation, and mail carriers find addresses accurately throughout the state.

## How to read this data
**NAME** — the street name like "MERIDIAN" without prefixes or suffixes. **TYPE** — the road type such as "DR" for Drive, "ST" for Street, or "RD" for Road. **R_F_ADD** and **R_T_ADD** — the address range on the right side of the street (like 600 to 698), telling you what house numbers exist on each block. **COUNTY_L** and **COUNTY_R** — which county covers the left and right sides of the street, like "WARREN" county, important since emergency services are organized by county boundaries.

## Try it yourself
Search for your street name in the NAME field to see how your road is officially mapped in Tennessee's 911 system. Check the address ranges (R_F_ADD, R_T_ADD, L_F_ADD, L_T_ADD) to confirm your house number falls within the recorded range, and verify that your county (COUNTY_L or COUNTY_R) matches what you expect for emergency service coverage.
