## What this tracks  
This dataset records publicly accessible trails and greenways across Tennessee, including their locations, management, and features.

## Why it matters to you  
If you’re planning a hike, bike ride, or wheelchair-accessible outing in Tennessee, this data helps you choose trails that match your needs. For example, a parent with a stroller can look for **ADA_Accessible** trails with concrete surfaces, while a road cyclist can check **Secondary_Use_Trail_Type** for designated biking routes. Small businesses near trailheads, like cafes or gear shops, can also use this to identify high-traffic outdoor recreation areas.

## How to read this data  
**Trail_Name** — The official name of the trail (e.g., "Townsend North Greenway Trail").  
**Primary_Use_Trail_Type** — The main activity the trail supports, like "Greenway" or "Road-biking."  
**Length_Miles** — The trail’s distance (e.g., 4.56 miles).  
**ADA_Accessible** — Whether the trail meets accessibility standards (values like "Yes" or "No Data").  

## Try it yourself  
Search for trails near your ZIP code using the **City** or **Zip_Code** fields to plan a weekend hike or bike route.
