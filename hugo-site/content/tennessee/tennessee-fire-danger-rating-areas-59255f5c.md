## What this tracks
This dataset maps Tennessee's fire danger rating areas, which are geographic zones used by the Tennessee Division of Forestry to assess wildfire risk and coordinate emergency response.

## Why it matters to you
If you live in a rural area or own property with trees, knowing your fire danger rating area helps you understand your wildfire risk level and prepare accordingly. During dry seasons, Tennessee forestry officials issue fire danger warnings by these specific areas, so you'll know when outdoor burning is restricted or when to take extra precautions around your property. Business owners in forestry, agriculture, or outdoor recreation can use this information to plan operations and assess insurance needs based on their area's fire risk designation.

## How to read this data
**AreaNumber** — A numerical identifier for each fire danger zone, with values like 6 that correspond to specific regions across Tennessee. **AreaDescription** — The common name for each fire danger area, such as "West Tennessee Delta," which helps you identify which zone covers your location. **Acres** — The total size of each fire danger area in acres, with some areas covering over 4 million acres, showing the scale of these risk assessment zones.

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
