## What this tracks  
This dataset records individual trail access points across Tennessee, including their locations, managing entities, and basic features like surface type and allowed uses.

## Why it matters to you  
Planning a family hike? This tells you whether the Madisonville Loop has paved paths for strollers before you drive there. Running a bike shop? Check which trails near your store are designated for mountain biking to stock the right gear. The data helps Tennessee residents verify trail conditions and accessibility directly from local sources like Monroe County's tourism department.

## How to read this data  
**Trail_Name** — Official trail name, like "Madisonville Loop (scenic bikeway route)."  
**Surface** — Path material, such as "Paved" or unpaved (sample shows "Paved").  
**Primary_Use_Trail_Type** — Main activity allowed, like "Mountain Biking" in the sample.  
**Managing_Entity_Name** — Who maintains the trail, e.g., "Monroe County Government: Department of Tourism."  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for trails near your ZIP code using the **City** or **County_Name** fields to find local routes matching your preferred activity (e.g., biking or hiking).
