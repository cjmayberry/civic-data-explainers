## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the seven administrative regions where the Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency (TWRA) manages wildlife conservation and public lands.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your TWRA region helps you find the right local office for hunting/fishing licenses, report wildlife issues, or learn about conservation programs in your area. If you're planning a hunting trip in Tennessee, this tells you which region's regulations apply to your destination. Landowners near region boundaries can confirm which TWRA officers handle nuisance wildlife calls.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The official region designation (e.g., "Region 1" covers 21 counties in northeast Tennessee including Knoxville). Regions are numbered 1 through 7.  
**Shape__Area** — Technical field showing the geographic size of each region in square units (not typically useful for residents).

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
