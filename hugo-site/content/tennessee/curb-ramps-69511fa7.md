## What this tracks  
This dataset records the location and accessibility features of curb ramps across Tennessee, including slope measurements, width, and truncated dome presence.

## Why it matters to you  
If you use a wheelchair, walk with a cane, or push a stroller, this data tells you whether a specific curb ramp meets ADA standards before you plan a route. Parents can check if ramps near schools have proper tactile domes for safe crossings. Small businesses can verify if their storefront's curb cuts comply with accessibility laws to avoid fines.

## How to read this data  
**RAMP_SLOPE_PCT** — The incline of the ramp (7.7% in the sample), where values under 8.3% meet ADA standards.  
**TRUNCATED_DOME_PRESENCE** — Indicates tactile warning surfaces (value "2" in the sample means absent).  
**FEATURE_DESCRIPTION** — Describes the ramp's context like "Driveway" or crosswalk connection.  
**LOCATION_DESCRIPTION** — Positions the ramp relative to the road ("Right" in the sample).
