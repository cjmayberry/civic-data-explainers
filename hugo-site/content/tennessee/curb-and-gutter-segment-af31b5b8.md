## What this tracks
This dataset records the locations and details of curb and gutter installations along Tennessee's major roads, including interstates, state routes, and locally-maintained highways.

## Why it matters to you
If you're planning construction, landscaping, or utility work near a major road in Tennessee, this data tells you exactly where curb and gutter infrastructure exists that could affect your project. Property developers can identify which road segments already have proper drainage infrastructure when evaluating sites. The data also helps residents understand why certain road sections handle stormwater better than others during heavy rains.

## How to read this data
**COUNTY_NAME** — The Tennessee county where the curb and gutter segment is located, such as "Lawrence." **ROUTE** — The specific road number like "05694" that identifies which highway or state route contains this infrastructure. **BEG_LOG** and **END_LOG** — The starting and ending mile markers (like 0.01 to 0.057) that pinpoint exactly where along the route this curb and gutter section begins and ends. **LOC_DESC** — Whether the curb and gutter is on the "Right" or left side of the road as you travel in the increasing mile marker direction.

## Try it yourself
Look up your county name in the dataset to see which state routes and highways near you have curb and gutter infrastructure. If you live near a numbered state route, search for that route number to see exactly which mile segments have curb and gutter installations versus sections that might only have ditches or no formal drainage.
