## What this tracks  
This dataset tracks sections of Tennessee highways adopted by volunteer groups through the state's Adopt-A-Highway program.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing which groups maintain specific highway stretches helps you report litter problems more effectively to TDOT. If you regularly commute on SR050 between mile markers 17.7 and 20, you can check whether that section has an active adopter before contacting authorities about debris. Businesses near adopted stretches may also coordinate with volunteer groups for joint cleanup events.

## How to read this data  
**NBR_RTE** — The highway route number, like "SR050" for State Route 50.  
**BEGIN_LOG_MILE** and **END_LOG_MILE** — The mile markers where the adopted section starts and ends, like 17.7 to 20.  
**COUNTY** — The county number where the adopted section is located, like "41" for Sevier County.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your regular commute route has an adopted section by searching for your highway number (like SR050) in TDOT's Adopt-A-Highway map viewer.
