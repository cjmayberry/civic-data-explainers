## What this tracks
This dataset records the locations and details of designated waterfowl hunting blinds across Tennessee's Wildlife Management Areas for the 2024-2025 hunting season.

## Why it matters to you
If you're a hunter planning waterfowl season in Tennessee, this data shows you exactly where legal hunting blinds are located before you apply for permits or plan your trips. Instead of driving to a Wildlife Management Area only to find all the good spots taken or not knowing which blinds are available, you can scout locations in advance and understand the different types of blinds offered. This helps you make informed decisions about where to hunt and what type of blind reservation to request.

## How to read this data
**BLINDNUM** — the unique identifier for each blind, like "45", which you'll need when making reservations or reporting to wildlife officials. **WMA** — the specific Wildlife Management Area where the blind is located, such as "AEDC - Woods Reservoir", telling you exactly which property to visit. **Type** — describes the blind style, with values like "Draw Blind" indicating whether it's a lottery-based assignment or other allocation method. **Tier** — shows the priority level of the blind, with "1" typically indicating prime locations that may require special permits or have different rules.

## Where this leaves you  
This dataset doesn't let you look anything up by your address, but it's still a window into how the state operates. Read it to understand the scope of the program and what the official records contain; the state's own interactive map (linked on this page) shows the locations involved.
