## What this tracks  
This dataset records the structural condition of bridges across Tennessee as inspected in 2022, including ownership and age details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you regularly commute over a rural bridge in Anderson County, this data tells you whether it's rated "Poor" and might delay emergency vehicles. Small trucking businesses can check **OVERALL_CONDITION** before routing heavy loads through specific counties. Residents near aging bridges (like the 1985-built example) can anticipate construction delays when contacting their county highway agency about repairs.

## How to read this data  
**COUNTY** — Where the bridge is located, like "Anderson"  
**OVERALL_CONDITION** — Safety rating: "Good" (minor issues), "Fair" (some wear), or "Poor" (significant damage)  
**YEAR_BUILT** — Construction year (e.g., 1985), which helps estimate remaining lifespan  
**OWNER** — Managing agency, such as "COUNTY HIGHWAY AGENCY" for local reporting  

## Try it yourself  
Look up bridges on your regular commute route by searching [Tennessee's bridge map](https://www.tn.gov/tdot.html) with the **ROUTE_NUMBER** and **LOG_MILE** values from this dataset.
