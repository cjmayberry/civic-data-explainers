## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the geographic boundaries of the Ozark Aquifer beneath Kansas, as mapped by the Kansas Geological Survey.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you rely on well water in Kansas, this data tells you whether your property sits above the Ozark Aquifer — a key water source. Farmers planning irrigation systems or homeowners considering drilling a new well can check if they’re in the aquifer’s zone. Developers can also use this to assess groundwater availability before breaking ground on projects.  

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — Identifies the aquifer; the only value here is "Ozark Aquifer," confirming this dataset covers that specific underground water system.  

## Try it yourself  
Zoom to your property on [Kansas’s interactive aquifer map](https://www.kgs.ku.edu/) to see if you’re within the Ozark Aquifer’s boundaries.
