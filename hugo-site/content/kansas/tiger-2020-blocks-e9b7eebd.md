## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the boundaries and basic demographics of every census block in Kansas as of 2020, including population and housing unit counts.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a small business in Kansas, this data tells you exactly how many people live near a potential location. A daycare owner could compare blocks to find neighborhoods with more families, while a coffee shop might target blocks with higher population density. Residents can also use it to understand their neighborhood's official boundaries when discussing local issues with city planners.

## How to read this data  
**HOUSING20** — The number of residential units in this block (e.g., "4" means four homes or apartments).  
**POP20** — Total residents counted here during the 2020 Census (e.g., "13" people live in this block).  
**UR20** — Whether this is urban ("U") or rural ("R") land — blank in our sample means unclassified.  
**INTPTLAT20/INTPTLON20** — The geographic center of this block (e.g., "+38.4850033, -100.9021188").  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your Kansas address on the [Census Geocoder](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/) to find your block code, then check how many neighbors you have in this dataset.
