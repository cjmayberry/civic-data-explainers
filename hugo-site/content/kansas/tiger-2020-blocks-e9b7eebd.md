## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the boundaries and basic demographic data for census blocks in Kansas as of 2020, including population and housing unit counts.  

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your census block helps you verify neighborhood-level data used for school funding, infrastructure projects, and emergency services in Kansas. A small business owner could use this to check whether their location falls in an urban or rural zone for grant eligibility, while residents might cross-reference population density when advocating for traffic calming measures on their street.  

## How to read this data  
**HOUSING20** — Total housing units in this block (e.g., "4" means four homes or apartments exist here).  
**POP20** — Total residents counted here in 2020 (e.g., "13" people live in this block).  
**UR20** — Urban ("U") or rural ("R") designation, which affects services and regulations (sample value is blank in this case).  
**INTPTLAT20/INTPTLON20** — Approximate center point of the block (e.g., "+38.4850033, -100.9021188" places this block in western Kansas).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your census block using the GEOID20 code (like "201719571005081") to see how many neighbors share your designated area for local planning purposes.
