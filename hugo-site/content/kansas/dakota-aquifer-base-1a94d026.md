## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the elevation and water quality characteristics of the Dakota Aquifer beneath Kansas, including depth and total dissolved solids in groundwater.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're drilling a well or planning agricultural irrigation in Kansas, this data tells you how deep you'll need to drill to reach usable water and whether the water may contain high mineral content that could damage equipment or affect crop yields. Developers can use it to assess groundwater availability before building in rural areas.

## How to read this data  
**ALTITUDE_F** — The elevation (in feet above sea level) where the aquifer begins, like "1300" meaning the water table starts 1,300 feet above mean sea level at that location.  
**DK_BASE_** — A code identifying different sections of the aquifer base, with values like "54" representing specific geological formations.
