## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the boundaries of Kansas state judicial districts as of 2015.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're filing a lawsuit or contesting a traffic ticket in Kansas, this data tells you which courthouse handles cases from your address. A business owner disputing a state tax assessment would use this to confirm they're filing in the correct district. Knowing your judicial district also helps when researching local judges' rulings or election campaigns for district attorney.

## How to read this data  
**REGION** — A number (like 3) indicating which judicial district a location falls under. Each number corresponds to specific counties served by that district's courts.  

## Try it yourself  
Check which judicial district covers your Kansas address by matching your county to the district map at [Kansas DASC](https://hub.kansasgis.org/).
