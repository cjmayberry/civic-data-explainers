## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the geographic boundaries of Kansas state judicial districts as of 2015.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're involved in a court case in Kansas, this data tells you which district handles your address—affecting where you file paperwork, attend hearings, or serve jury duty. Business owners can verify which district's courts would handle commercial disputes at their location. Residents can check which district's judges and prosecutors serve their neighborhood.

## How to read this data  
**REGION** — The numbered judicial district (e.g., "3" for the 3rd Judicial District covering Shawnee County). These numbers correspond to courthouse locations and administrative offices.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your county on the Kansas judicial branch website to see which REGION number applies to your address, then verify the district boundaries in this dataset.
