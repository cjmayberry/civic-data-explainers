## What this tracks  
This dataset records Kansas tax unit boundaries for property valuation and taxation purposes, certified annually by county and state officials.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own property in Kansas, this data determines which tax district your land falls under—affecting your property tax rates and school funding allocations. Small business owners can verify they're being taxed correctly by comparing their location to these boundaries. Homebuyers can check which tax unit a prospective property belongs to before purchasing.

## How to read this data  
**TAXUNIT** — The local tax district identifier (e.g., "060" for a specific area in Saline County).  
**COUNTY** — Which Kansas county the tax unit belongs to (e.g., "Saline County").  
**CERT_DATE** — When the boundary was officially approved (e.g., "03/15/2024").  
**REPORT2024** — Indicates if changes were made to this unit for the current tax year (e.g., "Changes").  

## Try it yourself  
Contact your county clerk's office with your property address to request the exact tax unit boundaries that apply to your home or business.
