## What this tracks  
This dataset identifies Kansas streams with water quality issues and lists specific pollutants found in each waterway.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're fishing in Kansas streams, this data tells you whether mercury or other contaminants might be present in your favorite fishing spot. Farmers can check if nearby waterways have fertilizer runoff issues (nitrate or phosphorus levels) that might affect irrigation plans. Homeowners near streams can see if E. coli contamination could impact recreational use of local waters.

## How to read this data  
**STREAM_NAME** — Identifies the waterway, with values like "Unnamed Stream" or specific creek names.  
**ATRAZINE** — Shows herbicide levels (sample value "2025" indicates measured concentration).  
**E_COLI** — Indicates presence of harmful bacteria when populated (blank means not detected).  
**TOTAL_PHOSPHORUS** — Measures nutrient pollution that can cause algal blooms.  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your local fishing spot or neighborhood creek appears in the dataset by searching for its name in the STREAM_NAME field.
