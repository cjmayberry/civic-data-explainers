## What this tracks  
This dataset records water quality issues in Kansas streams, listing pollutants like atrazine, lead, and E. coli that exceed safe levels under the Clean Water Act.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering fishing, swimming, or letting pets drink from a local stream, this data tells you which waterways have known contamination. Farmers can check if nearby streams have fertilizer-related pollutants before planning crop irrigation. Kansas residents can also use this to advocate for cleanup efforts in their watershed.  

## How to read this data  
**STREAM_NAME** — Identifies the waterway (e.g., "Unnamed Stream").  
**ATRAZINE** — Shows herbicide levels (sample value "2025" indicates measured concentration).  
**E_COLI** — Blank in samples means no exceedance, but populated fields would warn of bacterial contamination.  
**LEAD** — If populated, indicates dangerous heavy metal levels in the water.  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your nearest stream by name in the dataset to see if it has active pollution warnings before your next fishing trip.
