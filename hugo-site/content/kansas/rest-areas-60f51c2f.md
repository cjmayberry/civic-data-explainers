## What this tracks  
This dataset lists rest areas along Kansas state highways, including their amenities and exact locations.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a road trip through Kansas, this data helps you find safe places to rest without detouring off the highway. Truck drivers can verify which stops offer trailer dump stations before committing to a route. Families can identify rest areas with picnic tables and shade for meal breaks. Knowing ADA accessibility in advance is crucial for travelers with mobility needs.

## How to read this data  
**Route** — The highway where the rest area is located (e.g., "I-70").  
**LocationDescription** — Specific placement relative to landmarks (e.g., "4 MI WEST OF K-177").  
**Restroom** — "Y" indicates available restrooms, "N" means none.  
**TruckParking** — Number of dedicated semi-truck spaces (e.g., 14).  

## Try it yourself  
Before your next drive across Kansas, [visit KDOT's map](https://www.ksdot.gov) to find rest areas along your planned route that match your needs for parking, restrooms, or ADA access.
