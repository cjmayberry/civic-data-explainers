## What this tracks  
This dataset maps all state highways and roads in Kansas, including Interstates, U.S. routes, and Kansas-numbered routes.

## Why it matters to you  
If you’re planning a road trip or shipping goods across Kansas, this data tells you exactly which routes are state-maintained — meaning they’re more likely to be plowed quickly in winter or have timely pothole repairs. Trucking companies can verify state routes to avoid county-maintained roads with weight restrictions. Residents near routes like **us_routes=50** can check if their commute is on a priority snow-clearing corridor.

## How to read this data  
**us_routes** — The U.S. highway number (e.g., "50" for U.S. Route 50 running through western Kansas).  
**state_routes** — The Kansas state highway number (e.g., "4" for K-4 near Topeka).  
**FromMeasure/ToMeasure** — Milepost markers showing where a specific route segment begins and ends (e.g., 8.1 to 31.07 miles along a route).

## Try it yourself  
Look up your nearest state highway by searching [Kansas DOT’s traveler map](https://www.kandrive.org/) with the **us_routes** or **state_routes** numbers from this dataset.
