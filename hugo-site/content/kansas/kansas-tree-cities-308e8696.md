## What this tracks  
This dataset tracks Kansas communities certified as Tree Cities by the Arbor Day Foundation, including their tree maintenance efforts and budgets.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're choosing where to buy a home in Kansas, this tells you which communities actively invest in green spaces — Beloit spent $34,849 on trees last year. Business owners can see whether a town prioritizes aesthetics (like 16 new trees planted) before opening a storefront. The Growth Award flag shows which cities go beyond minimum requirements.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The certified community (e.g., Beloit).  
**DollarsExp** — Annual tree care budget (e.g., $34,849).  
**Planted/Pruned/Removed** — Annual tree changes (e.g., 16 planted, 15 pruned, 7 removed).  
**GrowthAwar** — Whether the city earned extra recognition (values like "No" or "Yes").

## Try it yourself  
Check if your Kansas community is listed by searching the [Arbor Day Foundation's Tree City map](https://www.arborday.org/programs/treecityusa/) with your town name.
