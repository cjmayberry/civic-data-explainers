## What this tracks  
This dataset lists Kansas communities certified as Tree Cities by the Arbor Day Foundation, along with their tree maintenance activities and budgets.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're choosing where to live in Kansas, this data shows which towns prioritize tree care—affecting shade, air quality, and property values. Business owners can gauge municipal investment in green infrastructure, which influences customer appeal and stormwater management costs. The Growth Award flag highlights communities going beyond basic requirements.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The certified community (e.g., "Beloit").  
**DollarsExp** — Annual budget for tree care (e.g., "$34,849").  
**Planted/Pruned/Removed** — Annual tree maintenance counts (e.g., "16 planted, 15 pruned, 7 removed").  
**GrowthAwar** — Whether the community earned extra recognition ("Yes" or "No").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your Kansas community is listed by searching the dataset for your town's name at [Arbor Day Foundation Tree City](https://www.arborday.org/programs/treecityusa/).
