## What this tracks  
This dataset records the administrative boundaries for Kansas state government departments, divided into numbered regions.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're applying for state services like business licenses or permits in Kansas, knowing your lottery region helps you identify which local office handles your application. A restaurant owner in Wichita could check this data to confirm whether they should contact Region 3 or Region 4 for health inspections, avoiding delays from filing paperwork with the wrong office.

## How to read this data  
**REGION** — A number (like 3) identifying which administrative area a location falls under. Different state services use these same regional boundaries.  

**SHAPE__Area** and **SHAPE__Length** are technical GIS measurements not meaningful for most users.  

## Try it yourself  
Visit Kansas's [geospatial catalog](https://hub.kansasgis.org/) to see if your business address falls in Region 1 (Northeast), Region 3 (South Central), or another lottery region when preparing state paperwork.
