## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the administrative boundaries used by Kansas state departments for lottery operations.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're applying for a lottery retailer license in Kansas, knowing your region helps you contact the correct state office for approvals and compliance checks. Residents checking lottery claim procedures can confirm which regional office handles their winning ticket based on where it was purchased.  

## How to read this data  
**REGION** — A numbered zone (e.g., `3`) designating which Kansas Lottery administrative office oversees a geographic area. Values range from 1 to 6, with each corresponding to a physical office location.  

## Try it yourself  
Check which lottery region your business address falls under by cross-referencing this map with Kansas Lottery's regional office contacts (https://www.kslottery.com).
