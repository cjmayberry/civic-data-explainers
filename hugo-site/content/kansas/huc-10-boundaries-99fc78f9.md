## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Kansas' watershed boundaries down to the 10-digit hydrologic unit code (HUC) level, defining small sub-watersheds like creeks and tributaries.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you own farmland, this tells you exactly which watershed your property sits in—critical for understanding flood risks or water runoff patterns affecting your crops. Developers in Kansas can check which waterways their construction projects might impact, avoiding costly environmental violations. Homebuyers near water bodies can identify potential flood zones not obvious on standard maps.  

## How to read this data  
**HUC_10** — A 10-digit code uniquely identifying a sub-watershed (e.g., `1024000509` for Little Tarkio Creek).  
**HU_10_NAME** — The watershed’s common name (e.g., "Little Tarkio Creek-Missouri River").  
**ACRES** — The watershed’s size in acres (e.g., `14077` acres), helping gauge its scale.  

## Try it yourself  
Search [Kansas DASC’s map viewer](https://hub.kansasgis.org/) with your address to see if your property falls within a HUC-10 boundary, especially if you’re near a creek or river.
