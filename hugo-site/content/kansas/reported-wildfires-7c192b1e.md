## What this tracks  
This dataset records wildfires reported in Kansas, including their location, size, cause, and impact on structures and resources.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying property in rural Kansas, this data shows which counties have frequent wildfires and whether homes are typically at risk. Business owners can check if their supply chain runs through high-risk areas during fire season. The **EstimatedCost** field reveals how much damage recent fires caused, helping you assess insurance needs.

## How to read this data  
**County** — Two-letter code for where the fire occurred (e.g., "BU" for Butler County).  
**Acres** — Size of burned area (e.g., "2190" means 2,190 acres burned).  
**Cause** — Single-letter code for fire origin (e.g., "U" means undetermined).  
**RedFlagWarning** — "Y" means conditions were extreme when the fire started.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the **County** field for wildfires near your property by searching [Kansas Forest Service's map](https://www.kansasforests.org/).
