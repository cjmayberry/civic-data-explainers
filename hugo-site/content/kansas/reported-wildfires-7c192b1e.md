## What this tracks  
This dataset records wildfires reported in Kansas, including their location, size, cause, and impact on structures and resources.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying property in rural Kansas, this data tells you whether the area has a history of wildfires and how severe they’ve been. For farmers or landowners, it shows which counties face higher fire risks, helping you decide whether to invest in firebreaks or additional insurance. Businesses in wildfire-prone areas can use this to assess evacuation plans or fireproofing needs.  

## How to read this data  
**County** — The Kansas county where the fire occurred (e.g., "BU" for Butler County).  
**Acres** — The size of the fire in acres (e.g., "2190" means over 2,000 acres burned).  
**Cause** — The reason the fire started, with codes like "U" for unknown or other descriptors (e.g., "Comments" may clarify with "Unknown Cause").  
**Structures** — The number of buildings damaged (e.g., "2" means two structures were affected).  

## Try it yourself  
Check the [Kansas Forest Service website](https://www.kansasforests.org/) to see if your county has recent wildfire activity by searching the dataset for your area.
