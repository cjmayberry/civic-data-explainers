## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the shoreline wetlands around Marion Lake in Marion County, Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own lakeside property near Marion Lake, this data shows exactly where protected wetlands begin, which affects where you can build docks or clear vegetation. Farmers can check if their fields border these wetlands before applying fertilizers that might runoff into protected areas. Anglers and kayakers can see how much wetland habitat exists for fish and wildlife.
