## What this tracks  
This dataset estimates the Annual Average Daily Traffic (AADT) on Kansas roads, including vehicle types like cars, trucks, and motorcycles.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a café or retail shop, this data tells you how many potential customers drive past a location daily. Residents can check traffic volume before buying a home to avoid high-congestion areas. Trucking companies can plan routes by avoiding roads with heavy commercial traffic.  

## How to read this data  
**AADTCount** — The total average daily vehicles passing a road segment, like 1744 cars per day.  
**CombinationTruck** — Heavy trucks (e.g., 18-wheelers) on the road, such as 486 per day.  
**SingleUnitTruck** — Smaller commercial trucks, like box trucks, with sample values like 46 daily.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your street or a business location to see how many vehicles pass by—helpful for estimating foot traffic or noise levels.
