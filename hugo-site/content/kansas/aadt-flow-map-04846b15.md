## What this tracks  
This dataset estimates the Annual Average Daily Traffic (AADT) on Kansas roads, including vehicle types like trucks and motorcycles.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a café or retail shop in Kansas, this data tells you how many potential customers drive past a location daily. Homebuyers can check traffic volume near a property to gauge noise levels. Delivery businesses can identify high-truck routes to optimize schedules. The numbers reveal which roads handle the most commuters during peak hours.

## How to read this data  
**AADTCount** — Total vehicles per day (e.g., 1744 means ~1,744 cars/trucks pass daily).  
**CombinationTruck** — Semi-trucks with trailers (e.g., 486 means ~486 heavy trucks daily).  
**SingleUnitTruck** — Delivery trucks or box trucks (e.g., 46 means ~46 medium trucks daily).  
**AADTCountYear** — The year counted (e.g., 2025 means data reflects 2024 traffic patterns).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your street in Kansas' traffic map to see if your commute route has over 2,000 daily vehicles—a threshold where congestion often starts.
