## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the top elevation of the Ozark Aquifer, a key groundwater source in Kansas, showing where it lies beneath the surface.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're drilling a well in Kansas, this data tells you how deep you'll need to dig to reach the Ozark Aquifer's water supply. Farmers can use it to estimate irrigation well costs, and homeowners can check if their property sits above this critical water source before planning a new well. Developers can also assess groundwater availability for new projects.

## How to read this data  
**FEET** — The elevation (in feet) of the aquifer's top surface relative to sea level. Sample value: -9999 indicates missing data.  
**CODE** — A numeric identifier for specific aquifer segments. Sample value: 222 corresponds to a mapped section.
