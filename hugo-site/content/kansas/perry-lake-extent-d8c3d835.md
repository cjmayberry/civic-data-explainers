## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the shoreline wetlands around Perry Lake in Jefferson County, Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a fishing trip, kayaking outing, or birdwatching visit to Perry Lake, this data shows where the wetland areas are—key spots for wildlife and calm waters. Homeowners near the lake can also use it to understand how shoreline changes might affect their property over time. For small businesses offering lake tours or rentals, it helps identify prime launch points or scenic routes.

## How to read this data  
**gridcode** — Indicates wetland type (sample value: 1). Higher numbers might differentiate marsh, swamp, or other habitats if the full metadata is available.  
**area_sqm** — The size of each wetland area in square meters (sample: 42,883,318). Larger areas likely support more diverse ecosystems.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the map before your next lakeside hike to see if your preferred trailhead borders a wetland area (which may have more mosquitoes or waterfowl).
