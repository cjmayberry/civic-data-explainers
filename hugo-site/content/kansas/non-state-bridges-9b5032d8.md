## What this tracks  
This dataset records inspection and structural details for non-state-owned bridges in Kansas that are longer than 20 feet, including ownership, condition ratings, and traffic data.

## Why it matters to you  
If you’re planning a delivery route for your small business, this data tells you whether a rural bridge can handle heavy trucks (check **POSTING** for weight limits). Residents can see when their local bridge was last inspected (**LASTINSP**) and its condition rating (**SUFF_RATE**) to anticipate potential closures or repairs. In Crawford County, for example, one bridge scored 97/100 for structural sufficiency, while others may show deterioration needing attention.

## How to read this data  
**SUFF_RATE** — A 0-100 score of the bridge’s structural adequacy (sample: 97.0 means excellent condition).  
**POSTING** — Weight limits like "At/Above Legal Loads" or restrictions for trucks.  
**LASTINSP** — Date of the most recent inspection (sample: 1646092800000 converts to March 2022).  
**LOCATION** — Relative position (sample: "2.8S 1.0E OF ARMA" means 2.8 miles south and 1 mile east of Arma).

## Try it yourself  
Check the **SUFF_RATE** and **POSTING** fields for bridges on your regular routes using the [KDOT Bridge Map](https://www.ksdot.gov/bureaus/burLocalProj/Bridge.asp) to avoid weight-restricted or poorly rated crossings.
