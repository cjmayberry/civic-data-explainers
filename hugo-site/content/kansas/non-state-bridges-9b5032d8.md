## What this tracks  
This dataset records inspection details and structural information for bridges longer than 20 feet that are owned and maintained by local authorities in Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you frequently drive over rural bridges in Kansas, this data tells you their condition ratings, weight limits, and when they were last inspected. For example, a trucking company could check **POSTING** values to avoid routes with weight-restricted bridges. Farmers hauling equipment can use **SUFF_RATE** (safety rating) to identify potentially risky crossings before planning harvest routes.

## How to read this data  
**SUFF_RATE** — Safety rating from 0-100 (sample: 97.0), where higher is better.  
**POSTING** — Weight limits like "Posted for load" (sample) or restrictions.  
**LASTINSP** — Date of last inspection (sample: 1646092800000 = March 2022).  
**LOCATION** — Bridge position like "2.8S 1.0E OF ARMA" (sample).  

## Try it yourself  
Check the [Kansas DOT Bridge Viewer](https://www.ksdot.gov/bureaus/burLocalProj/bridgeinventory.asp) to find inspection dates for bridges on your regular routes.
