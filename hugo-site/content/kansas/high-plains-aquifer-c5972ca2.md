## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries and characteristics of the High Plains Aquifer system in Kansas, including the Ogallala, Great Bend, and Equus Beds aquifers.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you rely on well water for your home or farm in Kansas, this data shows whether your property sits above the aquifer—and how much groundwater might be available. A business owner drilling a new irrigation well could check this to estimate water access before investing in equipment. Drought-prone areas may also use this to plan conservation efforts.  

## How to read this data  
**DESCRIPTION** — Classifies aquifer extent (e.g., "little" in samples), indicating how much of the area contains usable groundwater.  
**PERIMETER** — Measures the boundary length of aquifer sections (e.g., 364,911.4 feet), helping gauge the scale of water resources near your land.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the Kansas Geological Survey’s interactive map to see if your property overlaps with the aquifer’s "little" or more substantial sections.
