## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries and characteristics of the High Plains Aquifer system in Kansas, including the Ogallala, Great Bend, and Equus Beds aquifers.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you rely on well water for your home, farm, or business in Kansas, this data shows whether your property sits above these critical groundwater sources. Knowing your aquifer’s extent helps you anticipate water availability for irrigation or drinking—especially during droughts. Farmers can use it to plan crop rotations where groundwater depletion is a risk.  

## How to read this data  
**DESCRIPTION** — Indicates aquifer size or coverage (e.g., "little" in samples). Values classify areas by aquifer presence or significance.  
**HP_EXTENT_** — A numeric code (e.g., "2") marking aquifer boundaries; higher values may indicate more substantial groundwater resources.
