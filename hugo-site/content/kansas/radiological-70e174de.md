## What this tracks  
This dataset records radiological testing results from groundwater samples taken from public and private wells across Kansas between 1985 and 2001.

## Why it matters to you  
If you rely on well water in Kansas, this data shows historical radiation levels in your area’s groundwater. High uranium or radon levels could affect decisions like installing water filtration systems or testing your current well. For example, a resident near Butler County (listed as "BU" in the data) might prioritize radon mitigation if their well’s historical readings were elevated.

## How to read this data  
**Gross_Uranium_pCi/L** — Measures uranium concentration in picocuries per liter; sample value "15" indicates higher-than-typical levels (EPA’s maximum contaminant level is 30 pCi/L).  
**Radon-222_pCi/L** — Shows radon gas levels; sample value "685" far exceeds the EPA’s recommended action level of 300 pCi/L.  
**COUNTY** — Two-letter county code (e.g., "BU" for Butler); helps locate results geographically.
