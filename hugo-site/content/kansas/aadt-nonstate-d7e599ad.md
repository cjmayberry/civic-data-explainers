## What this tracks  
This dataset estimates the Annual Average Daily Traffic (AADT) on city and county roads in Kansas, KS, showing how many vehicles typically pass through specific road segments each day.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a business, this data tells you how much foot or car traffic a potential location gets—helping you pick a spot with higher visibility. Residents can use it to gauge how busy their commute might be or whether their street is a high-traffic shortcut. For example, knowing the AADT for 12th Street could help you decide whether to avoid it during rush hour.  

## How to read this data  
**AADTCount** — The estimated total daily vehicles (e.g., 716.0 means ~716 cars/day).  
**AADTCountYear** — The year the count was recorded (e.g., 2025).  
**RouteID** — A unique identifier for the road segment (e.g., 0015000010000000).  

## Try it yourself  
Check the AADT for your regular commute route in Kansas’s open data portal to see if traffic volumes justify leaving earlier or taking an alternate route.
