## What this tracks  
This dataset estimates the Annual Average Daily Traffic (AADT) on city and county roads in Kansas, measuring how many vehicles, trucks, and other road users pass through specific segments annually.

## Why it matters to you  
If you’re considering opening a business, this data tells you how many potential customers drive past a location daily. Residents can use it to compare traffic volumes when choosing between alternate routes for their commute or evaluating noise levels before moving to a new neighborhood. For example, a street with an AADT of 5,000 will feel very different from one with 500.

## How to read this data  
**AADTCount** — The estimated total vehicles per day (e.g., 716.0 means ~716 cars pass that spot daily).  
**AADTCountYear** — The year the count was taken (e.g., 2025).  
**RouteID** — A unique identifier for the road segment (e.g., 0015000010000000).  
**FromMeasure/ToMeasure** — The start and end points of the counted segment in miles (e.g., 4.048664 to 6.06033 means the count covers a 2-mile stretch).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up the AADT for your regular commute route to see if there’s a quieter alternative with lower traffic numbers.
