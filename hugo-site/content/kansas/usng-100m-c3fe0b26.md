## What this tracks  
This dataset provides US National Grid (USNG) 100-meter grid references for locations across Kansas, creating a standardized way to identify any point in the state.

## Why it matters to you  
During emergencies like floods or wildfires, first responders use USNG coordinates to locate people in distress. If you know your USNG grid square, you can quickly share your exact location with 911 operators when every second counts. Kansas residents in rural areas especially benefit from this system when traditional addresses (like "mile marker 12 on County Road 5") are ambiguous.

## How to read this data  
**GRID1MIL** — The broadest grid division (1,000,000m squares) covering Kansas. Sample value "01G" refers to a specific million-meter grid zone that contains multiple 100m grids.  

While the schema includes technical fields for GIS systems, the core value is that any Kansas location can be precisely described with a USNG address like "01G AB 1234 5678" (100m resolution). Emergency services and outdoor enthusiasts use this format.

## Try it yourself  
Next time you're hiking or checking your property boundaries, practice finding your USNG 100m grid reference using free tools like the USNG App or the official converter at usngcenter.org. Store it in your phone for emergencies.
