## What this tracks  
This dataset provides US National Grid (USNG) 100-meter grid references across Kansas for emergency response coordination.

## Why it matters to you  
When reporting an emergency in rural Kansas, giving responders your USNG location could save critical minutes compared to describing landmarks. Hunters and outdoor enthusiasts can note their USNG grid square before venturing into areas without street addresses. The Kansas Adjutant General's Emergency Management Department uses this system to coordinate disaster response across jurisdictions.

## How to read this data  
**GRID1MIL** — Identifies your 1,000,000-meter grid zone (like "01G" for parts of Kansas). This first-level reference helps narrow down locations to a broad region.  

The full USNG address combines multiple grid levels (like "01G AB 123 456") to pinpoint locations within 100 meters - crucial for emergency services finding remote properties or accident sites.

## Try it yourself  
Next time you're in an area without clear addresses, use a USNG-compatible map app to note your 100-meter grid square (like "01G AB 123 456") and share it with your hiking group or family for safety.
