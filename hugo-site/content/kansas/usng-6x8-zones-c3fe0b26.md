## What this tracks  
This dataset defines Kansas' US National Grid (USNG) 6x8 zones, a standardized geographic reference system used for emergency response and mapping.

## Why it matters to you  
When reporting an emergency in rural Kansas, first responders use USNG coordinates to locate you faster than street addresses. If you operate a farm or business outside city limits, knowing your USNG zone helps you communicate your exact location during 911 calls or disaster response. Hunters and outdoor recreationists can also use these zones to share precise wilderness locations.

## How to read this data  
**GRID1MIL** — Identifies your 1,000,000-meter grid zone (like "01G"), the broadest regional division. These alphanumeric codes divide Kansas into large, consistent sections for statewide coordination.  

## Try it yourself  
Find your property's USNG zone by cross-referencing this grid with your physical location on Kansas' emergency management maps at www.kansasgis.org.
