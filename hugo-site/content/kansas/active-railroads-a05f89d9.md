## What this tracks  
This dataset maps active railroad lines across Kansas, including ownership, classification, and operational details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying property near train tracks in Kansas, this data tells you whether the line is actively used (and how heavily) before you commit. Business owners shipping goods can identify which rail companies serve their area and whether tracks are part of major freight corridors like Union Pacific's Herington line. Homebuyers in Topeka or Wichita can check if that "occasional" train whistle might become a daily disruption.

## How to read this data  
**RailroadOwner** — The operating company (e.g., "UP" for Union Pacific).  
**Active** — Whether the line is in use (-1 typically means yes in GIS systems).  
**Class** — Railroad classification (e.g., "I" for major freight carriers like BNSF).  
**SubdivisionLogmile** — Precise location markers along tracks (e.g., "3.7" means 3.7 miles from the subdivision's start).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your street runs parallel to an active rail line by cross-referencing the subdivision names (like HERINGTON_I2) with visible tracks near you.
