## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the administrative boundaries for Kansas state parole regions.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you have a family member in the Kansas corrections system, knowing which parole office serves your area helps you find local support services and understand which caseworkers oversee their transition. Businesses near correctional facilities can also check which region they fall under to anticipate workforce availability from parolee job placement programs.  

## How to read this data  
**REGION** — A number (e.g., `3`) indicating which parole region a location belongs to. Contact the Kansas Department of Corrections to find the office address and services for your region.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the [Kansas geospatial catalog](https://hub.kansasgis.org/) to see which parole region covers your neighborhood.
