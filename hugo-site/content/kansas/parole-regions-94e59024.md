## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the administrative boundaries for Kansas state parole regions.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're contacting the Kansas Department of Corrections about a parolee, knowing which region handles your area ensures your inquiry reaches the right office faster. Families checking visitation rules or employers verifying parole work restrictions would use this to identify their local parole administration.

## How to read this data  
**REGION** — The numbered parole administration area (e.g., "3" for Region 3). These regions determine which state office manages parole cases in your part of Kansas.  

## Try it yourself  
Check which parole region covers your county by cross-referencing this map with your location: [Kansas Parole Regions](https://hub.kansasgis.org/).
