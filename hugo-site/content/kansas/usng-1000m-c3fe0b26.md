## What this tracks  
This dataset provides a standardized grid reference system (US National Grid) for locating points across Kansas at a 1,000-meter resolution.

## Why it matters to you  
During emergencies like floods or severe storms, responders use USNG codes to coordinate rescues and resource deployment. If you know your area’s grid code (e.g., "01G"), you can share it with emergency services for faster assistance. Kansas residents can also use these codes to interpret disaster alerts or evacuation zones more precisely.

## How to read this data  
**GRID1MIL** — A 3-character code (like "01G") representing a 1,000m x 1,000m grid square. The first two digits denote the grid zone, while the letter narrows it to a specific block.
