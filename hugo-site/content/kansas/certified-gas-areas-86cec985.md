## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries of areas in Kansas certified for natural gas service by specific utility companies.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're moving to a new home or business location in Kansas, this data tells you whether natural gas service is available at that address — and which utility company provides it. Knowing this upfront helps you compare energy costs, avoid properties where gas isn't an option, and contact the right provider for hookups. For businesses like restaurants that rely on gas appliances, this could be a deciding factor in choosing a location.  

## How to read this data  
**Company_Na** — Lists the gas utility company serving the area (e.g., "MIDWEST ENERGY, INC.").  
**Status** — Shows if service is currently "Active" or inactive in the zone.  
**Company_ID** — A shorter code representing the provider (e.g., "MDWG" for Midwest Energy).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your Kansas address falls within an "Active" certified gas area by viewing the map at [Kansas GIS Hub](https://hub.kansasgis.org/) and searching your neighborhood.
