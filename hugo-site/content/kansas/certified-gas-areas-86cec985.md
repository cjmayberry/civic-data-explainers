## What this tracks  
This dataset maps areas in Kansas where gas service is certified and provided by specific utility companies.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're moving to a new home or business location in Kansas, this data tells you which gas company serves that area—so you know who to contact for service hookups or outages. For business owners considering a location, it reveals whether Midwest Energy or another provider operates there, which could impact utility costs. Renters can verify if a landlord's claim about available gas service matches the official records.

## How to read this data  
**Company_Na** — The gas utility company name for the area (e.g., "MIDWEST ENERGY, INC.").  
**Status** — Whether the certification is currently active (sample value: "Active").  
**Company_ID** — Short code identifying the provider (sample: "MDWG").  

## Try it yourself  
Check which gas company serves your neighborhood by searching the Kansas geospatial catalog at [hub.kansasgis.org](https://hub.kansasgis.org/) with your address.
