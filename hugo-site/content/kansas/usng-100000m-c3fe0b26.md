## What this tracks  
This dataset provides the US National Grid (USNG) 100,000-meter grid reference system for Kansas, used for emergency response and mapping.

## Why it matters to you  
If you’re a Kansas resident planning outdoor activities like hiking or hunting, this grid system helps first responders locate you quickly in an emergency. Small business owners in rural areas can also use it to verify their precise location for deliveries or emergency services. The Kansas Adjutant General’s Emergency Management Department relies on this data for coordinating disaster response.

## How to read this data  
**GRID1MIL** — Identifies the 1,000,000-meter grid square (e.g., "01G"), which covers a large region. This high-level reference helps narrow down areas during statewide emergencies or large-scale operations.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the grid square for your area by cross-referencing the USNG with a local map or GPS tool to see how emergency services would identify your location.
