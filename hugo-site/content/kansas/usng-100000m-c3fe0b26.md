## What this tracks  
This dataset defines Kansas' grid locations using the U.S. National Grid (USNG) system, which provides standardized geographic references for emergency response.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're reporting an emergency in rural Kansas, responders may ask for your USNG location instead of a street address. Knowing how to find your USNG grid coordinates (like those on topographic maps or GPS devices) could save critical minutes during wildfires, floods, or medical emergencies. Hunters and hikers can also use this to share precise off-road locations with park rangers.

## How to read this data  
**GRID1MIL** — Identifies the 1,000,000-meter grid square (e.g., "01G" covers most of Kansas). These large grid zones help first responders narrow search areas before using more precise coordinates.  

## Try it yourself  
Next time you're outdoors, check if your GPS device or mapping app shows USNG coordinates alongside latitude/longitude—this is the format Kansas emergency services prefer for wilderness locations.
