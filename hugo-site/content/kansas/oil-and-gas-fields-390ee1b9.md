## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries and key details of active and inactive oil and gas fields across Kansas.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you own land in Kansas, this data tells you whether your property sits on or near an active oil or gas field, which could affect leasing opportunities or environmental considerations. For small businesses in energy services, it reveals where active extraction is happening, helping target equipment sales or maintenance contracts. Residents can also check whether nearby fields are still producing or have been inactive for years.  

## How to read this data  
**FIELD_NAME** — The official name of the oil/gas field (e.g., "FOREST CITY COAL GAS AREA").  
**STATUS** — Whether the field is "Active" or inactive, indicating current production.  
**FIELD_TYPE** — Specifies if the field produces "Gas," "Oil," or both (sample: "Gas").  
**LASTODATE/LASTGDATE** — The most recent month/year oil or gas was produced (e.g., "6-2018").  

## Try it yourself  
Search the map for your county or town to see if active oil/gas fields exist nearby, and check their **STATUS** to gauge local industry activity.
