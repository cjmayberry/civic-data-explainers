## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries and production details of active and inactive oil and gas fields across Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying land in Kansas, this data reveals whether the property sits on a historic or active oil/gas field, which could impact land use or future drilling rights. For small businesses in agriculture or construction, knowing nearby field activity helps assess potential disruptions from extraction equipment. The **STATUS** field tells you if operations are ongoing ("Active") or closed, while **FIELD_TYPE** clarifies whether the site produces oil, gas, or both.

## How to read this data  
**FIELD_NAME** — Official name of the oil/gas field (e.g., "FOREST CITY COAL GAS AREA").  
**STATUS** — Current operational state: "Active" means recent production, while inactive fields may still have infrastructure.  
**LASTGDATE** / **LASTODATE** — Most recent month/year of gas or oil production (e.g., "4-2018" for gas in the sample).  
**AVGDEPTH** — Typical well depth in feet (e.g., 922 ft), indicating how deep drilling occurs.

## Try it yourself  
Search the [Kansas Geological Survey’s interactive map](https://www.kgs.ku.edu/) for your county to see if nearby fields are active—look for "FIELD_TYPE: Gas" if you’re researching natural gas potential.
