## What this tracks  
This dataset measures pollution levels and water quality issues in Kansas lakes that are considered impaired under the Clean Water Act.

## Why it matters to you  
If you fish at Norton Lake, the **DISSOLVED_OXYGEN** field tells you whether the water has enough oxygen to support healthy fish populations. A family planning to swim at a lake can check the **EUTROPHICATION** field to see if harmful algal blooms are present. Farmers near impaired waterways can use this data to adjust fertilizer use when **TOTAL_PHOSPHORUS** levels are high.

## How to read this data  
**LAKE_NAME** — Official lake names, like "Norton Lake (Sebelius Lake)", where water quality is monitored.  
**DISSOLVED_OXYGEN** — Measures oxygen levels critical for aquatic life; sample value "Low" indicates poor conditions.  
**EUTROPHICATION** — Rates nutrient pollution effects like algal blooms; "High" means severe impacts.  
**APURL** — Links to detailed reports, like "https://maps.kdhe.state.ks.us/tmdlreports/mo/BrownCoSFL.pdf" for specific pollution solutions.

## Try it yourself  
Search for your favorite Kansas fishing or swimming lake by name to see its current pollution concerns and access the full water quality report.
