## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the locations and basic information of all public K-12 schools in Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're moving to a new neighborhood in Kansas, this data helps you identify nearby schools for your children before choosing a home. Small business owners can use it to see which schools might drive foot traffic near potential retail locations. Families can also verify school addresses when registering children or planning carpools.

## How to read this data  
**School** — The name of the educational institution, like "Elkhart Elem".  
**Address** — The physical street address where the school is located, such as "331 S Cosmos".  
**City** — The municipality where the school operates, for example "Elkhart".  
**Zip** — The postal code for mailing purposes, shown as numbers like 67950.

## Try it yourself  
Look up your nearest elementary school by searching the dataset for schools in your ZIP code or city.
