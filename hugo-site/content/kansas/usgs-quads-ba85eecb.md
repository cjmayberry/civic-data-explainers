## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries and details of USGS topographic quadrangle maps covering Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning outdoor activities like hiking or hunting, this data tells you which topographic map covers your area — crucial for navigation where cell service is spotty. Farmers can check which quads cover their land to assess elevation changes affecting irrigation. Developers in Kansas City or Wichita can quickly identify which historical maps exist for due diligence on a property.

## How to read this data  
**QUADNAME** — The name of the topographic quadrangle (e.g., "Guymon"), matching the printed map title.  
**QUADDATE** — The year the map was published (e.g., 1979), showing how current the terrain data is.  
**BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_** — The exact geographic boundaries in latitude/longitude (e.g., 36.5° to 37°N, -102° to -101°W).  

## Try it yourself  
Find which topographic quad covers your property by matching your GPS coordinates to the BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_ values at [Kansas GIS Hub](https://hub.kansasgis.org/).
