## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries and metadata of USGS topographic quadrangles (quads) covering Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
These quads are the building blocks for land surveys, property boundaries, and historical land use records across Kansas. If you're researching property history (like a farm's past ownership) or planning outdoor activities (like hunting on public land), knowing which quad covers your area helps locate original survey maps. The **QUADDATE** field reveals whether your area's map reflects recent changes or decades-old landscapes.

## How to read this data  
**QUADNAME** — The official name of the quadrangle (e.g., "Guymon"), often matching a prominent local feature or town.  
**QUADDATE** — The year the quad's map was published (e.g., 1979), indicating how current the geographic features are.  
**BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_** — The latitude and longitude boundaries (e.g., 36.5 to 37°N, -102 to -101°W) showing the quad's exact coverage area.  

## Try it yourself  
Find which quad covers your property by matching your GPS coordinates to the **BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_** fields at [Kansas DASC](https://hub.kansasgis.org/).
