## What this tracks  
This dataset records levels of radioactive elements like uranium, radium, and radon in groundwater samples from Kansas wells between 1985 and 2001.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own property with a well in Kansas, this data reveals whether your water source historically contained unsafe radiation levels. For example, a radon reading of 685 pCi/L (like the sample value) is over 18 times the EPA's recommended action level. Homebuyers could check this before purchasing rural properties, and well owners might use it to decide whether to test their current water supply.

## How to read this data  
**SITE_NAME** — Identifies the well location, like "SG04WHITEWATER#02".  
**Gross_Uranium_pCi/L** — Measures uranium concentration; sample shows 15 pCi/L (EPA's maximum is 30).  
**Radon-222_pCi/L** — Shows radon gas levels; sample value is 685 pCi/L (EPA recommends fixing above 4).  
**COUNTY** — Two-letter county code where the well is located, like "BU" for Butler County.  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your county code in the dataset to see if any wells near you had high radiation readings during the testing period.
