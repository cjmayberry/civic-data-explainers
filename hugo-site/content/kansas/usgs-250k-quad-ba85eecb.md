## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries and metadata of USGS 250K topographic quadrangle maps covering Kansas.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning outdoor activities like hiking, farming, or land development in rural Kansas, this data helps you locate the right topographic map for your area. Knowing the map's coverage (e.g., Guymon quadrangle) and vintage (e.g., 1979) tells you whether elevation and terrain details are likely up-to-date for your project. Hunters can also use it to identify county boundaries within quadrangles.  

## How to read this data  
**QUADNAME** — The name of the quadrangle map (e.g., "Guymon").  
**QUADDATE** — The year the map was published (e.g., 1979).  
**BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_** — The latitude and longitude boundaries of the quadrangle (e.g., 36.5° to 37°N, -102° to -101°W).  

## Try it yourself  
Find which quadrangle covers your property by matching its coordinates to the BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_ fields in [Kansas's geospatial catalog](https://hub.kansasgis.org/).
