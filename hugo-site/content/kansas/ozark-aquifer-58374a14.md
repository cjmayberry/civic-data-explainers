## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the depth and extent of the Ozark aquifer beneath Kansas, including the elevation of its base and top layers.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're drilling a well or planning agricultural irrigation in Kansas, this data tells you how deep you'll need to drill to reach groundwater. Property buyers near the aquifer's edge can check whether their land sits above reliable water sources. Developers use it to assess groundwater availability before building in rural areas.

## How to read this data  
**OZ_BASE_** — Elevation (in feet) of the aquifer's bottom layer, with sample values like 1 (indicating shallow depth)  
**FEET** — Depth measurements, where -9999 appears to indicate missing data  
**CODE** — Numeric identifiers for aquifer segments (e.g., 222), useful for cross-referencing with geological surveys  

## Try it yourself  
Contact the Kansas Geological Survey with your property coordinates to request the aquifer depth at your location before drilling a well.
