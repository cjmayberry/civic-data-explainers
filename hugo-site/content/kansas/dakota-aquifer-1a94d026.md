## What this tracks  
This dataset measures the elevation, extent, and water quality of the Dakota Aquifer beneath Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're drilling a well or planning agricultural irrigation in Kansas, this data tells you how deep to dig to reach the aquifer and whether the water is likely to be salty. Farmers can check if their land sits above usable groundwater, while rural homeowners can estimate drilling costs based on the aquifer's depth beneath their property.

## How to read this data  
**DK_BASE_** — Depth to the base of the aquifer in feet (sample value: 54)  
**ALTITUDE_F** — Elevation of the aquifer's top surface above sea level (sample value: 1300 feet)  

## Try it yourself  
Check the aquifer depth under your Kansas property by cross-referencing these elevation values with your local topographic maps before drilling a well.
