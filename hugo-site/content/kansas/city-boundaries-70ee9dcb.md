## What this tracks  
This dataset records the official boundaries of cities and towns across Kansas, as maintained by county governments.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a business, this tells you exactly which city's zoning laws and tax rates apply to your storefront location — a coffee shop on one side of a street might pay different fees than one across the street. Homebuyers can verify whether a property falls within Kansas City limits for school districts or municipal services like trash pickup.

## How to read this data  
**COUNTY** — Which county the municipality is in (e.g., "Bourbon County").  
**MUNI** — The official name of the city or town (e.g., "Uniontown").  
**SOURCE** — How the boundary data was submitted (e.g., "ORKA" for Kansas Regional Agencies).  
**PLACEFP** — A unique 5-digit code assigned to each municipality by the Census Bureau (e.g., "72525").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your home address falls within Kansas City proper by comparing your location to the boundary map — this affects everything from voting precincts to snowplow routes.
