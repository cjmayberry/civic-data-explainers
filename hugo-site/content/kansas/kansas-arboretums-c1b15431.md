## What this tracks  
This dataset lists public arboretums across Kansas with labeled tree collections, including their locations and visiting details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a family outing or school field trip in Kansas, this tells you where to find arboretums with educational tree displays. A small business owner hosting an outdoor event could check **Fee** fields to find free botanical spaces for gatherings. Residents can use **Hours** to avoid arriving at unlit locations after dark.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The arboretum's formal title, like "Veteran's Memorial Park".  
**Address** — Physical location with street address (e.g., "1025 S Harrison St").  
**Hours** — Visiting times and lighting conditions ("Open 24 hours, but not well lighted").  
**Webpage** — Direct link to park details (e.g., olatheks.org/parksrec/veterans-memorial-park).  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for arboretums in your Kansas city, then check their **Webpage** fields for seasonal tree bloom schedules before visiting.
