## What this tracks  
This dataset lists public arboretums across Kansas that feature labeled trees, including their locations, hours, and available resources.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a family outing or educational field trip in Kansas, this data helps you find nearby arboretums with free admission and accessible hours. Teachers can identify locations with tree species lists for science lessons, while residents discover green spaces for recreation. The notes field warns about practical details like poor lighting for evening visits.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The arboretum's formal name (e.g., "Veteran's Memorial Park").  
**Address** — Full street address (e.g., "1025 S Harrison St, Olathe, KS 66061").  
**Hours** — Operating times and special conditions (e.g., "Open 24 hours, but not well lighted").  
**Fee** — Indicates free access (e.g., "No") or potential costs.
