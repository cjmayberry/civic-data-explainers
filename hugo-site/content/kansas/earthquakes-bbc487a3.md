## What this tracks  
This dataset records earthquake occurrences in Kansas, including their magnitude, depth, location, and time.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying a home in Saline County, this data tells you how often earthquakes occur nearby and how strong they tend to be. Kansas residents can check whether recent seismic activity might explain mysterious cracks in their foundations. Business owners storing fragile inventory can assess whether to invest in earthquake-proof shelving based on their area's quake frequency.

## How to read this data  
**MAGNITUDE** — Strength of the earthquake on the Richter scale (sample value: 2).  
**DEPTH** — How far below the surface the quake started, in kilometers (sample value: 4.7).  
**COUNTY_NAME** — Where the earthquake was centered (sample value: Saline).  
**LOCAL_TIME** — When residents felt the quake in local time (sample value: 1732057067000).  

## Try it yourself  
Check earthquake history near your Kansas property by searching the county name at the Kansas Geological Survey's Earthquake Information page.
