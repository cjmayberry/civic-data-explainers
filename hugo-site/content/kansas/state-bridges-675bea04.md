## What this tracks  
This dataset records all state-maintained bridges in Kansas, including their locations, structural details, and maintenance status.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing which bridges are under construction helps you plan alternate routes for daily commutes or truck deliveries. The vertical clearance data tells truck drivers whether their load will fit under a specific bridge. For residents near waterways like Big Creek, the "scour critical" flag indicates which bridges are most vulnerable to flood damage.

## How to read this data  
**LOCATION** — Describes where the bridge is, like "1.09 MI E TREGO CO LINE" (1.09 miles east of the Trego County line).  
**VERT_CLEAR** — Shows vertical clearance in feet (sample: 99.9 ft), crucial for oversized vehicles.  
**UNDER_CONSTRUCTION** — Indicates active work with "Y" or "N" values (sample: "N" for no current construction).  
**BUILT_DATE** — The year the bridge was constructed (sample: 1965), helping assess infrastructure age.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the vertical clearance of bridges along your regular route by searching for nearby crossings using the LATITUDE and LONGITUDE fields in the data.
