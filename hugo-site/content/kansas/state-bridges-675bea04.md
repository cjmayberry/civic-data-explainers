## What this tracks  
This dataset records all state-maintained bridges in Kansas that span 20 feet or longer, including their locations, structural details, and maintenance status.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning a trucking route through Kansas, this data tells you whether a bridge can handle your vehicle's weight (**DES_LOAD_2** shows the design load in tons). Residents can check **SUF_RATE** (a 0-100 score) to see if a bridge they use daily is rated as structurally deficient before major storms. Business owners shipping goods can verify **VERT_CLEAR** to avoid low-clearance bridges.

## How to read this data  
**FACILITY** — The highway name where the bridge is located (e.g., "K18 HWY").  
**FEATURE_CROSSED_DESCRIPTION** — What the bridge passes over (e.g., "YAUGER CREEK DRAINAGE").  
**SUF_RATE** — Structural sufficiency rating from 0-100 (sample: 98.5 means excellent condition).  
**BUILT_DATE** — Year the bridge was constructed (e.g., 1959 indicates potential aging infrastructure).  

## Try it yourself  
Check the **SUF_RATE** of bridges along your regular commute by searching for nearby **FACILITY** names like "K14" or "K18 HWY" in the dataset.
