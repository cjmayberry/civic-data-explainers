## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the shoreline wetlands around Cheney Lake in Reno, Kingman, and Sedgwick Counties, Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own lakeside property or operate a business near Cheney Lake, this data shows where protected wetlands begin—critical for planning docks, construction, or landscaping without violating environmental regulations. Anglers and hunters can use it to identify prime wetland habitats for fish and waterfowl. Farmers in the watershed can see how close their fields are to these sensitive areas when planning irrigation or fertilizer use.

## How to read this data  
**gridcode** — Classifies wetland types (sample value: 1, likely indicating a specific wetland category).  
**area_sqm** — Shows the size of each wetland area in square meters (sample value: 40,546,903, roughly 10,000 acres).  

## Try it yourself  
Zoom to your property on the map to see if any shoreline wetlands border your land—this affects what permits you’ll need for lakeside projects.
