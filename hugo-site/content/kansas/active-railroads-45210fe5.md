## What this tracks  
This dataset maps active railroad segments in Kansas, including ownership, location, and operational details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying property near train tracks in Kansas, this data tells you whether active freight lines run nearby and who operates them. Business owners shipping goods can identify which rail companies serve their area and compare route capacities. Residents can check if proposed developments might increase rail traffic near their neighborhoods.

## How to read this data  
**RailroadOwner** — The company operating the track (e.g., "VSR" for V&S Railway).  
**BeginMile/EndMile** — Where the segment starts and ends along the rail line (e.g., 22.0 to 24.18 means this 2.18-mile section).  
**LRSKey** — The formal name of the rail segment (e.g., "V & S_MEDICINE LODGE" identifies a specific subdivision).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if active railroads pass within 5 miles of your Kansas address using the state's [Railroad Map Viewer](https://www.kdot.ks.gov/Maps/Railroad-Map-Viewer).
