## What this tracks  
This dataset records the official boundaries of counties, American Indian reservations, and other administrative areas in Kansas as of 2020.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a business in Kansas, this data tells you exactly which county you're operating in—affecting tax rates, zoning laws, and permitting requirements. Residents can use it to confirm voting districts or school zones when relocating.  

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The common name of the county (e.g., "Greenwood").  
**NAMELSAD** — The full legal name (e.g., "Greenwood County").  
**INTPTLAT / INTPTLON** — The approximate center of the county in latitude/longitude (e.g., "+37.8793472, -096.2417321").  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your county's official boundary to verify if your home or business address falls within a specific jurisdiction for taxes or services.
