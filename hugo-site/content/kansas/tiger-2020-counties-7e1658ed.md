## What this tracks  
This dataset shows official boundaries for counties, American Indian reservations, and other political divisions in Kansas as of 2020.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your exact county boundary helps when applying for county-specific services like Greenwood County's small business grants or checking which sheriff's department handles your property. It also clarifies which county health department's COVID-19 guidelines apply to your location, as rules often vary between adjacent counties like Greenwood and Butler.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The common county name (e.g., "Greenwood").  
**NAMELSAD** — The full legal name (e.g., "Greenwood County").  
**INTPTLAT/INTPTLON** — The approximate geographic center of the county (e.g., +37.8793472, -096.2417321 for Greenwood County).  
**AWATER** — Total water area in square meters (e.g., 24,145,021 means ~9.3 square miles of water).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your county's boundary on Kansas' official GIS portal to verify whether your property falls under Greenwood County or a neighboring jurisdiction for tax purposes.
