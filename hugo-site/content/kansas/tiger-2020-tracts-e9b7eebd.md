## What this tracks  
This dataset shows 2020 U.S. Census boundaries for Kansas, including census tracts, blocks, and population/housing unit counts.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're opening a small business in Kansas, this data tells you exactly how many people live within specific neighborhood blocks — crucial for estimating local customer bases. Residents can see how densely populated their immediate area is compared to surrounding blocks, which affects everything from school district planning to emergency services coverage. The urban/rural codes help determine if you qualify for certain agricultural or small business grants.

## How to read this data  
**HOUSING20** — Total housing units in this block (e.g., "4" means four homes/apartments exist there).  
**POP20** — Total residents counted in 2020 (e.g., "13" people live in that block).  
**UR20** — Urban ("U") or rural ("R") designation, blank in samples but critical for grant eligibility.  
**INTPTLAT20/INTPTLON20** — Geographic center of the block (e.g., "+38.4850033, -100.9021188").  

## Try it yourself  
Search your Kansas address on the Census Bureau's [Geocoder](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/) to find your block's population density and housing count.
