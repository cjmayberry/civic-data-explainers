## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the underground boundaries of the Ozark Aquifer beneath Kansas, showing where this water source exists.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're drilling a well for your home, farm, or business in Kansas, this data tells you whether the Ozark Aquifer runs beneath your property — a key factor in estimating drilling costs and water availability. Farmers planning irrigation systems can check if they're in the aquifer's footprint before investing in equipment. Developers can avoid costly surprises by confirming groundwater access before breaking ground on new projects.

## How to read this data  
**OZ_BASE_** — Indicates whether a location is within the aquifer (1) or outside it (0).  
**FEET** — Shows aquifer depth where measured; -9999 means no depth data is available.  
**CODE** — A numeric identifier for aquifer segments; "222" is one example from the sample data.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the [Kansas Geological Survey's interactive map](https://www.kgs.ku.edu/) to see if your property sits above the Ozark Aquifer before planning a well project.
