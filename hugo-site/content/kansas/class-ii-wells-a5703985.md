## What this tracks  
This dataset records Class II injection wells in Kansas used for disposing of fluids or enhancing oil recovery, including their locations, operators, and status.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying land in Barton County, this data tells you whether the property has an active or abandoned injection well nearby, which could affect groundwater concerns or future development plans. For small oilfield service businesses, it identifies potential clients by showing active operators like PRAIRIE OIL FIELD SERVICE and their well locations.

## How to read this data  
**WELL_CLASS** — Shows the well's current status, like "Plugged and Abandoned" or active use.  
**STATUS_TXT** — Provides details like "Salt Water Disposal, Plugged" describing the well's purpose and condition.  
**CURR_OPERATOR** — Lists the company currently managing the well (blank in samples means inactive).  
**COUNTY_NAME** — Identifies the county location, such as "Barton" in the samples.  

## Try it yourself  
Check the Kansas Corporation Commission's GIS map to see if any Class II wells exist near your property by searching with the township/range/section values (e.g., Township 18S Range 15W Section 31).
