## What this tracks  
This dataset records Class II injection wells in Kansas used for fluid disposal or oil/gas recovery operations, including their locations, operators, and status.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying land in rural Kansas, this data tells you whether there are active or abandoned injection wells on or near the property, which could affect groundwater risks or future drilling activity. For farmers, nearby disposal wells may indicate potential soil contamination risks. The status field reveals whether a well is still operational or safely plugged.

## How to read this data  
**WELL_TYPE** — Indicates the well's purpose, like "SWD" (Salt Water Disposal) or oil recovery.  
**STATUS_TXT** — Current condition, such as "Salt Water Disposal, Plugged" for inactive wells.  
**COUNTY_NAME** — The Kansas county where the well is located (e.g., "Barton").  
**PLUG_DATE_TXT** — When the well was sealed (e.g., "11/01/1965"), important for assessing legacy sites.

Check for injection wells near a specific Kansas address by cross-referencing the county and township/range/section fields with local property records.
