## What this tracks  
This dataset shows 2020 U.S. Census boundaries for counties, census tracts, and blocks in Kansas, along with basic population and housing data.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering opening a small business in Kansas, this data tells you how many potential customers live within specific census blocks near your target location. Residents can check population density when researching neighborhood demographics before moving. The **HOUSING20** field reveals whether an area has mostly single-family homes or multi-unit buildings.

## How to read this data  
**NAME20** — Identifies specific census blocks (e.g., "Block 5081"), helping you pinpoint exact areas.  
**POP20** — Shows total residents in a block (e.g., 13 people), useful for estimating local customer bases.  
**INTPTLAT20/INTPTLON20** — Geographic coordinates (e.g., +38.4850033, -100.9021188) for mapping exact block locations.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your current Kansas address on the Census Bureau's [Geocoder](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/) to find which census block you live in and see its population density.
