## What this tracks  
This dataset shows 2020 census boundaries for counties, tracts, blocks, and other geographic areas in Kansas, along with population and housing unit counts.

## Why it matters to you  
When considering opening a business in Kansas, this data tells you exactly how many people live in specific census blocks—like whether a rural block has 13 residents or an urban one has thousands. Residents can check population density near their home to anticipate school district changes or infrastructure needs. The urban/rural codes help determine if a property qualifies for certain USDA loans or broadband expansion programs.

## How to read this data  
**POP20** — Total people living in this area in 2020 (e.g., "13" for a rural block).  
**HOUSING20** — Number of residential units (e.g., "4" means four homes or apartments).  
**UR20** — Urban ("U") or rural ("R") designation, blank in samples but critical for grant eligibility.  
**INTPTLAT20/INTPTLON20** — Center point coordinates (e.g., "+38.4850033, -100.9021188") to locate the area on a map.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your census block by entering your Kansas address into the [Census Geocoder](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/) to see its population density and urban/rural status.
