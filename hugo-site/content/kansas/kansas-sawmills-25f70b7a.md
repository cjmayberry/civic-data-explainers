## What this tracks  
This dataset lists sawmills in Kansas recommended by the Kansas Forest Service, including their locations, contact details, and services offered.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own wooded land in Kansas and need to sell timber, this data helps you find local sawmills that buy your specific tree species. Knowing whether a mill has a kiln (like **Kiln** = "No" for Hawley Logging) tells you if they can process dried lumber, which affects your pricing and buyer options. Farmers clearing land can also use this to quickly compare mills by **BuyingArea** to avoid long hauling distances.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The sawmill's business name (e.g., "Hawley Logging & Lumber Co").  
**Species** — Types of trees the mill accepts (e.g., "All" means they buy any species).  
**Kiln** — Whether the mill has drying equipment (values like "No" or blank if unknown).  
**PhoneNumbe** — Direct contact to negotiate prices (e.g., "(620)496-3468").  

## Try it yourself  
Call the nearest mill to your property (filter by **City**) and ask if they’re currently buying your type of timber — for example, a landowner near Elsmore could contact Hawley Logging at the number listed.
