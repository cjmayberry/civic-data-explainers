## What this tracks  
This dataset lists sawmills in Kansas recommended by the Kansas Forest Service, including their locations, contact details, and services offered.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own wooded land in Kansas and need to sell timber, this data helps you find local sawmills that buy your tree species. For contractors or woodworkers, it identifies mills with kilns for dried lumber. Knowing which mills operate near your town (like Hawley Logging in Elsmore) saves hauling costs compared to distant buyers.

## How to read this data  
**Name** — The sawmill's business name (e.g., "Hawley Logging & Lumber Co").  
**City** — Where the mill is located (e.g., "Elsmore").  
**Species** — Tree types the mill accepts (e.g., "All" means they buy any species).  
**Kiln** — Whether the mill has drying equipment (e.g., "No" means they only sell green lumber).  

## Try it yourself  
Check for sawmills within 30 miles of your Kansas town by filtering the dataset's "City" field, then call their "PhoneNumbe" (like (620)496-3468) to ask about current timber prices.
