## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Kansas voting districts, including their boundaries and associated legislative districts as of 2022.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your voting district helps you identify which candidates will appear on your ballot and where to vote in Kansas elections. If you’re advocating for a local issue, this data tells you which state representative (HSE), senator (SEN), or congressional representative (CONG) represents your neighborhood. For small businesses, it clarifies which officials influence zoning or tax policies in your area.

## How to read this data  
**VTD_2022_N** — Your precinct’s name, like "Deer Creek Township."  
**HSE** — Your Kansas House District number (e.g., 9).  
**SEN** — Your Kansas Senate District number (e.g., 12).  
**CONG** — Your U.S. Congressional District number (e.g., 2).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your address on the [Kansas Secretary of State’s voter portal](https://myvoteinfo.voteks.org/) to see which districts match the VTD_2022_N, HSE, and SEN values here.
