## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Kansas voting districts as defined in 2022, including their boundaries and associated legislative districts.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing your exact voting district ensures you receive the correct ballot for local elections in Allen County and across Kansas. This data tells you which candidates will appear on your ballot for State House (District 9), State Senate (District 12), and Congressional races (District 2). Small businesses can use this to identify which legislators represent their commercial property for advocacy purposes.

## How to read this data  
**VTD_2022_N** — Your precinct name, like "Deer Creek Township"  
**HSE** — Your Kansas House District number (e.g., 9 means you're in District 9)  
**CONG** — Your U.S. Congressional District (e.g., 2 for Kansas' 2nd District)  
**POPULATION** — Total residents in your precinct (e.g., 117 in Deer Creek Township)  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your address on the Kansas Secretary of State's voter portal to see which precinct and districts match these codes.
