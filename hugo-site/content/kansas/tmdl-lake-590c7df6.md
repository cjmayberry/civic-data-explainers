## What this tracks  
This dataset records water quality issues in Kansas lakes and streams, listing specific pollutants like atrazine or E. coli that exceed safe levels.

## Why it matters to you  
Before fishing in a Kansas stream, check this data to see if mercury or other contaminants are present. Farmers can verify if nearby waterways have fertilizer runoff issues (marked as "TOTAL_NITROGEN" or "TOTAL_PHOSPHORUS") that might affect irrigation. Homebuyers near water bodies can assess long-term environmental risks.

## How to read this data  
**STREAM_NAME** — Identifies the water body (e.g., "Unnamed Stream").  
**ATRAZINE** — Shows herbicide levels (sample value: "2025"); blank means not tested.  
**E_COLI** — Indicates bacterial contamination if populated; blank means no issues detected.  
**TOTAL_PHOSPHORUS** — Measures nutrient pollution from fertilizers or sewage; high values signal algae bloom risks.  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your nearest stream using the "STREAM_NAME" field to check for active pollution warnings before recreational use.
