## What this tracks  
This dataset identifies Kansas waterways with pollution levels exceeding water quality standards, requiring cleanup plans under the Clean Water Act.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying property near a stream, this data reveals whether the water has unsafe levels of contaminants like lead or agricultural runoff. Anglers can check **ATRAZINE** levels (a common herbicide) before eating fish from local waters. Kansas residents planning lakeside recreation can avoid areas with **E_COLI** contamination warnings.

## How to read this data  
**STREAM_NAME** — Identifies affected waterways, like "Unnamed Stream" or major rivers. Blank values indicate unnamed tributaries.  
**ATRAZINE** — Shows herbicide concentrations (sample value "2025" likely represents parts per billion). Blank means no detected contamination.  
**E_COLI** — Indicates fecal bacteria presence (blank = safe levels; any value = contamination).  

## Try it yourself  
Search for your nearest stream using [KDHE's Water Portal](https://www.kdhe.ks.gov/210/Water-Portal) to see if it has active contamination warnings for swimming or fishing.
