## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Kansas' 4,216-mile rail system, showing ownership, segments, and operational status of railroad lines across the state.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying property near rail lines in Kansas, this data reveals whether active freight trains run nearby — affecting noise levels and safety. Business owners shipping goods can identify which railroad companies serve their area (like VSR in the sample) to negotiate freight rates or plan warehouse locations. Farmers can check **WeightCapacity** to see if local tracks support heavy agricultural equipment shipments.

## How to read this data  
**RailroadOwner** — The company operating the line (e.g., "VSR"). Contact them for freight services or noise complaints.  
**BeginMile** / **EndMile** — Physical segment locations (e.g., 22.0 to 24.18 marks a 2.18-mile stretch).  
**Active** — Whether the line is currently in use (1) or abandoned (0). Check this before assuming rail proximity affects property values.
