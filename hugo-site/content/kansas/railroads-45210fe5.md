## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the 4,216-mile rail system in Kansas, including ownership, segments, and operational details of freight railroads.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a farmer shipping grain or a business receiving bulk materials, this data shows which railroads serve your area and their capacity. For example, knowing the **WeightCapacity** of a line near your warehouse in Wichita tells you whether trains can haul your heavy machinery. Homebuyers near Topeka can check **Active** status to gauge how often trains might pass through their neighborhood.

## How to read this data  
**RailroadOwner** — The company operating the line, like "VSR" (sample value).  
**BeginMile**/**EndMile** — Where a segment starts and ends (e.g., 22.0 to 24.18 means this 2.18-mile stretch is tracked).  
**LRSKey** — Combines owner and subdivision names, like "V & S_MEDICINE LODGE" (sample), useful for identifying specific corridors.  

## Try it yourself  
Check [Kansas's rail map](https://www.ksdot.org/rail.asp) to see which lines run near your property—enter your county to see local segments and their owners.
