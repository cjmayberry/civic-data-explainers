## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Kansas tax unit boundaries for 2025, showing which local government entities collect property taxes in each area.

## Why it matters to you  
When buying property in Osage County or elsewhere in Kansas, this tells you exactly which school districts, fire departments, and other services your taxes will fund. Business owners can compare tax unit boundaries when choosing locations, since adjacent properties might pay different mill rates. Homebuyers can verify they're looking at homes within preferred school districts before making offers.

## How to read this data  
**TAXUNIT** — A unique code for each taxing jurisdiction (e.g., "194" for a specific school district in Osage County).  
**COUNTY** — The county where the tax unit operates (sample: "Osage County").  
**CERT_DATE** — When the boundary was officially approved (sample: "01/30/2025").  
**REPORT2025** — Whether boundaries changed from prior years (sample: "No Changes" means identical to 2024).

## Try it yourself  
Check the [Kansas Archive Hub](https://archivehub.kansasgis.org/) to see if your property's 2025 tax unit boundaries differ from current ones—this could affect next year's tax bill.
