## What this tracks  
This dataset provides US National Grid (USNG) 10,000-meter grid references for locations in Kansas, used for emergency response and mapping.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're a Kansas resident planning outdoor activities like hiking or hunting, knowing your USNG grid location helps emergency responders find you faster if you need help. Small businesses with delivery or field teams can use these grid references to standardize location reporting across devices and maps, reducing confusion in rural areas.  

## How to read this data  
**GRID1MIL** — The 1,000,000-meter grid identifier (e.g., "01G"), which divides Kansas into broad zones for large-scale navigation. This is the coarsest level of the USNG system.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your current location’s USNG grid using a free online converter (like [USNG App](https://usngapp.org/)) and note the 10,000-meter code—share it with family when venturing into remote areas.
