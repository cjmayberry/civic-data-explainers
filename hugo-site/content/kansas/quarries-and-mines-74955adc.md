## What this tracks  
This dataset records locations of active and abandoned quarries and mines in Kansas, including what materials they extract.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying rural property in Kansas, this data tells you whether there's an active limestone quarry or abandoned mine nearby that could affect land value or groundwater quality. Contractors can check which companies operate nearby quarries when sourcing local stone for construction projects. Note this data hasn't been updated since 2000, so newer operations won't appear.

## How to read this data  
**PRODUCT_CATEGORY** — The primary material extracted, like "Limestone" in the sample.  
**QUARRY_TYPE** — Whether operations are "Surface" (open pit) or presumably underground (though no samples show this).  
**STATUS** — Shows if the site is "Active" or abandoned (sample shows active operation).  
**OPERATOR_NAME** — Lists who runs the site, like "Cheyenne County Road Dept" in the sample.
