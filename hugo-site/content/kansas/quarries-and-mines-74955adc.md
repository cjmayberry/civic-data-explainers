## What this tracks  
This dataset records locations of active and abandoned quarries and mines in Kansas, including what materials are extracted and who operates them.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying rural property in Kansas, this data reveals whether active mining operations exist nearby — important for assessing noise, truck traffic, or water use impacts. Homebuilders can check if local limestone quarries (like the Cheyenne County Road Dept operation) offer materials for construction projects. The status field helps identify abandoned sites that may pose safety risks if exploring rural land.

## How to read this data  
**PRODUCT_CATEGORY** — The primary material extracted, like "Limestone" in the sample.  
**STATUS** — Shows if operational ("Active") or closed; blank fields may indicate unknown status.  
**OPERATOR_NAME** — The company managing the site, such as "Cheyenne County Road Dept".  
**TOWNSHIP/RANGE** — Legal land descriptions (e.g., "4S 40W") to locate sites on county maps.  

## Try it yourself  
Check for quarries near your county by cross-referencing the COUNTY_CODE (23 = Cheyenne County) with Kansas county maps, then look for matching TOWNSHIP/RANGE coordinates in property deeds.
