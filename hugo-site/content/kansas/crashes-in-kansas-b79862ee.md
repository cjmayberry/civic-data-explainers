## What this tracks  
This dataset records all reported motor vehicle crashes in Kansas that resulted in injury, fatality, over $1,000 in property damage, or damage to KDOT property.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're choosing between routes for your daily commute in Wichita, this data shows which intersections have recurring rear-end collisions or pedestrian incidents. Parents can check if their teen’s driving route has a high rate of speed-related crashes. Small trucking businesses can identify roads with frequent large-vehicle accidents to adjust delivery routes.

## How to read this data  
**ACC_SEVERITY** — Injury level: values like "INJURY" or "FATALITY"  
**CWOV_FHE_ACC_TYPE** — Collision type: "REAR END", "SIDESWIPE", or "HEAD ON"  
**ACC_HOUR** — Time of crash: "21" means 9 PM  
**ON_ROAD_TYPE** — Road classification: "Local Route" or "State Highway"  

## Try it yourself  
Search crashes near your Wichita neighborhood by filtering for "ACC_CITY = WICHITA" and checking the DOT_LATITUDE/DOT_LONGITUDE coordinates against your area.
