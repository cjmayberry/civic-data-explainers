## What this tracks  
This dataset records all reported motor vehicle crashes in Kansas that resulted in fatalities, injuries, property damage over $1,000, or damage to state property.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you’re choosing a route for your daily commute or evaluating a neighborhood’s safety, this data shows collision hotspots, common causes (like speeding or distracted driving), and high-risk times. For example, parents in Overland Park could check for school bus-involved crashes near their child’s route, or delivery drivers might avoid roads with frequent rear-end collisions during rush hour.  

## How to read this data  
**ACC_SEVERITY** — Injury or damage level, like "PROPERTY DAMAGE ONLY" or "FATALITY."  
**CWOV_FHE_ACC_TYPE** — Crash type, such as "REAR END" or "SIDESWIPE."  
**ACC_DAY_OF_WEEK** — When crashes occur (e.g., "Friday").  
**SPEED_RELATED_ACCS** — "1" means speed was a factor; "0" means it wasn’t.  

## Try it yourself  
Search crashes near your home using [KDOT’s map tool](https://www.kdotgis.org/kcrs/) by entering your address and filtering for recent years.
