## What this tracks  
This dataset records abandoned railroad segments across Kansas, including ownership details and physical characteristics of the rail lines.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying property near an inactive rail line in Kansas, this data tells you whether the tracks might be repurposed or reactivated — affecting future noise, traffic, or redevelopment potential. Farmers and businesses shipping goods can check if nearby rail infrastructure is operational before planning logistics. Developers can identify potential rail-to-trail conversion opportunities in their area.

## How to read this data  
**RailroadOwner** — The company that owns the rail segment (e.g., "VSR").  
**BeginMile/EndMile** — Physical location markers where the segment starts and ends (e.g., 22.0 to 24.18353975).  
**LRSKey** — The formal name of the rail subdivision (e.g., "V & S_MEDICINE LODGE").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if there are abandoned rail segments near your Kansas property by searching the dataset for your county name in the LRSKey field.
