## What this tracks  
This dataset tracks abandoned and active railroad segments across Kansas, including ownership and operational details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering buying property near railroad tracks in Kansas, this data tells you whether the line is active (meaning freight trains may pass regularly) or abandoned (potentially available for rail-to-trail conversions). Business owners shipping goods can check which railroads serve their area and whether lines are part of the Primary Freight Network for reliable service.

## How to read this data  
**RailroadOwner** — The company that owns the track (e.g., "VSR").  
**Active** — Whether the track is currently in use (1) or abandoned (0).  
**BeginMile/EndMile** — The physical location of the segment (e.g., 22.0 to 24.18353975).  
**LRSKey** — The formal name of the railroad subdivision (e.g., "V & S_MEDICINE LODGE").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if the railroad near your Kansas property is active or abandoned by matching the BeginMile/EndMile values to your location along the rail corridor.
