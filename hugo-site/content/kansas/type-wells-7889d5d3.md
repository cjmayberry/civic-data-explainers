## What this tracks  
This dataset records locations and operational details of oil and gas wells in Kansas, including their status, ownership, and drilling history.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying land in Kansas, this data tells you whether the property has active, inactive, or abandoned wells—critical for assessing environmental risks or future drilling potential. Farmers can check if nearby wells might affect water sources, and small businesses (like equipment suppliers) can identify active operators for potential partnerships.  

## How to read this data  
**WELL_CLASS** — Current status of the well, like "Plugged and Abandoned" (sample value).  
**OPERATOR_NAME** — Company currently managing the well, e.g., "Sunray Mid-Continent Oil Co."  
**PRODUCING_FORMATION** — Geological layer where resources were extracted, such as "ARBUCKLE" (sample value).  
**SPUD_DATE_TXT** — Date drilling began, formatted as "02/19/1962" (sample value).  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset by county or township to see if your property or neighborhood has documented wells nearby.
