## What this tracks  
This dataset shows the boundaries and basic details of reservoirs and lakes managed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) in Kansas and surrounding areas.

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing where these reservoirs are located helps you plan fishing trips, assess flood risks near your property, or understand local water supply sources. For example, if you're considering buying land downstream from a USACE dam, this data tells you whether heavy rains could put your property at risk of controlled releases. Business owners offering lakefront services can verify which reservoirs are actively maintained (not "dry") before investing in advertising or equipment.

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The official reservoir name, like "Arcadia Lake."  
**DAM_NAME** — Specific dam structure associated with the reservoir, such as "Arcadia Lake Dam."  
**DRY** — Indicates if the reservoir is non-functional ("Yes" or "No").  
**DISTRICT** — Which USACE office manages it, like "Tulsa District."  

## Try it yourself  
Check if any USACE reservoirs are within 20 miles of your Kansas address using the [DASC map viewer](https://hub.kansasgis.org/)—look for water bodies labeled with dam names in the "Infrastructure" category.
