## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries and metadata of USGS 100K topographic quadrangle maps covering Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning outdoor activities like hiking or surveying land in rural Kansas, this data tells you exactly which topographic map sheet covers your area. Farmers can verify which historical maps include their fields, and local historians can identify maps for researching landscape changes over time. The quadrangle names match the paper maps you'd purchase for detailed terrain navigation.

## How to read this data  
**QUADNAME** — The official name of the quadrangle map, like "Guymon," matching the printed map title you'd search for.  
**QUADDATE** — The year the map was originally published (e.g., 1979), helping you assess its recency.  
**BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_** — The latitude and longitude boundaries (e.g., 36.5°N to 37°N) defining the map's coverage area.  
**FIPS_C** — Lists county codes (like 20129) for all counties that overlap this quadrangle.  

## Try it yourself  
Find which quadrangle map covers your property by comparing your GPS coordinates to the BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_ values in this dataset.
