## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Kansas' sub-watershed boundaries using 12-digit Hydrologic Unit Codes (HUCs), which divide the state's water drainage areas.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying property near a creek or planning agricultural irrigation in Kansas, this data shows exactly which watershed your land sits in. Knowing your HUC 12 boundary helps predict flood risks, as rainfall runoff follows these mapped paths. Developers use it to comply with water runoff regulations when building in areas like Skunkie Arroyo or Lower Crooked Creek.  

## How to read this data  
**HUC_12** — A unique code like "110400070505" identifying your exact sub-watershed, similar to a ZIP code for water flow.  
**HU_12_NAME** — The local name of the water feature, such as "Skunkie Arroyo."  
**ACRES** — The watershed's size in acres (e.g., 26,308), indicating how much land drains into that area.  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your Kansas address on the [Kansas Watershed Map](https://www.kansasgis.org/) to see if your property falls within flood-prone HUC 12 zones like Lower Crooked Creek.
