## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Kansas' sub-watershed boundaries using 12-digit Hydrologic Unit Codes (HUCs), which divide land areas based on water flow patterns.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying property in Kansas, this data shows whether your land sits in a flood-prone watershed or drains into protected waterways like the Lower Crooked Creek. Farmers can check which sub-watershed their fields belong to when planning irrigation or applying for conservation programs. Developers in Wichita or Topeka use these boundaries to comply with stormwater runoff regulations.

## How to read this data  
**HUC_12** — A 12-digit code identifying specific sub-watersheds (e.g., 110400070505 for Skunkie Arroyo).  
**HU_12_NAME** — Common names for waterways (e.g., "Skunkie Arroyo").  
**ACRES** — Size of the watershed area in acres (e.g., 26,308 acres).  
**HU_12_TYPE** — Classifies the water feature type (e.g., "S" for standard perennial stream).  

## Try it yourself  
Look up your property on Kansas' HUC Finder (https://www.ksdot.gov/maps/HUCFinder/) to see which watershed drains rainfall from your land.
