## What this tracks  
This dataset records the posted speed limits along Kansas state highways, including standard, truck, and nighttime speed limits.  

## Why it matters to you  
Knowing the exact speed limits on Kansas highways helps you avoid speeding tickets when driving unfamiliar routes. For truck drivers, it clarifies whether lower limits apply to commercial vehicles. The data also shows if reduced nighttime speeds are enforced, which is crucial for planning safe late-night travel.  

## How to read this data  
**SpeedLimit** — The standard daytime speed limit in mph (e.g., 65).  
**TruckSpeedLimit** — Separate speed limit for commercial trucks if different (e.g., 65).  
**NightSpeedLimit** — Enforced after dark; matches daytime limit if no reduction exists (e.g., 65).  
**SpeedReg** — "P" indicates the limit is permanently posted (not seasonal/temporary).  

## Try it yourself  
Before a road trip, check the speed limit for your route segment at [KDOT’s traveler information map](https://www.kandrive.org/).
