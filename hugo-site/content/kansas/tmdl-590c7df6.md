## What this tracks  
This dataset records water quality issues in Kansas streams and rivers, listing specific pollutants that exceed safe levels under the Clean Water Act.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering fishing, swimming, or letting pets drink from a local stream, this data tells you which waterways have unsafe levels of contaminants like E. coli or mercury. Farmers can check for nitrate levels that might affect irrigation plans, and homeowners near waterways can identify potential risks to well water quality.

## How to read this data  
**STREAM_NAME** — Identifies the affected waterway, like "Unnamed Stream" or specific river names.  
**ATRAZINE** — Shows herbicide levels (sample value "2025" likely indicates concentration in parts per billion).  
**E_COLI** — Indicates fecal contamination when populated (blank means not tested or within limits).  
**TOTAL_PHOSPHORUS** — Measures nutrient pollution that causes algae blooms (blank means acceptable levels).  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your nearest creek or fishing spot appears in the dataset by searching for its name in the STREAM_NAME field.
