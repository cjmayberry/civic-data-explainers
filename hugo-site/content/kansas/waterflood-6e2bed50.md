## What this tracks  
This dataset maps areas in Kansas where water or other fluids are injected into oil wells to boost production through pressure maintenance techniques.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own land in Kansas, this data tells you whether your property falls within an active waterflood zone, which could affect mineral rights negotiations or surface use agreements. Farmers can check if nearby injection activity might impact water tables. Small businesses servicing oil operations can identify active project areas by operator name to target services.

## How to read this data  
**FIELDNAME** — The oil field being flooded (e.g., "Bemis-Shutts").  
**OPERATOR** — The company managing injections (e.g., "Lario Oil & Gas Company").  
**ACTIVE** — Shows currently operating injection wells (sample value "0" means inactive).  
**TOTALOIL** — Annual oil production in barrels from this flood project (e.g., "6778").  

## Try it yourself  
Check if your county appears in the COUNTY field (like "ELLIS COUNTY") to see local waterflood activity.
