## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and ownership details of cell towers and other antenna structures across Kansas, as registered with the FCC.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're frustrated with spotty cell service at home or work, this data shows nearby towers and who operates them — helping you target complaints to the right provider (like CTI Towers Inc.). Small businesses considering a location can check tower proximity to gauge likely signal strength before signing a lease. Farmers and rural residents can identify gaps in coverage when advocating for better infrastructure.

## How to read this data  
**ENTITY_NAM** — The company owning the tower, like "CTI Towers Inc."  
**STRUCTURE1** — The street address of the tower, such as "1701 N 3RD" in Burlington.  
**OVERALL_HE** — Total height in meters (e.g., 121.9m) — taller towers often provide wider coverage.  
**STATUS_COD** — "C" means currently active; other codes indicate planned or decommissioned towers.

Search the dataset for your ZIP code (like 66839) to see all active towers near your neighborhood.
