## What this tracks  
This dataset records the locations and technical details of cell towers registered with the FCC in Kansas, including ownership, height, and construction dates.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're experiencing poor cell service in Kansas, this data helps you identify whether a tower exists near your home or business—or if one is planned. Residents can use it to advocate for better coverage by contacting the tower owner (**ENTITY_NAM**) directly. Small businesses relying on mobile transactions (like food trucks or outdoor vendors) can check tower proximity before committing to a location.  

## How to read this data  
**ENTITY_NAM** — The company that owns the tower, like "CTI Towers Inc."  
**STRUCTURE1** — The tower's street address, such as "1701 N 3RD" in Burlington.  
**OVERALL_HE** — Total height in meters (e.g., 121.9m) — useful for assessing signal range.  
**STATUS_COD** — "C" means the tower is currently active; other codes indicate planned or defunct towers.  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset for towers near your Kansas address using **STRUCTURE1** or **STRUCTUR_3** (city) fields to see if coverage gaps might explain your spotty service.
