## What this tracks  
This dataset records details about water wells drilled in Kansas, including their location, depth, purpose, and construction details.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're considering drilling a well on your property, this data shows existing wells nearby and their depth—helping you estimate drilling costs. Farmers can check water availability before purchasing land. In Ford County, for example, you can see if industrial wells like Fina Oil and Chemical Co.'s monitoring well (43 ft deep) might affect your groundwater access.

## How to read this data  
**DEPTH_TXT** — Well depth in feet, like "43" for a shallow monitoring well.  
**USE_DESC** — The well's purpose, such as "Monitoring well/observation/piezometer" for environmental tracking.  
**LOCATION_DIRECTIONS** — Physical address or landmark, like "304 E Wyatt Earp Blvd, Dodge City".  
**STATIC_WATER_LEVEL** — Depth to water table, shown as "30" (feet below surface) where available.
