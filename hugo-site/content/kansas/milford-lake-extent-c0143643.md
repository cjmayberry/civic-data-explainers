## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the shoreline wetlands around Milford Lake in Geary, Clay, and Dickinson Counties, Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning to fish, boat, or build near Milford Lake, this data shows where protected wetlands begin. Knowing these boundaries helps you avoid fines for disturbing sensitive habitats. Hunters can use it to identify prime waterfowl areas, while property owners can check if their land borders protected zones before making improvements.

## How to read this data  
**gridcode** — Indicates wetland type (sample value: 1). While specific classifications aren't provided, higher numbers likely denote different wetland categories.  
**Shape__Area** — Measures the size of wetland areas in square units, though sample values aren't available. Larger values mean more extensive wetland zones.
