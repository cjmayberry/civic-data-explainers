## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the shoreline wetlands around Milford Lake in Geary, Clay, and Dickinson Counties, Kansas.

## Why it matters to you  
If you own lakeside property or operate a business near Milford Lake, this data helps identify protected wetland areas where development may be restricted. Anglers and boaters can use it to locate shallow or marshy zones to avoid when navigating. Local farmers can check if their land borders these wetlands, which may require special conservation practices.

## How to read this data  
**gridcode** — Indicates wetland type (sample value: 1), where higher numbers may correspond to different wetland classifications.  
**Shape__Area** — Shows the size of each wetland area in square units (though sample values are empty, this field would help compare wetland footprints).

## Try it yourself  
Check whether your property or favorite fishing spot borders a mapped wetland by comparing this dataset with local parcel maps at the Geary, Clay, or Dickinson County planning offices.
