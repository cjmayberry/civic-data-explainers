## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries and details of USGS 24K topographic quadrangles (quads) covering Kansas.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you're planning outdoor activities like hiking, hunting, or land surveying in Kansas, this data helps you identify which topographic map covers your area. Knowing the quad name and date lets you check if the map reflects recent terrain changes—critical for avoiding outdated features like dried-up streams or new roads. Farmers can also verify which quads cover their land for soil or elevation reference.  

## How to read this data  
**QUADNAME** — The name of the quadrangle (e.g., "Guymon"), matching the title of the corresponding paper map.  
**QUADDATE** — The year the map was published (e.g., 1979), indicating how current the terrain data is.  
**BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_** — The latitude and longitude bounds (e.g., 36.5 to 37°N, -102 to -101°W) showing the quad’s coverage area.  

## Try it yourself  
Find which quad covers your property by matching your GPS coordinates to the **BOTTOM/TOP_/LEFT_/RIGHT_** bounds in the dataset.
