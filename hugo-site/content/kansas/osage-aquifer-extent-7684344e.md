## What this tracks  
This dataset maps the boundaries of major aquifers in Kansas, including the Osage Aquifer, as identified by the Kansas Geological Survey.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you rely on well water for your home or business, this data tells you whether your property sits above a major aquifer—critical for estimating water availability and drilling costs. Farmers can use it to assess irrigation potential before purchasing land. Developers in Kansas can check aquifer locations to avoid areas where groundwater depletion might trigger stricter regulations.  

## How to read this data  
**NAME** — The aquifer's official designation (e.g., "Ozark Aquifer"). This tells you which underground water system exists beneath a given area.  
**Shape__Area** and **Shape__Length** — Technical GIS fields (not meaningful for residents) that calculate the aquifer's geographic footprint.  

## Try it yourself  
Search the Kansas Geological Survey's interactive map with your address to see if you're above the Osage Aquifer or another groundwater source.
