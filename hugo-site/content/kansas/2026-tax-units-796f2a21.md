## What this tracks  
This dataset records certified tax unit boundaries across Kansas counties for 2026, showing where different property tax rates apply.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying property in Lincoln County, this tells you exactly which tax district it falls under before you make an offer. Business owners can compare REPORT2026 statuses between locations to anticipate potential tax changes when expanding operations. Homeowners can verify their TAXUNIT matches county records to catch assessment errors.

## How to read this data  
**TAXUNIT** — The 3-digit code identifying your local tax district (e.g., 040).  
**COUNTY** — The county name where the tax unit applies (e.g., Lincoln County).  
**REPORT2026** — Shows if boundaries changed ("No Changes" means stable rates).  
**CERT_DATE** — When the tax unit was officially approved (e.g., 01/27/2026).  

## Try it yourself  
Check your property's 2026 tax district by matching your county and TAXUNIT code at [KansasGIS Archive Hub](https://archivehub.kansasgis.org/).
