## What this tracks  
This dataset records certified tax unit boundaries across Kansas for 2026, showing how properties are grouped for taxation purposes.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying property in Lincoln County or neighboring areas, this data shows exactly which tax district it falls into — crucial for estimating your future property taxes. Business owners expanding to new locations can compare tax unit boundaries to see if they qualify for economic development zones with different tax rates. The "REPORT2026" field tells you whether a tax unit's boundaries changed recently, which could affect your tax obligations.

## How to read this data  
**TAXUNIT** — A three-digit code identifying specific tax districts (e.g., "040" for one Lincoln County area).  
**COUNTY** — The county where the tax unit is located (e.g., "Lincoln County").  
**REPORT2026** — Indicates if boundaries changed ("No Changes" means stable tax districts).  
**CERT_DATE** — When the tax unit was officially certified (e.g., "01/27/2026").
