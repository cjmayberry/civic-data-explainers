## What this tracks  
This dataset records active oil and gas leases in Kansas, including operator details, production status, and precise geographic coordinates.  

## Why it matters to you  
If you own land in Kansas, this data tells you whether your property is under an active oil or gas lease and who operates it. Farmers can check if nearby drilling might affect their water sources, while prospective homebuyers can identify potential industrial activity near rural properties.  

## How to read this data  
**LEASE_NAME** — The name of the leaseholder (e.g., "J. G. HAVENER").  
**OPERATOR_NAME** — The company extracting resources (e.g., "Horton, John").  
**PRODUCES_OIL** / **PRODUCES_GAS** — Whether the site actively yields oil ("Yes") or gas ("No").  
**LATITUDE** / **LONGITUDE** — Exact coordinates (e.g., 37.132045, -95.882477) using the NAD27 standard.  

## Try it yourself  
Search the dataset by your county code (e.g., 125 for Labette County) to see active leases near your property.
