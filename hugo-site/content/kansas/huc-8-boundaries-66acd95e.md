## What this tracks  
This dataset maps Kansas' 8-digit hydrologic unit code (HUC) boundaries, which define watershed areas where water flows to a common point.

## Why it matters to you  
If you're buying property near a river or planning agricultural irrigation in Kansas, this data shows which watershed your land sits in—critical for understanding flood risks, water rights, and conservation rules. Developers use it to check stormwater runoff requirements before breaking ground. Farmers reference it to coordinate with local water districts.

## How to read this data  
**HUC_8** — An 8-digit code (like 10240005) identifying your specific watershed.  
**HU_8_STATE** — Lists states (e.g., "IA KS MO NE") where this watershed crosses borders.  
**SQ_MILES** — The watershed's size (e.g., 1,701 square miles for the Tarkio-Wolf subbasin).  

## Try it yourself  
Check which watershed your Kansas property falls into by matching its location to the HUC_8 codes at [Kansas GIS Hub](https://hub.kansasgis.org/).
